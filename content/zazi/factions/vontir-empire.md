# 🏳️ Vontir Empire

> The large western power that [[zazi/locations/zazi|Zazi]] is "loosely aligned" with — semi-autonomy included.

## As the party learns it (Session 9, from young Hazi)
- **Zazi** is a semi-autonomous city, loosely aligned with the western Vontir Empire.
- In the **past era**, the empire is experiencing **minor rebellions throughout its western outposts**.
- Its history with Di's people: **~450–500 years** before the past era, the Vontir's "**soft encroachment**" was repelled by Di's ancestors; they were **never conquered** (Di's genealogical studies — his great-ancestor hero of that battle is still an unnamed open thread).

## Why it matters
- The empire is the geopolitical backdrop for the whole region: trade (tea, yams, "bustling trade routes" per Session 1), the pyramid's causeway caravans, and the "treasure buried under the palace" rumor (Session 11).
- Whether Vontir is expanding again, retreating, or distracted by the [[zazi/worldbuilding/northern-war|Northern War]] is not yet known.
