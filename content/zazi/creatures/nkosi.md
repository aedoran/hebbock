# 🐱 Nkosi

> The cat-folk of [[zazi/locations/zazi|Zazi]] — common in the **past** era, rare or absent in the **present**. The party's "City of Cats" gets its name from them (inference — flagged, not ruled).

## As the party meets them
- **Pyramid ambush (Session 6):** six cat-people in stasis in the basin room; touching the orange scarab wakes them. They fight in a pack: one roars and **transforms into a lion** (lion form can pounce, bite, claw). Lachma-as-constrictor squeezes the lion out; Bishop dual-dagger finishes it; the party then lights all seven scarabs (Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Violet, Purple) and the stone tablet moves aside.
- **The Pride Room reveal (Session 4):** the "falcon-headed men slay lions" carving is an **illusion** — the true carving shows Nkosi "in status, weapons at the ready." Di: "Sorcerous shenanigans!"
- **Past era (Session 9):** the Nkosi are "a higher percentage" of the city crowd — the party's shock at their abundance is a key marker of the time shift.

## Inference (unconfirmed)
- Given [[zazi/worldbuilding/pantheon|Bastet]] (cat goddess), the cat symbolism everywhere (bronze cat with gem eyes, cat-scarab mosaics, Tellya's "scroll with a cat symbol"), and the city's name — the Nkosi are probably Bastet's people. **Open question** in [[_questions|Open Questions]].
