# ⚙️ Warforged

> "New mechanical beings... magically created, self-aware constructs 'forged for war.'" The defining new technology of the [[zazi/worldbuilding/northern-war|Northern War]] — and the reason [[campaigns/team-c/characters/scout|SCOUT]] is terrified of the past.

## As the world knows them
- Forged by the **wizards** who fight the Northern War; the wizards "fought each other" (SCOUT's account, Session 10).
- Common belief: they have **no free will** — "creatures of war."
- In the **past era** (~20 years before the party's present), they are **rebelling against their magical masters**.
- In the present: still at war; the party's world still full of them.

## As the party meets them (past era)
- **SCOUT is the only one** anyone there has ever seen. He wears a cloak, hood up, and hides. "I've got a bad feeling about this." He may have "another version of himself with a different memory" here; his memory "may have been erased many times."
- **Session 13 — the ambush:** four or five humanoids in **brown cloaks** (two with **warforged ankles** — metal glinting in street lamps, "voices... like they emerge from soup cans") are following orders about the party ("They're close. They're staying at the inn. We must follow through with our orders"). A squad of four (two confirmed warforged among them, "Kraftwerk"-esque, harsher aesthetics than SCOUT) attacks **Bishop** in the apartment-block streets; the party beats them back to the [[zazi/locations/charnel-house|charnel house]] as the city watch bell tolls.
- **Session 10–11:** a **green-smoke woman** (low-cut dress, green necklace — there to collect "the corpse of her uncle") vanishes into green smoke at the charnel house; SCOUT's last warning and his disappearance happen in the same timeframe. The party suspects a connection (unresolved).
