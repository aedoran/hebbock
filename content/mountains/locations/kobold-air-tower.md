# 🌪️ Kobold air tower

> The [[mountains/factions/order-of-uncarved-block|Order of the Uncarved Block]]'s tower on the **Eastern Ridge** — built on the wind, now held by **kobolds** ("tiny trapping lizards of draconic descent") and their **urd** slingers. The party's second act (Sessions 10–14) ends here, aloft.

## The approach
- A steep, rocky hill climb under **urd sling fire** (Session 10): Xuanzang's hole trap, a rock-net trap, Sir's shield retrieved from a pit, then the door.
- The kobolds hope to catch the party **as a sacrifice to the dragon** (Thora, by her language skills, reads their intent) — and the dragon is [[mountains/npcs/rhiltannaxis|Rhiltannäxis]].

## Inside (floor by floor, as cleared)
- **Structure:** rickety wooden ladders up; a wooden ring lining the tower's edge every ten feet.
- **Ground:** the front entrance, once closed; traps on entry (Xuanzang narrowly dodges falling rocks).
- **Second floor:** an **Old Vontir mural**.
- **Third floor:** **greased** (Sir and Buckley slide down safely, following Hans' lead); a painted **wolf's-head shield** (Sir's) and some books.
- **Fourth floor:** **dias** and a kitchen — a **dragon figurine** and a scorched quarterstaff.
- **Fifth floor:** **monk statues, each with a glyph — four meditating, one holding kamas**; aligning the statues opens the base of the kama statue: **five gems (green, red, yellow, deep blue, white-silver), a scroll, and a tarnished silver dagger**. A rope is run across the statues so Xuanzang can shimmy to the door.
- **The "Winds of Balance":** the order's practice — *rise with the sun, descend with the moon* — with an element↔direction mapping; the full poem is in [[campaigns/team-b/sessions/session-13|Session 13]].
- **The wind dials:** seven dials of wind directions on the third floor; **up-up-up / up-down-down-down** (Orenstein brute-forces the 128 combos in under 15 minutes) locks them and a **cyclone of wind** erupts — Buckley is tossed for 7 damage; Sir catches Orenstein.
- **The metal safe:** a leather scroll tube, a corked glass vial of pearly white liquid, and a leather pouch of **20 gp in old [[zazi/factions/vontir-empire|Vontir]] coinage** (eagle and face sides).
- **The overlook portal:** inactive; two flying monsters to the west.
- **The [[mountains/items/leyline-scroll|Ley Line navigation scroll]]** (Old Vontir) — from the safe's scroll tube; its text is quoted in [[campaigns/team-b/sessions/session-14|Session 14]].

## The ending of the second act
Xuanzang practices the meditation and an **eight-foot yin-yang carpet** answers; the party rides it up along the **[[mountains/worldbuilding/leylines|ridge leyline]]** — and something large approaches the tower as the session ends.

## In play
- The tower is the place [[mountains/npcs/rhiltannaxis|Rhiltannäxis]] waits for — and where [[campaigns/team-b/characters/thora|Thora]]'s quest to fight him lives.
