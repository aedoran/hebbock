# ☯️ Yin-yang tomb

> A cave of **yin-yang symbols** where the notes end on a long rest (Session 20, 3/16/2025) — the campaign's current frontier. Where it sits relative to [[mountains/locations/stonehaven|Stonehaven]] and the towers is **not yet established** — see [[_questions|Open Questions]].

## What's known
- **The entrance:** a cave mouth marked with yin-yang symbols; a long group talk about life, death, and politics waits at the threshold (Session 17).
- **Air and earth:** the air **gusts**, a **mummified monk**, and **five points in the ground** the party ponders before moving deeper (Session 18); a **deep pit with rising wind**; a **stinking bog** with human bones and an **elf skull** (brains slurped).
- **The architecture is deliberate** — "by Frank Lloyd Wright" (as written); a **shrieking-fungus room**; a coin marked with an **eye and a sword**.
- **Inhabitants encountered:** a **fish-person with a claw-topped staff** (carrying a fish, a pincher, a cast-iron hook), pursued out of the session (Session 20); **giant toads** (Session 18); **frog-reptiles** (Session 19); a **huge goblin with a greatsword** (attacks an illusory [[campaigns/team-b/npcs/kim|Kim]]; dropped by battleaxe + Kick Galore).
- **Loot in the bog:** a backpack with climber kits, silk ropes, gems, a scroll tube, 2 potions, a "spider coin," a gold plaque (worth 50), and 12 straps (worth 50sp).

## The bearded face
On the wall, found by Buckley in the session's final fight: a **large bearded face in impressive stonework** — likely **dwarven**. Xuanzang may recognize it (the notes gesture at "perhaps Moradin?" from a book of Hebbock history: "after the wars… northeastern wars, orc wars"). Sir's thought: *fish-people and dwarves have more in common than she thought* — and she has killed a part of herself.
