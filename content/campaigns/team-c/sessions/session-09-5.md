# 📓 Session 9.5 — Khortum's Pilgrimage

**Date (as written):** 2/22/26 · **Previous:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-09|Session 9]] · **Next:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-10|Session 10]]

> "What happened to Khortum…"

## Beats
- [[campaigns/team-c/characters/khortum-fil|Khortum]] **sees a group of Loxodons** in past-era [[zazi/locations/zazi|Zazi]] and **follows "almost with no accord of his own"** — "drawn to the herd he has never experienced in the present."
- He follows them to **a temple devoted to his lost god — the [[zazi/worldbuilding/lost-one|Lost One]]** — "again, something Khortum has never experienced."
  - **Roadside shrines and hidden stations of devotion** "were shadows of the temple before him."
  - The temple: **cool, made of stone, dimly lit, roiling with incense smoke.**
- Khortum **sits before a large statue of the Lost One**, closes his eyes "to find the serenity and purpose impossible in the present time."
- "**It is the end of a pilgrimage across time.**"
- "**Khortum is imbued with a purpose. The herd must be protected. The Lost One has brought him here, from the lonely island.**"

## Threads opened
- The herd in past-era Zazi — the one he's now sworn to protect (see [[campaigns/team-c/characters/khortum-fil|Khortum Fil]]).
- The Lost One, alive in the past, with a real temple — vs. dead/absent in the present.
- "From the lonely island" — the Loxodon home (see [[zazi/factions/loxodon-kingdom|Loxodon Kingdom]]).
