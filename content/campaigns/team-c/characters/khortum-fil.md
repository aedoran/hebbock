# 🐘 Khortum Fil

> **Loxodon paladin.** "Takes things slow, slow but strong." (CAST OF CHARACTERS)

## Known
- **AC 19**, warhammer (the "Hammer of Justice" when it cooperates), shield ("Shield of Faith"), javelin.
- Carries **a fragment of his lost god's soul** — the [[zazi/worldbuilding/lost-one|Lost One]], god of the [[zazi/factions/loxodon-kingdom|Loxodons]], is dead or absent in the present; alive in the past. (The DM ruled: "the god spread out the soul before" — Khortum's time-travel is *not* the cause of the god's faltering.)
- "Slow" is a combat fact, not just a quirk: in Session 3 the party decides "Khortum needs to swing slower."
- **Serenity** — the secret word that calms the donkey (Session 10 plan; used on Lachma-as-ass).
- From "the lonely island" (Session 9.5) — the Loxodon home.
- In the past, **shocked to see Loxodons at all** (Session 8) — then drawn "almost with no accord of his own" to the herd and to the temple (Session 9.5).

## Key moments
- **Session 1:** intercepts the pickpocket; "Khortum, alarmed, agrees" to adventure.
- **Session 3:** Shield of Faith up, the boss gnoll falls to the warhammer; eats nine goodberry poops.
- **Session 4:** believes the feline-embossed chest "is related to an ancient god or religion"; leads the march with shield and hooded lantern.
- **Session 5:** his lantern is borrowed by Lachma on the ladder down.
- **Session 6:** collapses into a trance at the trapdoor reveal; the party props his head with the zebra rug ("make sure the ears are safe"); SCOUT keeps watch.
- **Session 7:** "Face the avatar of the Lost One!"; smashes a skeleton into dust.
- **Session 9.5:** the pilgrimage — the temple, the statue, "the end of a pilgrimage across time." "Khortum is imbued with a purpose. **The herd must be protected.**"
- **Session 10:** calms the ass with "serenity" (the plan's linchpin); accepts Bishop's fruit "as a gesture of friendship."
- **Session 11:** offers the gnoll "a chance to repent"; the gnoll spits; the divine smite lands.
- **Session 12:** the Lost One answers — every blow against his shield meets "the image of a **ramming elephant made of holy light**."
- **Session 13:** strikes the last pursuing warforged down; scoops up the knocked-out one.
