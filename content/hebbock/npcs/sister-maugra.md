# Sister Maugra

> A dwarven cleric of the [[hebbock/factions/vault-of-dumathoin|Vault of Dumathoin]] — the one the gods protect (touch her and take a *Sacred Flame*), the one who shoulders the party aside and says, "Let us handle this."

## Overview
Maugra is the expedition's second sponsor and its quietest storm. She took internal injuries in the golem fight, led the party to the [[hebbock/locations/cathedral-of-dumathoin|Cathedral of Dumathoin]], and — in Session III — simply *walked away*, down the gorge, alone, into the cathedral.

## Details
- Race: dwarf
- Role: cleric, Vault of Dumathoin
- Note: the gods "do not look kindly on those who touch Sister Maugra."

## Relationships
- [[hebbock/npcs/brother-hethrin|Brother Hethrin]] — fellow cleric; "I could not stop her. She told me to stay, to see you succeed."
- [[hebbock/npcs/cororic|Cororic]] — the one she went to meet, alone.

## Where She Is
- **Inside the Cathedral of Dumathoin, alone** — as of Session III's end. Her fate is **not yet handled** in the chronicle (aedoran's ruling) — an open thread into Session V and beyond.

## Appearances
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-01|Session I]] — between the party and the widow; wounded in the golem fight.
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-02|Session II]] — "Behold — the Vault of Dumathoin. And that is where Cororic is."; the traverse rope.
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-03|Session III]] — walks away; opens the great doors; goes inside.
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-04|Session IV]] — absent. Unknown.
