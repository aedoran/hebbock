# 🎲 Session 16 — Dragontown

Three days of downtime in the hidden dragonborn community of Vryrrk Basta Caverns: library lore, Bardstock, and the road to the cave with the yin-yang symbol carved into the rock.

## What Happened

- After 3 days of downtime, the party levels (to 3rd). The caverns hold a vibrant community of dragonborn with many downtime options — the entrance is hidden, stone stairs lead down into a dark, vast cave with torches lit like fireflies in the distance.
- Orenstein Alabaster has access to the library and reads for three days. He finds a parchment that looks like pig skin (or "foot long pig skin" as written): *The Curious Case of Argyle McBottoms* — this version says Argyle is driven mad, jumps off a cliff, and lands paralyzed with his eyes pecked out.
- Buckley was barding: "Bard orgy! Bardstock, man. Smoke a doob! And a boob!" — the session's **Dude** quotes continue unabated.
- Sir has been spending time with barbarian dragonborn among the wild, performing hunts and feats of strength.
- Xohn meets a dragonborn monk and the two discuss the yin-yang symbol. A day away is a cave in which the symbol is carved. Is it a tomb? The monk corpse is unmolested (hope so!).
- Orenstein finds a clue: among three large scrolls in unusually large scroll cases, in the same language as Xohn's scroll, the towers' decline matches that of the Old Vontir Empire — "After the long war, despite victory, the Old Vontir empire collapsed." The author fled the city and settled in the dwarven settlement **Rockhaven**. The author's name is made up on the spot: player Cotter decides it is **Yongo the Younger**. The year is 3,334 — but "their calendar year is super weird, so it's not the future, but the past even though the number is bigger."
- "The Order of the Uncarved Block descends from the ancients. It predates science. Myriad moons ago." (as written)
- Orenstein's imp arrives after Orenstein knocks himself out by communion with the Great Old One. Orenstein runs the gamut of summons trying to find one he likes, but the task is impossible — all four are horrifically flawed. (as written)
- The party picks a direction: fight Rhiltannäxis (written "Réaltenaxis" as written) at the kobold tower (Thora's quest — "she's not Jewish as far as we know"), go to the monk tomb, or return to the original tower. "To the Unmolested Tomb!" "Let's get leid!"
- On the way, they stumble on a creature in a mountain valley. Beezos flies up to take a "visual inspection." Orenstein's attempt to sneak to a safe spot fails: the ogre's javelin finds purchase in Orenstein's guts. Bezos opportunistically attacks the ogre charging Xohn; the ogre smashes Bezos into oblivion.
- The fight: Buckley takes cover and casts *Reduce* ("It's a nice day for ensmallenenen"); Xohn's Flurry of Blows ends with a kick to the "dwarven-skin-covered ogre junk" — "an amazing round of martial prowess from the halfling monk." The ogre stays upright with its tree club; Sir's attack is absorbed by layers of pelts; Buckley lands a "devastating insult" and the ogre's nose is bleeding; from cover, Orenstein's Eldritch Blast delivers the killing blow.
- The ogre's bag: a collection of moldy cheese, battered helmets, and animal fur.
- A short rest; Orenstein summons a sprite. The party arrives at the cave with the yin-yang symbol carved into the rock.

## World Changes

- The party reaches 3rd level after the downtime.
- New lore established: the towers' decline tracks the collapse of the Old Vontir Empire after the long war; the manuscript's author fled to the dwarven settlement Rockhaven (Yongo the Younger, 3,334 by a weird calendar — the past, not the future).
- The yin-yang cave is located a day's travel away, its contents (a monk corpse) so far unmolested.
