# Cororic's Compass

> [[hebbock/npcs/cororic|Cororic]]'s compass — found in the debris of the first magnetite golem, its **needle spinning uselessly**.

## Description
A compass of the lost holy man, recovered from the golem fight in Session I. Its needle spins uselessly — a fitting omen for a delve where compasses are useless and the walls themselves pull at the party's metal.

## Properties
- The needle spins uselessly. Whether that is the golem's magnetism, the delve's magic, or something pointed at the Witness is unknown.
