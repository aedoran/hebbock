# 🍃 High Elven leaf treasure

> A chest of **four velvet pouches** (leaf-marked) with an **Elvish riddle plaque**, found behind a secret door at the [[mountains/locations/blackrock-peak|Blackrock Peak watchtower]] — alongside racks of broken weapons (Session 5–6). All items are golden-leaf-marked, in the style of the High Elven King [[mountains/npcs/kalvulaen-viltannen|Kalvulaen Vil'Tannen]] of Vil'Tanee.

## Contents (as taken)
- **Gold ring, leaf design** — now on [[campaigns/team-b/characters/sir|Sir]]'s pinky (she bit it to test; "really into her new ring").
- **Tortoise amulet, golden leaf** — a symbol of Kalvulaen Vil'Tannen.
- **A large golden circlet** (another leaf) — worn by [[campaigns/team-b/characters/xohn|Xohn]] — **and it bears the mark of the traitor** (Session 8).
- **Three d20s, each etched with a leaf.**

## The trap
The chest is a trap: Orenstein's mage hand lifts a bag and **two suits of armor and four flying swords** spring from the chest (S6) — "Hi, Cotter — the cartographers could be nicer." The animated armor is later crushed by Thora's warhammer; Xohn headbutts it (S7/8 area, as the session pages tell it).
