# 🐐 Xohn

> The halfling monk the party calls **Xohn** — the GOAT. "WU-TANG 4EVA." (Session 1.)

## Who
- A member of the **[[mountains/factions/order-of-uncarved-block|Order of the Uncarved Block]]** who **broke a rule** and was sent on a **quest to find balance again** — he doesn't know how. He knows little of the order's undercover/spy subset.
- **Equipment:** darts (and "kick galore"), a **Kaiser monkey figurine named Hans** ("Do you want to see my monkey??" — "Unleash the Hun!"), a **helmeted ape figurine** (a WWI-marauder type), and the **circlet** of the [[campaigns/team-b/items/high-elven-leaf-treasure|High Elven leaf treasure]] — **which bears the mark of the traitor** (Session 8).
- **The attunement:** in Session 7, Sir notices a subtle change — Xohn has "the hunter's attunement."
- **The scrolls:** he carries a scroll in the same (unnamed) language as the [[mountains/items/leyline-scroll|leyline scroll]] and the Vryrrk Basta library manuscript.

## In play
- **S1–S7:** assembles the party in [[mountains/locations/stonehaven|Stonehaven]]; kicks the unseen foe in the junk; "Xohn's kick galore!" on the spider; the secret door to the weapon cache; "Xohn falls. Makes his first save."
- **S8–S9:** the leaf treasure goes on him; the rappelling and the parapet ghost; "Xohn Xohn Xohn!"
- **S10–S14:** the hole trap; the falling-rocks trap (he "feels a bit dumb"); shimmies across the fifth-floor rope; brute-force the wind dials with Orenstein; **practices the meditation and an 8-foot yin-yang carpet answers**; flies the carpet (concentration wavers in the cold; willpower holds; an air rapid drops them).
- **S15–S20:** the carpet flight; at Vryrrk Basta, takes the **Path of the Open Hand**; kicks and kills the frog-reptile; may recognize the **bearded face** ("perhaps Moradin?" from the History of Hebbock — "the northeastern wars, orc wars").
