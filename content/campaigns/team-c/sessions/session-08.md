# 📓 Session 8 — The Sands of Time

**Date (as written):** 1/25/26 · **Previous:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-07|Session 7]] · **Next:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-09|Session 9]]

## Beats
- **The rest:** the party recovers after the mummy's sand-filled tomb; it's time to leave the [[zazi/locations/pyramid-of-tiberesh|pyramid]]; Lachma heals himself.
- **The shift:** a deep rumble from the pyramid's base; the floor shifts; the party is knocked off balance; dust shakes from the ceiling; large stone cracks.
- **The golden doors, changed:**
  - "Greg's mom is there, texting everyone that the schools in New York City are closed." (the notes' meta-voice)
  - The sandswept path is **replaced with a spacescape**; the entrance has a **"shimmering glass" appearance** — "a foreign sky, swirling incomprehensibly."
  - Among the constellations: **the symbol of Enoreth, the god of time** — recognized by Lachma and Di.
  - The rumbling gives way to **absolute silence.**
- **The arms:** **two arms reach through the portal** — one a **black humanoid**; the other a smaller arm **giving the ok symbol.**
- **The Chamber of Stars:** Khortum hustles into the stars, guided beyond the portal; the others follow.
  - A **50-foot cylinder room.**
  - **[[zazi/npcs/sandstone|Sandstone]]** — "a older human in heavily worn armor."
  - **[[zazi/npcs/zing|Zing]]** — "a female gnome with a sparkle in her eyes and goggles on her head... the artificer extraordinaire."
  - "We are glad you made it out. **Welcome to a chamber of stars.**" (Zing rolls her eyes.)
  - Khortum thanks them; "There isn't much time."
  - Introductions begin — **Sandstone stops him: "We know who you are."** Khortum: "… Oh good, that will save some time."
  - **The walls are stars and constellations, "the aeons of space."** The portal behind has sealed.
  - **Flickering images:** flying ships made of **metal**, beams of light across the sky, **pyramids.** "These magical pictures are presented to us as flickering images."
  - **The send-off:** an exit appears. Zing: "This will be fun!" Sandstone: "They're not going, but the party has to go. This will lead right back to the inn Nuzul in which you began. **Find Hazi. Give him the map.** Things will be different, but go now. **We'll see you in the future.**" Zing, laughing: "Nice one, buddy."
  - Lachma clarifies (the order). Khortum says ok. The party jumps.
- **The past:** they appear in front of the [[zazi/locations/nuzul-inn|Nuzul Inn]] —
  - Same placard, **new coat of paint; a crescent on the sign**; the building looks **newer.**
  - **Different crowds:** the cat-people [[zazi/creatures/nkosi|Nkosi]] are "a higher percentage"; the **humans are dressed differently** (fashion, designs).
  - "And somehow, someway, **we are in the distant past!**"
  - Passersby give [[campaigns/team-c/characters/scout|SCOUT]] "very strange looks" — he **hoods up.** "I've got a bad feeling about this."
  - Lachma "intuitively knows we're somehow in a parallel existence."
  - **[[campaigns/team-c/characters/khortum-fil|Khortum]] sees two Loxodons and is shocked** — "shocked to his core." They take no notice of him.
  - **[[campaigns/team-c/characters/bishop|Bishop]] speaks! From the mind! Psychic whispers.** (His telepathy — present in the past, not in the present — is established here.)
- **The big questions (the notes' own list):**
  - Khortum: "can I go home and see my land and its people flourishing?" If he carries part of his god's soul and **the god is alive now** — what does that mean for him, and for the god? Does time apply to gods? **Is Khortum the reason his god falters?** — *(DM ruling in the notes: No — "the god spread out the soul before.")*
  - **Lachma:** through "a greater understanding of dreams," he understands that **[[zazi/npcs/bimbubop|Bimbubop]] is a conduit of fae communication to this world** — reaching out to see if Lachma can be a **Fae champion.**
  - **SCOUT:** worried, "knowing the origins of the warforged and how they were used."
  - **[[campaigns/team-c/characters/di-guan|Di]]:** "can I meet my ancestors?" — and he **can now summon a celestial hawk named Peng.**
- **The session's closing question:** "What does this time-travel mean for our heroes?"
