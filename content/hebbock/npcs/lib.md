# Lib

> A kuo-toa cultist of unusual sanity — the meeker of the two, whipped into a frenzy by promises of baptism and salvation, who made the twenty-foot leap and died the way he lived: enthusiastically.

## Overview
Lib was the other kuo-toa hidden in the gorge's alcove, with [[hebbock/npcs/glul|Glul]]. When Randælfine's "squeeze out the magic" deception and Vlix's ice-cream-truck jingle hit him, Lib — the meeker one — *leapt*. He rolled a one. He belly-flopped headfirst into six inches of water, grappled his goblin, was cut clean in half at the gut by a scimitar, and died mid-enthusiasm in the [[hebbock/locations/silt-pool|Silt Pool]].

## Details
- Race: kuo-toa
- Role: cultist, Silt Pool
- State: **deceased** (Session III, the Battle of the Silt Pool)

## Personality
- Meeker than Glul; easily roused; dies the way he lives — enthusiastically.
