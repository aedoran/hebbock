# Miscallers

> Eyeless, four-limbed, ape-quick horrors — pale as drowned flesh, all claw and hiss. Named by the party for what they can do with a voice: **throw other creatures' voices** (ventriloquism), luring their prey into the dark.

## Biology / Nature
- Eyeless; see by some other sense (they "peered around the corner" of Baelson's torchlight in Session I).
- Four limbs; move with ape-quickness.
- Clawed; "claws no person possesses" — they opened [[hebbock/npcs/vesque|Vesque]].
- All claw and hiss; no mouth visible enough to explain the thrown voices (a party mystery — how do the eyeless *speak*?).

## Habitat
- The upper halls of the [[hebbock/locations/hargate-delve|Hargate Delve]].

## Behavior
- **The lure:** they throw a victim's voice (or a known person's voice) to draw the party off course or into isolation. Pim's voice screamed in agony from down the hall — the monster was *in front of them*.
- **The false trail:** they'll feint a pursuit into a tunnel they never entered (the mist-deception of Session II).
- They are patient, and they are *game* — Randælfine felt "professional respect."

## Threat
- High. They hit hard, flee smart, and fight in multiples. A *Vicious Mockery* insulted one — it shrugged it off.

## Notable Encounters
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-01|Session I]] — two burst from the dark as the session ended; the "I found the way!" voice was their lure over [[hebbock/npcs/vesque|Vesque]]'s body.
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-02|Session II]] — the party names them; one escapes into the dark.
