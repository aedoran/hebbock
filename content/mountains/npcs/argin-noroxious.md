# 🐲 Argin Noroxious

> A dragonborn greeter of [[mountains/locations/vryrrk-basta-caverns|Vryrrk Basta Caverns]] (Session 16, 2/22/2025) — "he loved Buckley's performance greatly, so we are to be introduced to the tribe."

## What he does
- Greets the party at the hidden stone stairs and **shows them the way to the cavern** after [[campaigns/team-b/characters/sir|Sir]] introduces herself as a friend of [[campaigns/team-b/characters/thora|Thora]].
- The party is then introduced to the five dragonborn who await them, including the venerable priest [[mountains/npcs/thrallax|Thrallax]].

## In play
- Argin is the door into the hidden dragonborn community — and the first confirmation that Thora's people are real and waiting.
