# 🎲 Session 17 — The Cave of the Yin-Yang

The party stands at the entrance to the cave of the yin-yang symbol — and first spends a long time talking about life, death, and politics, then about D&D, before they play D&D.

## What Happened

- The party stands at the entrance to the cave with the yin-yang symbols.
- As a group, they spend a long time talking about life, death, and politics — then "talked about D&D instead of playing it." Ben points out that they "haven't yet Orenstein play" (as written). A certain "Hello AI!" appears in the notes as well (as written).
- Then they played! The site is the conjunction of two mountain ridges, where two large slabs converge with a gap, creating the cave with the yin-yang symbol. Xuanzang / Xohn's spiritual journey is at the precipice of another chapter. Sir has provided snacks.
- Xuanzang / Xohn's initial investigation reveals no additional secrets. The weather begins to turn to rain.
- Orenstein Alabaster dismisses Kim ("ruuuuuude; she snaps fingers in the air"), and Xuanzang / Xohn searches for traps.
- After the entrance, the 20'x30' room opens up — no moss or lichen, but a breeze (Summer breeze / makes me feel fine / blowing through the jasmine in my miiiinnnnnnddd). The room continues, with a shadowy figure in meditation, and in the center of the room something on the floor that looks like divots but could be identified with closer examination.
- Xuanzang / Xohn brings his torch closer to examine the divots; Sir and Orenstein enter cautiously. Buckley noodles on a tune called "Ass of Colossus."
- The divots: a circle with slots.
- When Xuanzang / Xohn steps past, this becomes the cave of the breaking wind! Smelly. The party must make Athletics checks: Xuanzang / Xohn and Sir hold fast and the torch goes out; the others are blown out. Sir moves against the wind and eventually gets blown out. Xuanzang / Xohn settles in to meditate, to go with the flow.
- Sir and Orenstein cannot make it back into the cave after trying their own ways. Sir says she has solved the puzzle — it was difficult, but "we have to go… the other way."
- Xuanzang / Xohn is also blown out of the cave and wonders how they are going to explore it.
