# 🍺 Nuzul Inn

> The tavern where the [[campaigns/team-c/README|Team C]] party assembles — and the doorway between two eras.

## The present (Sessions 1, 8–10)
- Where [[campaigns/team-c/characters/di-guan|Di Guan]] arrives by boat and orders ceremonial tea from [[zazi/npcs/fatima|Fatima]], the "professional wench."
- Where [[campaigns/team-c/characters/khortum-fil|Khortum]] recruits the pickpocket, where [[campaigns/team-c/characters/scout|SCOUT]] recruits Di, and "the avengers assemble."
- In the **past**, the party reappears *in front of* the same inn after the Chamber of Stars — same placard, new coat of paint, a **crescent on the sign**, a newer look, a more chaotic and diverse crowd.
- Bishop's first telepathy demonstration happens at its dart board; the party keeps a room there in both eras; hostile cloaked figures are seen watching the inn at night (Sessions 12–13).

## Notes
- The "crescent on the sign" (past) vs. the bare placard (present) is an unexplained detail — possible open question.
- Greg (player) is referenced in the notes' meta-voice ("it's difficult for Greg to count that many" — three or four blocks to Karima).
