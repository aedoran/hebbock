# Cororic's Notebook

> A metal-bound notebook written in the **scratch-script of the kuo-toa**, found in the debris of the first magnetite golem. One phrase, repeated over and over:

> *"It's not a wishing well. It's a question."*

## Description
Metal-bound, covered in kuo-toa scratch-script. [[hebbock/npcs/sister-maugra|Sister Maugra]] translated the single repeated phrase in Session I.

## Properties
- The phrase is a riddle — and possibly the arc's key. *What* is not a wishing well, and *what* is the question?
- Written in kuo-toa script by a dwarf holy man — implying Cororic spent time with the kuo-toa (or the deep) before his fall.
