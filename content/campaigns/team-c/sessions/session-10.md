# 📓 Session 10 — A Crazy Ass Plan

**Date (as written):** 2/22/26 · **Previous:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-09-5|Session 9.5]] · **Next:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-11|Session 11]]

## Beats
- **The morning after the pilgrimage:**
  - Di stayed up all night reading. **Khortum slept at the temple**, woken by "an adherent sweeping the sand"; returns to the inn, content. **SCOUT stays outside.**
  - Breakfast: Bishop offers fruit to Khortum "as a gesture of friendship"; Khortum accepts. Lachma brings everyone up to speed.
- **Young [[zazi/npcs/hazi-sucineri|Hazi]]** enters the inn — "his hair waving dramatically"; sits, stands, "unnecessarily dramatic... obnoxiously performative confidence."
  - "Karima is looking for help. I can give directions to where she is 'holding court.'"
  - "Thank you for the maps, they will be put to good use."
  - Directions: **three or four blocks away** ("it is difficult for Greg to count that many"); the gnolls "may be intimidating, but we can announce our presence and intention honestly."
  - Lachma: "Do the gnolls have any problems with any people we should know about?" Hazi: gnolls should be fine. "**Karima likes meeting people in both high and low places. She does not discriminate.**"
  - "Hazi, as information broker, has completed his job." He leaves.
- **The vibe check:** Khortum calms Lachma's anxiety; Di is excited "to possibly meet his ancestors"; SCOUT is freaked out as the only warforged; Bishop takes in information and overhears:
  - **Skyhammers** — airships "with magical sails, made from northern trees and fueled by dark magic and the elements."
  - **Hidden, buried treasure!** And **djinn, "if they are real."**
- **The comm plan:** SCOUT → Bishop (telepathy) → Di (telepathy) → the party (loud). "Lachma is talking on behalf of a mouse, Bishop is telepathically communicating with scout and Di, and Khortum talks slow as an elephant does."
- **SCOUT's account:** the wizards who made the warforged "fought each other"; "somehow, my independence was granted or earned"; "my memory may have been erased many times over"; "I theoretically could be here, now! With another version of myself with a different memory." Di is very concerned; "seeks serenity through battle."
- **[[zazi/npcs/karima|Karima's tent]]** (three/four blocks, in the liminal space):
  - "The tent is not a formal affair. Many species are present, and the scents of coffee and exotic spices linger in the air."
  - Karima's description: "She carries herself with grace and elegance. A long purple cloak covers her slender limbs and a yellow headscarf frames a face that is clearly canine, yet also beautiful. She has long, dark eyebrows, bright, flashing eyes, and smooth, lustrous fur." "She neither barks nor bites."
  - She is gracious after Di's polite introduction.
- **The job:**
  - Her **scholar friend was found dead this morning** — "had been studying Bastet, deciphering texts." His body was found **by the River of Sand.**
  - Retrieve his body **and his belongings** from the **[[zazi/locations/charnel-house|charnel house]]**, run by **[[zazi/npcs/sultan-shukranil|the Sultan]]** — "Sultan Shuk-ri Nill Mo Chatoor." "The Sultan hates Karima and will do anything to hurt her."
  - **The price: 500 gold, gratitude, and "maybe a passionate night for anyone into furries (not Khortum)."**
  - The friend: **[[zazi/npcs/tellya-hakkam|(Tellya Inasec) Hakkam]]** — "His name is Hakam, and last I saw he was wearing what was a brown robe with some stars on the shoulder. He had a necklace, gold necklace, with a sun medallion. Brown hair, human. Force the shells off a little bit."
  - "The charnel [house] is near **the great sand pyramid, which is not the pyramid of Tibberish**." (The second-pyramid thread.)
  - **Rendezvous: "the southeastern tip of the pyramid near the Jackal's Run Bridge at dawn tomorrow."**
  - Warning: "Sultan Shukranil Mo Shator is not someone to trifle with. He's cunning and he'll stop at nothing to protect his interests. If he suspects you're working for me, well, just be prepared for anything. Okay?"
- **Bishop cases the joint:**
  - "About 15 feet tall. Bell-shaped. Made out of sandstone. No windows... kind of like a handbell. An entrance... maybe an iron door... smoke coming from some opening at the top. **No backdoor. No guards outside.**"
  - "Bashtoor Street, Cornel House, Dash. Hours of opening, dawn to dusk."
- **The plan (the "crazy ass plan"):**
  - Lachma + Khortum bring **Bishop in as a corpse**; **Di causes a commotion**; tweaks: **Lachma becomes a donkey** and goes crazy as a distraction; "guards are not part of the game, and they yell and chase us out" — that's Bishop's window to find the body; **Khortum arrives and calms the donkey with the secret word "serenity"**; **SCOUT keeps the perimeter.**
  - Di rings the bell. The iron door creaks open. "From the gnoll's voice, a hint of madness, something unhinged. He is somber." — "**Welcome to the charnel house. I am the sultan.**" Hints of a manic smile.
  - Ground floor: a small platform; a stairway winding down to the east; **a blazing charcoal pit**; a **trio of wax-encrusted chandeliers**.
  - Two robed gnolls — "poorly concealed meat cleavers" and **spice pouches**. Di telepathically to Bishop: the gnolls intend to eat corpses. Bishop: "**Fuck.**"
  - "These are my assistants at the house of the dead. I see you need some help disposing of a body. A loved one? A friend?" — "A friend." — "We'd be happy to help him on his journey to the afterlife." — "Thank you. My ass is nervous."
  - The outside bell rings; the Sultan answers; **Lachma-as-ass begins to flip out**, intending to charge down the stairs with Bishop on his back.
  - Lachma sprints to the bottom — **the lower mortuary:** a wide firepit; timber; a low wall; **human-size iron carts used in the fire pits.** One gnoll gets kicked in the face.
  - Khortum pleads that he can calm the ass. A **door with a stained-glass panel**; **four alcoves on the north side, a body each.**
  - Lachma bucks Bishop off (safely). A gnoll "licks its lips near the corpses."
  - **A beautiful young woman in a robe, grieving** (the green-smoke woman), sees the scene; the gnolls are pushed aside, spices spill.
  - **The Sultan and the woman argue; he slams the door on her. "A different cloud escapes up from the chimney."**
  - "The party rolls for initiative, at the precipice of adventure!"
  - The Sultan cackles: "**No one interrupts feeding time, you have no idea who you're messing with.**"
  - "**Also, a parrot flies out.**"
