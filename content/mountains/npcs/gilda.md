# 🍻 Gilda

> The **town drunk** of [[mountains/locations/stonehaven|Stonehaven]] — "a lady dwarf" (as written, Session 1, 9/1/2024). The only one who knows where the old mountain prison is.

## What she is
- Chats with the party in the [[mountains/npcs/thraine|Mountain's Respite]] about Thora's vision — *rusty gates, packed in old snow, strange noises and lights*.
- "Doesn't want to go down the tomb."
- Has a **"shituational memory"** — Sir basically has to threaten her before she gives anything up, and then **falls asleep**.
- Her signature move: **the biggest, most disgusting burp**, delivered straight into Orenstein's face.

## In play
- Gilda is the unreliable source for the prison's location — a classic bar-stool map. The party never managed to get her to actually go with them.
