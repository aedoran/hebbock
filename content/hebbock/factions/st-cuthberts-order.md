# Order of St. Cuthbert

> The order that [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/baelson|Baelson]] claims to serve — the **Order of St. Cuthbert**. His "righteous fury," his "law of men," his prayers in the dark: all addressed to this order.

## Overview
Baelson — the self-styled **Avenging Night Angel of St. Cuthbert** — prays to this order, invokes its justice, and announces its fury. It is the foundation of his whole character: grim, zealot, loud.

**It is real.** Aedoran's ruling: the Order of St. Cuthbert is a genuine order in Hebbock — not Baelson's self-styled fantasy. Baelson, then, is a very loud and very wrong member of a real institution. What that means — other members, houses, relics, how the order reacts to the Night Angel's methods — is all to be discovered in play.
