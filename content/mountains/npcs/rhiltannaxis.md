# 🐲 Rhiltannäxis

> A **red dragon** — "a cleric of one of the fire drake deities of Bahamut" (Session 9, 12/8/2024). The dragon the kobolds hope to offer the party to, and the one [[campaigns/team-b/characters/thora|Thora]]'s quest points at.

## Who
- A dragon of the [[mountains/README|mountain region]], associated with the **[[mountains/locations/kobold-air-tower|kobold air tower]]** on the Eastern Ridge — a dragon sighted in the night sky during Session 9.
- His clerical role (a fire drake deity of Bahamut) is unusual and unexplained — one of the campaign's open threads.

## In play
- **Thora's quest: fight him at the kobold tower** (per the notes' account). The party has not yet met him in the notes as written — the "something large" that approaches the tower as Session 14 ends may be the first sign of it. See [[_questions|Open Questions]].
