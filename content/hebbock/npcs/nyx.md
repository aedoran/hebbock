# Nyx

> A goblin entrepreneur of the mushroom trade, much put-upon. Chief of the workers at the [[hebbock/locations/silt-pool|Silt Pool]].

## Overview
Nyx is not a warrior; he's a businessman whose livelihood is the silt. When the party won the Battle of the Silt Pool, he was dunked headfirst in the mud by [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/baelson|Baelson]], protested his people's innocence — *"All we want is the mushrooms! You fishes are ridiculous!"* — and, ransacked, sold the party the [[hebbock/items/eye-stone|Eye Stone]] in exchange for a freedom he *completely believed* was real (Randælfine's deception roll of ten).

## Details
- Race: goblin
- Role: chief / mushroom entrepreneur, Silt Pool
- State: alive, charmed, and gone

## Personality
- The voice of the worker: "You fishes are ridiculous."
- Insists the four gold pieces are his.
- A trader first: the stone "came in trade from a fish-person."

## Relationships
- [[hebbock/npcs/gri-the-asht-chewer|Gri the Asht-Chewer]] — the shaman who cooks the [[hebbock/items/brain-cane|brain cane]].
- [[hebbock/npcs/glul|Glul]] & [[hebbock/npcs/lib|Lib]] — the kuo-toa of the gorge; Glul led the party to the camp.
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/vlix-boggs|Vlix]] — charmed her outright (DM rolled a one); she learned the stone's true nature from him.

## Where He Is
- In the dark. He "splashed off into the darkness" after the last free goblin wailed "Leave Nyx alone!" — Baelson let him go: "A goblin in hand is worth two in the silt."

## Appearances
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-03|Session III]] — the Silt Pool, the dunking, the charmed sale of the Eye Stone.
