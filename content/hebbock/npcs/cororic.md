# Cororic

> A holy man of the [[hebbock/factions/vault-of-dumathoin|Vault of Dumathoin]] who vanished into the deep a generation ago — now a smiling old dwarf standing pleasantly over the body of a small boy, conducting the counting dark.

## Overview
The party was hired to bring Cororic home, kindly. What they found instead: a keeper of treasures who went mad searching for something the vault did not hold — his carvings fill the cathedral's antechamber, saner near the door, madder toward the back: *"It's not here. It's not here."* → *"This is not the prize."* He is now [[hebbock/npcs/the-witness|the Witness]]'s herald, and "the one from the stone" (the [[hebbock/items/eye-stone|Eye Stone]] showed him smiling over a body, right before its holder died).

## Details
- Race: dwarf
- Former role: holy man / keeper of treasures, Vault of Dumathoin
- State: alive, entranced — smiling, *pleasant*, and counting

## Appearance
A small, smiling old dwarf. Touches people gently. Tilts his head "like a confused dog" when a death he tolls refuses to come.

## Personality
- Pleasant to a terrifying degree. "Welcome! I didn't realize you would come too. It's wonderful."
- He *counts* rather than fights. When Vlix refused to die, he was confused, not angry.
- To [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/baelson|Baelson]]: "Fellow paladin, there is no need for this violence" — which charmed the Night Angel into doubt.
- He is a man who sought and never found. The party (and Baelson, most of all) hears it.

## Relationships
- [[hebbock/npcs/the-witness|The Witness]] — his master; "The eye is at hand!"
- [[hebbock/factions/vault-of-dumathoin|Vault of Dumathoin]] — the order he "abandoned" (Baelson's words); still its lost saint.
- [[hebbock/npcs/brother-hethrin|Brother Hethrin]] — his former brother in the order; Hethrin turned on the party to defend him.
- The small boy — "almost done. Almost counted."

## Where He Is
- [[hebbock/locations/cathedral-of-dumathoin|Cathedral of Dumathoin]] — mid-battle with the party (Session IV end).

## Appearances
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-01|Session I]] — as the missing: his voice calls the townsfolk; his compass and kuo-toa-script notebook are found in the golem debris. *"It's not a wishing well. It's a question."*
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-02|Session II]] — the cathedral is "where Cororic is."
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-03|Session III]] — the party is told "Cororic is inside"; Vlix's Eye-Stone vision is of him.
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-04|Session IV]] — met at last. He fought, fled, and the session ends with him mid-flight from Vlix's Witch Bolt, still smiling.
