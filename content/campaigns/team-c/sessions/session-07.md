# 📓 Session 7 — What the hell is going on?

**Date (as written):** 1/18/26 · **Previous:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-06|Session 6]] · **Next:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-08|Session 8]]

## Beats
- **The morning after the Nkosi fight:** [[campaigns/team-c/characters/khortum-fil|Khortum]] wakes groggy; SCOUT kept watch; the party recounts the battle; Khortum "expresses regret for not being there to protect others."
- **The riddles:** Di struggles ("too many half-platinum words"); Lachma is also confused. **[[campaigns/team-c/characters/bishop|Bishop]] takes a sword from SCOUT to communicate the answers:**
  - North riddle — *I sustain and guide all who live, while caged in a hidden, white prison* → **the heart, trapped in the ribcage** (sword to the chest).
  - South riddle — *I am a twinkling beacon sheathed in stone; we all are drawn to doom* → **a ceremonial dagger.**
- **Bishop's scout run:**
  - North: the path is "coated in sand, pebbles, bone chips"; turns right; he returns to the group.
  - South: a similar hall, with **stairs descending on the left.**
- **The gypsosphinx trigger:** Bishop rounds the corner — deeper debris, more sand, more stones. **60 ft from the corner: an immense statue — a [[zazi/creatures/gypsosphinx|gypsosphinx]]** (feline body, human head, wings), **paws curved around a large cylinder at its base.** "Bishop recognizes this icon!" (which icon? open question.)
  - A click. The statue moves closer. SCOUT: "he must have triggered something."
  - **A portcullis descends in front of the party.** "#triggered."
  - The statue moves **10 ft/round**; Bishop runs back to the safe side; SCOUT hops left; Di sprints south "ensuring no portcullis traps us in the corner"; Lachma and Bishop wait out the sphinx.
  - The portcullis closes; the statue grinds to a halt against it.
  - Di lifts the portcullis with Khortum's help (creaks, groans, gives way); Lachma and SCOUT build a rock wedge to hold it.
- **Climbing over:** Bishop, Khortum, Di easy; **SCOUT injures himself**; Lachma scoots — **Bimbubop falls out of his pocket → Lachma slips** (minor injury). The party is across; another corridor lies to the south.
- **The onyx anglerfish doors:** gigantic, turquoise design; an **anglerfish carved into the door, its nostrils as pull-rings** ("carved into the door, the fish writes it went to Yale"). Two small shelves, two **turquoise runestones each** — **lion, scarab, cat, snake.** Di and SCOUT clear for traps. The tension "mounts, as if it were boiling water." Khortum pulls with all his strength; the doors slowly give way.
  - (Bishop, who "is familiar with oceanic life, marine biology and fish in general, because one time he went to the docks of a port city.")
- **[[zazi/npcs/tiberesh|Tiberesh's tomb]]** (the [[zazi/locations/pyramid-of-tiberesh|Pyramid of Tiberesh]]'s heart):
  - The room extends **80 ft, then turns south**; large sand drifts; **two large braziers**; walls of **translucent alabaster panels**; at center: shallow steps, a platform, a table.
  - In the middle of the room: **a rectangular sarcophagus carved from alabaster.**
  - Bishop skulks the circumference — no obvious danger.
  - **The doorway erupts into flame.** The sand begins filling the room. The flames swirl around the sarcophagus; **the lid slides off, crashing to the floor.**
  - A human-like figure wrapped in lines emerges from **roiling green smoke**:
    > "**I am reborn! I will turn this world upside-down on your behalf!**"
- **The mummy fight:**
  - Di acts first — **diplomacy** ("asking for the news from beyond"). Fails.
  - Khortum charges, shield raised, issuing a challenge; SCOUT fires an arrow — strikes the mummy's chest.
  - Bimbubop does **not** bless the mummy; Lachma's **Infestation** — "a small plague! But the mummy laughs off the spell, chastising Lachma for thinking he could stop the mummy with mere bugs."
  - Bishop's **mind sliver — to no effect.**
  - The mummy lurches forward; **skeletal creatures rise from the sands** and rush SCOUT.
  - The mummy's "bright fist" smashes Di; Lachma rushes in with timely healing.
  - Khortum smashes the mummy while vulnerable: "**Face the avatar of the Lost One!**"
  - The mummy rises to meet Khortum "with hatred." Skeletons target Bishop and Khortum (no effect); a **skeletal archer** hits Lachma, then just misses him.
  - Bishop, using Khortum's distraction, **sneak attacks the mummy "with a furious attack."**
  - Di and Khortum attack furiously — the mummy staggers backward.
  - The skeletons swarm SCOUT; the mummy "looks to the sky to summon magical healing energy from the sarcophagus."
  - Bishop repositions behind the sarcophagus — and **looks in: a blackened object — a mummified hand, glowing with magic.** He shoots an arrow, striking the mummy in the back.
  - Di's "whirlwind of hits" — the mummy falls. It keeps falling. Khortum smashes a skeleton into dust.
  - The sarcophagus's green light strikes the ceiling; the mummy rises again, mocking: "**There's no way you mortals can defeat me!**"
  - Lachma rushes to SCOUT; SCOUT **thunderwaves** the two skeletons away (per the notes' phrasing). The skeletons return and hit Lachma hard.
  - **Bishop jumps into the sarcophagus and stabs the blackened hand.** Sharp sounds erupt; **the alabaster walls rupture with giant lightning cracks; platter-sized holes**; the mummy **roars and collapses**; the skeletons collapse; **sand pours out of the holes like water**, filling the room.
- **The escape:**
  - Di runs for the **golden doors** on the opposite side; Khortum for the entrance; SCOUT and Lachma east.
  - The braziers are obscured by rushing sand; the room darkens.
  - Bishop, "cool as a cucumber," strides to the locked door (mechanism unclear) and, with "a very good performance," **slides the latching mechanism** — the door bumps forward.
  - Di pushes; he and Bishop rush the **portcullis — which is holding firm.**
  - Khortum helps SCOUT up and over the statue; the party reunites — "having saved themselves from a giant sand trap!"
  - They return to the trapdoor room "to take a well-deserved breather."
