# 🐫 Pyramid of Tiberesh

> "The burial place of the prophet and self-proclaimed god-king [[zazi/npcs/tiberesh|Tiberesh]." First entered by the [[campaigns/team-c/README|Team C]] party (Sessions 2–8).

## Outside
- **50 feet tall, 100 feet wide**, surrounded by a **10-foot high wall**; limestone blocks.
- A **15-foot-wide causeway** of limestone, rutted deep by heavy caravans, leads from the city side to a large archway across the **River Nuria**.
- Along the causeway: low walls with **alabaster reliefs of a fish-headed god** ("They're pescatarians"); **columns depicting a setting sun**; alcoves at the causeway's end showing **naked men sacrificing themselves** — "a hallmark of the god Tiberesh" (Di's recognition, Session 2).
- Bishop's insight, drawn in the sand: **the strength of the river ↔ the strength of Tiberesh** (Session 2).

## The interior (as mapped by the party)
1. **Twin golden doors** — the entrance; the fish-headed god faces across, left hand a dagger, right hand a curved rod. Bishop alone opens them (Sessions 2–3).
2. **Antechamber (55 ft wide)** — three gnoll corpses in dried blood (relatively fresh), 13 arrows, 15 silver, a small **bronze cat with gem eyes** in a pocket; blood trail to the northeast.
3. **Sarcophagus hall** — long, undecorated, single-file; urns, a **bronze chest** (red velvet, feline face embossed — "related to an ancient god or religion," per Khortum), and **four stone sarcophagi mounted on the ceiling** with limestone name plaques: **Xerx, Phi, Tosanath, Baelxi** (language initially unclear). Southern wooden door — the gnoll garrison bursts through it (Sessions 2–4).
   - Sarcophagi outcomes: one erupts with a **beetle swarm** (semi-organic hive, attackable, "each one probably has its own political opinions"); a second swarm under the NE lid; **Tosanath's** holds **ancient coins**; **Phi** is empty ("No beetles. No Beatles. No treasure."); Xerx's fate not recorded.
4. **Canopic storage room** — lower ceiling, piled with canopic jars and wooden crates; **11 decorative bronze boxes** (matching the coins' style) holding **20 vials of evaporated mystery liquid** (≈5 gp conservatively, more to the right buyer). "The ancestors have not been well respected." A dead-end room; Lachma is left here "in the security of a dead-end room" (Session 4).
5. **The Pride Room** — 50 ft long, up to 100 ft wide; 7 magical torches (Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Indigo, Violet), each a different color; a shallow pool on the north wall making a kaleidoscope; three bronze lion statues around the pool; carvings of **falcon-headed men slaying lions** (an **illusion** — Arcane Insight reveals the true subjects are [[zazi/creatures/nkosi|Nkosi]] "in status, weapons at the ready"); a 10-foot table of multicolor scarabs; a rusted-iron-locked metallic east door; a sarcophagus to the west (Session 4).
   - **Scarab wall** (south): seven carved scarabs — Left: Yellow, Blue, Red · Center: Violet · Right: Green, Purple, Orange.
6. **De-organing room** — 50 ft across, two granite slabs and a pillar; canopic jars with iron instruments; bloodstains; "used to prepare humanoids for mummification" (Lachma's medicine). A **painted golden door** (lapis-lazuli scarabs + jade serpent mosaic — Bishop slides most of it out, ≈40 gp at auction) with a heavy brass lock. Under a table lip: the **[[zazi/items/lever-of-the-dead|Lever of the Dead]]** (Anubis-faced). Pulled once: the granite block over Di's trapped leg *resets*; pulled twice: it drops (the "worst slot machine") (Session 5).
7. **Basin room** — 40×50 ft; a 20-foot circular basin (shallow, "like Paris Hilton"), two rusted green-stained protrusions on the east wall (venom-drawing / religious, per Di), pockmarked floor, a tattered **zebra rug** over a **trapdoor** with a large urn (Session 5).
8. **Below the trapdoor** — a 20-ft ladder down to a hall flanked by two statues before golden doors with riddle plaques:
   - North: *"I sustain and guide all who live, while caged in a hidden, white prison."* → the **heart** (answered with a sword to the chest).
   - South: *"I am a twinkling beacon sheathed in stone; we all are drawn to doom."* → a **ceremonial dagger** (Session 6–7).
   - The **Nkosi ambush**: touching the orange scarab (with two pool-dipped fingers) wakes the six cat-folk from stasis; the party fights them in the basin room, then lights all seven scarabs (Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Violet, Purple sequence completed by Bishop) and the stone tablet moves aside (Session 6).
9. **Gypsosphinx hall** — a 60-ft corridor with an immense [[zazi/creatures/gypsosphinx|gypsosphinx]] statue (feline body, human head, wings) moving **10 ft/round** on a trigger; a **portcullis** closes behind the party; the party climbs over (SCOUT injures himself; Lachma slips when Bimbubop falls out of his pocket) (Session 7).
10. **Onyx anglerfish doors** — gigantic, turquoise design; the anglerfish's nostrils are the pull-rings; "the fish wrote it went to Yale." Two shelves of turquoise runestones (lion, scarab, cat, snake) (Session 7).
11. **Tiberesh's tomb** — 80 ft, turning south; sand drifts, two large braziers, translucent alabaster wall panels; a platform, steps, and table at center; a rectangular **alabaster sarcophagus**. The doorway erupts into flame, the room begins filling with sand, the lid slides off, and the [[zazi/npcs/tiberesh|mummy]] rises: *"I am reborn! I will turn this world upside-down on your behalf!"*
    - The fight: skeletal warriors and a skeletal archer rise from the sands; the mummy draws healing light from the sarcophagus; Bishop spots **a blackened, glowing mummified hand** inside the sarcophagus and **stabs it** — the alabaster walls crack "with giant lightning cracks," the mummy roars and collapses, and **sand pours in like water** through platter-sized holes (Session 7).
    - The escape: Di and Bishop force the locked **golden doors** at the far side; the portcullis holds; the party reunites over the statue and regroups in the basin room (Session 7).
12. **The exit → the stars** — after a rest, the pyramid shifts; the path is replaced by a **spacescape** and a "shimmering glass" entrance opening onto a swirling foreign sky, the constellation of **Enoreth** among them. Two arms reach through (one black humanoid, one smaller giving the "ok" symbol). The party steps into the **Chamber of Stars** (see [[zazi/worldbuilding/zazi#the-chamber-of-stars|world overview]]) (Session 8).
