## Beats
- **The gnoll garrison fight** (continuation of the Session 2 erupting door, in the sarcophagus hall):
  - [[campaigns/team-c/characters/bishop|Bishop]] "takes decisive action" — a shortbow arrow at the **boss gnoll** "striking with outrageous force," then ducks.
  - [[campaigns/team-c/characters/lachma|Lachma]] "freaks out and waddles behind cover"; **poison spray to no effect** — "that gnoll shoots him a glare! He is seen."
  - A gnoll bites [[campaigns/team-c/characters/khortum-fil|Khortum]]'s arm — **4 damage**.
  - [[campaigns/team-c/characters/di-guan|Di Guan]] steps up "flashing his prowess with his spear"; the gnoll laughs off the attacks.
  - **[[campaigns/team-c/characters/scout|SCOUT]]** "computes, calculates, connives, and conjures a longbow" — the boss gnoll, "square in the chest."
  - The boss wields a **glaive** and charges Di (solid blow) and Khortum (shield interposed, "gets no credit for saving the bacon").
  - Two gnolls "join Glenn Frey on stage to keep beating the crap out of Khortum's AC 19."
  - Khortum casts **Shield of Faith** on himself; the **Hammer of Justice** smashes the boss — but leaves himself open.
  - Bishop's sneak attack: "twists the knife into a gnoll and rolls for PAIN."
  - Lachma: **poison mist** — "super effective"; the gnoll "needs medical attention... a referral from his primary care veterinarian."
  - A gnoll in front of Khortum **misses critically**, entangled, weapon stuck in the ground. Di's answer: **the butt** — "Di's butt is in the gnoll leader's face, and yells 'for the people!'"
  - "The halberd spins with an action surge, Di is chopping dog people." SCOUT: "I FOUND MY TARGET" — the "flea-bitten hyena" is removed from combat.
  - The last gnoll "doesn't speak Common or Ur-Common... the only other language it knows: violence."
  - Bishop's **psychic sliver** into a gnoll's brain — "2 psychic damage, too dumb to die."
  - Lachma's tide-change: blocks the doorway, **Infestation** — "a cloud of bugs and vermin... ew it's gross."
  - Bishop: "real blades, makes with the stabbity" — the knife "inserted into the gnoll's behind, a proctologist appointment that won't be forgotten any time soon"; a right-torso specialist visit; the gnoll's back is "arched." ("Is there a chiropractor in the house?")
  - Lachma, two-handed staff, invoking **Bimbubop** — the **Tiny Blessing** received "in the gnoll's noggin."
  - House ruling: Khortum swings slower; Di gets his skipped turn back — **second wind** and he "**Di-capitates**" the last gnoll.
- **Aftermath:** "There is mystery treasure." Post-fight healing. **Bimbubop's nine goodberry poops** are eaten by Khortum; a tenth "dingleberry is constipated for a later time."
