# 🪓 Sir

> A half-orc barbarian who answers to "Sir" — "Sir's not a sir, but a half-orc girl!" (Donnie's song, Session 3). Eagle totem spirit (Session 16).

## Who
- **Backstory (from the notes):** left on the doorstep of a hut in a small village; brusque even by peasant standards; outgrew the village; wandered the land hunting and taking odd jobs for just enough coin — "It wasn't until she entered a lowly tavern one day that her life of adventure began."
- Her weapon is a **battleaxe** (and she's the one who "squeezes kobolds like dishrags"); her recovered **painted wolf's-head shield** is from the kobold air tower.

## In play
- **S1–S2:** assembles the party at the Mountain's Respite in [[mountains/locations/stonehaven|Stonehaven]]; swarmed by starving rats at the [[mountains/locations/blackrock-peak|watchtower gate]]; breaks out of the web.
- **S3–S5:** "the hunter's attunement" appears; the high-five hunt; pins the manticore's wing after dropping [[campaigns/team-b/characters/thora|Thora]] and stabilizing her — "her blood on her hands"; drops the manticore; smashes the giant spider with Thora.
- **S8:** bites the leaf ring — it slides onto her pinky, and she is "really into her new ring."
- **S12–S14:** follows Thora's disappearance into "reckless pursuit"; squeezes a kobold "like a dishrag"; slides down the greased third floor; takes 7 damage in the wind cyclone (Orenstein is caught by Sir); helps the party aloft on the carpet.
- **S16–S20:** joins the party at [[mountains/locations/vryrrk-basta-caverns|Vryrrk Basta]]; leads into the [[mountains/locations/yin-yang-tomb|yin-yang tomb]]; killed a fish-person and "realized she has in fact killed a part of herself" — the bearded face's "great wisdom."
