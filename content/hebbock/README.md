# 🌍 Hebbock

> The world. Everything here is **world canon** — persistent, campaign-independent truth: places, powers, people of the realm, things, beasts, and the deep lore.
>
> If a campaign *changes* the world (a city falls, a king dies, a faction rises), the change is recorded here, dated, and linked to the session/arc that caused it.

## Sections
| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `locations/` | Regions, cities, dungeons, landmarks — hierarchical, parent→child linked |
| `factions/` | Organizations that span the world: guilds, temples, houses, powers, cults |
| `items/` | World-famous items, artifacts, relics (campaign-specific loot lives in the campaign) |
| `creatures/` | Beasts and monsters of the realm, unique or recurring |
| `npcs/` | World NPCs — figures who exist regardless of which campaign they appear in |
| `worldbuilding/` | History, gods, magic, cosmology, politics, the entry page for Hebbock itself |

## Conventions
- One page per entity, `kebab-case` slug filename, full frontmatter (see [[01-Guidelines|guidelines]]).
- Cross-link aggressively — no orphans, no dangling links.
- Every world entity gets a row in [[_names|_names.md]].
- Pages are `draft` until aedoran confirms, then `canonical`.
- If a world entity only matters to one campaign, still file it here — it's part of the world — and tag it with that campaign.

_See [[campaigns/README|Campaigns]] for the per-campaign records._ 🎶
