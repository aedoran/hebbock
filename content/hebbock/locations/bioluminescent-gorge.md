# The Bioluminescent Gorge

> A vast gorge, like the inside of a hollow mountain — the first true wonder the party sees in the deep, and the last stretch before the cathedral.

## Geography
A hollow-mountain chasm. A cloud-ceiling of mist hangs above, and where each dripping drop strikes stone it bursts into a small blue biological flash, trickling down slopes strewn with patches of bioluminescent fungus — a galaxy of little lights descending into the dark. The [[hebbock/locations/cathedral-of-dumathoin|Cathedral of Dumathoin]] is built into the living rock at the very bottom, flanked by two giant carved dwarves, its door dimly lit by braziers.

## Notable Features
- **The thin northern ledge** — a treacherous line the party climbs single file, past pools where impossible mushrooms grow heads out of heads, and over glowing water where orange lightning-bugs drift.
- **The traverse** — a ten-foot gap of sheer wall, eighty feet above the gorge floor, crossed by the dwarves with rope.
- **The Silt Pool** — a firelit pool on the gorge floor, the goblins' mushroom camp (see [[hebbock/locations/silt-pool|Silt Pool]]).

## Inhabitants & Powers
- Goblin mushroom harvesters at the [[hebbock/locations/silt-pool|Silt Pool]] — led by [[hebbock/npcs/nyx|Nyx]], with the shaman [[hebbock/npcs/gri-the-asht-chewer|Gri the Asht-Chewer]].
- Kuo-toa hidden in the gorge's alcoves (see [[hebbock/npcs/glul|Glul]], [[hebbock/npcs/lib|Lib]], [[hebbock/npcs/ulu|Ulu]]).

## Current State
**Contested.** The goblins and kuo-toa are wary of the party; the drug trade ([[hebbock/items/brain-cane|brain cane]]) runs off the pool.
