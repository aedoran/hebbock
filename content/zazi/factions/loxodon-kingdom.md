# 🐘 Loxodon Kingdom

> A growing **island kingdom** — "elephant people" — far to the (unspecified) horizon; home of [[campaigns/team-c/characters/khortum-fil|Khortum Fil]] and the god they call the [[zazi/worldbuilding/lost-one|Lost One]].

## As known
- In the **past era** (Session 9, from young Hazi): an island kingdom **growing in power**, with **strange rumors of a divine presence in the material world** — a god or godlike being physically manifesting among them. Hazi raises his eyebrows at the word "gods": unusual, controversial, significant.
- There is also a **Loxodon herd in [[zazi/locations/zazi|Zazi]] itself** in the past — the one Khortum follows, "almost with no accord of his own," to the [[zazi/worldbuilding/lost-one#in-play|Lost One's temple]] (Session 9.5).
- In the **present era**, Khortum is *shocked* to see two Loxodons in the city — they are rare or gone (Session 8). The "lonely island" of his home is implied in Session 9.5.

## The Lost One
The kingdom's god is alive and worshipped in the past; dead or absent in the present. Khortum carries "a fragment of his lost god's soul." Per the DM ruling in the notes, the god "spread out the soul before" — so Khortum's time-displacement is *not* the cause of the god's faltering (Session 8).
