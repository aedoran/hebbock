# Vault of Dumathoin

> Hoarder-monks of the Underdark — xenophobic but not greedy, who see the gathering of treasures into the vault as a **benevolent liberation**. The order that hired the party to bring home its lost holy man, [[hebbock/npcs/cororic|Cororic]].

## Purpose & Goals
- Preserve and gather the treasures of the world into the vault — understood by the order as a *liberation* of the treasures from the chaos of the surface.
- Recover lost members. Cororic vanished into the deep a generation ago; something now calls the townsfolk in his voice, and the order will mount an expedition to bring him home, kindly.

## Structure & Ranks
- Uses the "Brother / Sister" forms for its clerics. The eye is sacred to the order; to deface one is a grave transgression.

## Headquarters
- [[hebbock/locations/cathedral-of-dumathoin|Cathedral of Dumathoin]] — the seat of the order, at the floor of the [[hebbock/locations/bioluminescent-gorge|Bioluminescent Gorge]].

## History
- A generation ago, [[hebbock/npcs/cororic|Cororic]] vanished into the deep below [[hebbock/locations/hargate|Hargate]].
- The cathedral has since been **defiled** — new eyes carved over the statues' eyes — and filled with a mass grave of two hundred dead.

## Involvement in Campaigns
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/arcs/the-counting-dark|The Counting Dark]] — the order is the arc's sponsor; its clerics form the party's moral compass, and its lost saint has become the arc's most dangerous smile.

> **Naming note (resolved):** "the Vault of Dumathoin" is the name of the **order** (aedoran's ruling). The building is the [[hebbock/locations/cathedral-of-dumathoin|Cathedral of Dumathoin]] — the order's seat.
