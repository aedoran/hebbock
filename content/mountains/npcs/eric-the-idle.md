# 🎸 Eric the Idle

> The singing stranger the party camps beside among the pines at the [[mountains/locations/kobold-air-tower|kobold air tower]] (Session 12, 1/25/2025). "Eric the Idle is the man's name and he would like some provisions."

## Who
- A wandering singer with a bag of curious treasures: **4 flasks of pink bubbly liquid, a dragon statuette, and a monkey wearing a helmet**.
- He exchanges all three for **half the party's rations** — and declines to go with the group into the tower.
- His [[campaigns/team-b/items/dragon-statuette|dragon statuette]] is the object that makes [[campaigns/team-b/characters/thora|Thora]] vanish in a flash of blue-green flame.

## In play
- Eric's statuette is the campaign's missing-link: it is the last connection to Thora, the payment to [[mountains/npcs/thrallax|Thrallax]], and the reason the party owes a dragon a debt. Where Eric is now is unknown. See [[_questions|Open Questions]].
