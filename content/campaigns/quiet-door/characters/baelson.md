# Baelson

> The self-styled **Avenging Night Angel of St. Cuthbert** — a grim zealot in chain mail who does not investigate so much as *pursue*, with justice at the end of a mace.

## Overview
Baelson is the party's engine and its greatest risk. Forged from the armored excess of nineties comic books, he came to [[hebbock/locations/hargate|Hargate]] hunting an imagined criminal underground and found a real one — of a different kind. His answer to nearly every question is justice: loudly, and with a mace.

> "My greatest nightmare is a world of injustice." — Baelson, without a trace of irony.

## Personality
- Pursues rather than investigates; intimidation is his first language.
- Capable of sudden, accidental empathy: in Session IV he began to *vibe* with the despair of [[hebbock/npcs/cororic|Cororic]], the man who sought and never found.
- When doubt entered him in the cathedral (charmed by Cororic's "Fellow paladin, there is no need for this violence"), he responded not with violence but with a tragic reading: "I understand why you abandoned the Dumathoin and gave way to this heresy."
