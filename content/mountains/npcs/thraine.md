# 🍺 Thraine

> A **dwarf** who runs the **Mountain's Respite** inn in [[mountains/locations/stonehaven|Stonehaven]] (Session 1, 9/1/2024). The door is "stained and weathered, oak, solidly constructed for dwarfs."

## What he does
- Hosts the party on the campaign's first night.
- Tells Orenstein, candidly, that [[mountains/npcs/gilda|Gilda]] "is basically a drunk."

## Notes
- The notes spell his name **Thraine** (once also "Thrain") — one entry, one person.

## In play
- The Mountain's Respite is the party's home base in the only town they know.
