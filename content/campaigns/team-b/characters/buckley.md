# 🎼 Buckley

> A **blue dragonborn** whose light is **blue-green** — the mirror of [[campaigns/team-b/characters/thora|Thora]]'s green-blue. Found among the wildflowers next to the [[mountains/locations/kobold-air-tower|kobold air tower]] (Session 13, 2/1/2025).

## Who
- **"Bardstock, man."** His session-16 character work quotes The Dude; he bards, and he **charms the tower's kobolds with a song of draconic things** — until they see Boomerstein and go into a raging frenzy.
- A floor in the tower is, by context, **his** — murals of monks harnessing the wind (S13).
- Background figures from the notes: **Jeff / Scott Jeffkiller** (kept as written, unexplained).

## In play
- **S13:** joins the party (the "wildflowers" session); slides the greased third floor; takes 7 damage in the wind cyclone; helps the party aloft on the carpet.
- **S15–S16:** survives the carpet flight; performs the draconic song that wins the party an audience with [[mountains/npcs/argin-noroxious|Argin Noroxious]] and the five dragonborn at [[mountains/locations/vryrrk-basta-caverns|Vryrrk Basta Caverns]].
- **S18–S20:** leads the party deeper into the [[mountains/locations/yin-yang-tomb|yin-yang tomb]]; Thunderwave-blinds the fish-person's room; finds the **bearded face** on the wall: "I am upon him!"
