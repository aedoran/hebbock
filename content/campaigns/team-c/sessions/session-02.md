# 📓 Session 2 — On the Precipice of Adventure

**Date (as written):** 6/22/25 · **Previous:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-01|Session 1]] · **Next:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-03|Session 3]]

## Beats
- **The assignment:** provide a map; the GFA(C) requires their share, in part or entirely, of what the party retrieves from the pyramid.
- **Approach to the [[zazi/locations/pyramid-of-tiberesh|Pyramid of Tiberesh]]** (first full description):
  - 50 ft tall, 100 ft wide, 10-ft high wall, limestone blocks.
  - A **15-ft-wide causeway** to a large archway; **deep ruts** worn by caravans bearing heavy weights.
  - Low walls with **alabaster reliefs of a fish-headed god** — "They're pescatarians."
  - **Columns depicting a setting sun.**
  - **Alcoves at the causeway's end: naked men sacrificing themselves** — Di recognizes them "as a hallmark of the god Tiberesh."
- **[[campaigns/team-c/characters/bishop|Bishop]]'s insight**, drawn in the sand: **a connection between the strength of the river (Nuria) and the strength of Tiberesh.** Most of the party figures it out.
- **The twin golden doors** — pull-rings; the fish-headed god faces the opposite door, **left hand a dagger, right hand a curved rod**. Di and Khortum fail to open them; **Bishop's technique is "most impressive."**
- **Antechamber** (55 ft wide):
  - **3 gnolls in puddles of dried blood**, tools strewn about; bodies **relatively fresh**; armor broken.
  - **13 arrows, 15 silver**; in a pocket: **a small bronze cat with gems set for eyes.**
  - A **blood trail leads northeast.**
- **The northeast hall:** an archway on the right; "references to gnoll guards with weapons extending to each other's shoulders" (wall art?); narrow, single-file.
- The archway opens to a long, undecorated room: **urns, a bronze chest, and four stone sarcophagi mounted on the ceiling**, each with a limestone name plaque — **Xerx, Phi, Tosanath, Baelxi** ("the language is unclear at first").
- **A wooden door on the southern wall** — from behind it: a "whiny, gutteral noise." **Four gnolls erupt from the door!** An army, armed, "have arms, but no armistice." One gnoll is larger and armored.
- A quick one runs at Khortum — **Di strikes with the halberd**; the gnoll is deftly struck and **wildly howls**.
- "Bishop and Lachma ready their rebuttals as we are just hanging on to the cliff!"

## Loot
- 13 arrows, 15 silver, bronze cat with gem eyes.

## Threads opened
- Who are the four named dead in the ceiling sarcophagi (Xerx, Phi, Tosanath, Baelxi)?
- The river ↔ Tiberesh connection — what does it *mean* mechanically or cosmically?
- The fish-headed god (unnamed; see [[zazi/worldbuilding/pantheon|the gods]]).
- The fresh gnoll corpses — a previous expedition? A garrison?
- The bronze cat — Khortum's "ancient god or religion" hunch (carried into Session 4).
