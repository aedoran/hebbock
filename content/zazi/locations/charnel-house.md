# 🕯️ The Charnel House

> "The house of the dead" on **Bashtoor Street** in the [[zazi/locations/district-of-the-hyena|District of the Hyena]], run by [[zazi/npcs/sultan-shukranil|Sultan Shukranil Mo Shator]] — who *eats* the corpses he is paid to incinerate.

## The building (Bishop's casing, Session 10)
- **15 feet tall**, **bell-shaped** sandstone — "kind of like a handbell."
- **No windows**; a single entrance (probably an iron door); **no backdoor**; **no guards outside**; smoke from an opening at the top.
- Hours: **dawn to dusk**.
- Nearby: the **"great sand pyramid"** — *not* the [[zazi/locations/pyramid-of-tiberesh|Pyramid of Tiberesh]] — whose it is and what lies inside are **mysteries by design** (ruled 2026-08-21) — and **Jackal's Run Bridge** at the pyramid's southeastern tip (the dawn rendezvous point with [[zazi/npcs/karima|Karima]]).

## Inside
- **Ground floor:** a small platform; a stairway winding down to the east; a **blazing charcoal pit**; a trio of wax-encrusted chandeliers.
- **Lower mortuary:** a wide firepit with timber and a low wall; **human-sized iron carts** used in the firepits. A door with a stained-glass panel; **four alcoves on the north side**, a body each.
- **Behind the stained-glass door** (opened with the Sultan's small key, Session 11): a **bed chamber**, a **small library**, and a **shrine to death magic** with Anubis iconography — plus a collection room (a "museum") where a mummy holds a **hooked blade with gold trim**.
- **The library:** dark cookbooks (including *"To Serve Man"*), illustrated treaties on embalming, a **spellbook of 18 spells** (1 hour of study per spell to decipher), a **gold-trimmed map of the Southlands**, and a book of poetry in an **alligator-hide cover** with gold and obsidian buckle (alligators may be common in the city sewers — Lachma's deduction).

## The job (Sessions 10–12)
[[zazi/npcs/karima|Karima]] hires the party (500 gold, gratitude, "maybe a passionate night") to retrieve her scholar friend **[[zazi/npcs/tellya-hakkam|Tellya Hakkam]]**'s body and belongings. The plan: Lachma-as-donkey chaos, Bishop-as-corpse, Di's commotion, Khortum calming the ass with the secret word **"serenity,"** SCOUT on perimeter. The plan goes gloriously wrong; combat; the Sultan dies in the firepit (Thunderwave off a chandelier, "like a hot dog on a grill"); the party burns the gnolls, finds **two keys** (large and small), and finds the body — marked "S," brown hair, white robe, red scarf, mouth full of sand, sooty *small* handprints on the satchel, and an erection with a money belt: **30 platinum, 3 jet stones, a scroll with a cat symbol, and a parchment fragment** (the Bastet rhyme — "Nineteen karams back he should crawl… cast a karam of rope into the river with a prayer…").

The other two bodies are putrified, marked "R." A white sheet marked "R" reeks of rotting flesh.

## The Anubis creature
Under a rug in the death shrine, an **uncanny jackaloid** bursts out — "an avatar of Anubis" per Khortum. It dissolves into sand and reconstitutes; Khortum's Shield of Faith answers each blow with "the image of a ramming elephant made of holy light." It wins (Session 11–12): a sand whirlwind restrains Khortum, Di poses over the monster "as a delta male," and the party — beaten — locks up and carries the body out on Lachma's ass.

## Threads
- **SCOUT is abducted** around the same time as the **green-smoke woman** (low-cut dress, green necklace; came to collect "the corpse of her uncle"; argued with the Sultan and vanished). The party suspects a connection.
- The **R/S markings** and the small handprints are unexplained.
- The **Bastet rhyme** is unsolved — a set of instructions involving crawling, rope, stakes, shadows, and "secrets below… his to be caught."
- The **parrot** flew out of the charnel house at the start of the fight (Session 10) — Bishop's parrot? A new one? Unresolved.
