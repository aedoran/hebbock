# 📓 Session 9 — Timing is Everything

**Date (as written):** 2/1/26 · **Previous:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-08|Session 8]] · **Next:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-09-5|Session 9.5]]

## Beats
- **The past-era morning at the [[zazi/locations/nuzul-inn|Nuzul Inn]]:** cat-people, knolls, and more of [[campaigns/team-c/characters/khortum-fil|Khortum]]'s people walk the streets; **SCOUT is getting weird looks.** "Everybody is stunned by the strange change." **Khortum and Scout have wandered off.**
  - The inn: same building, **looks newer; a crescent on the sign**; "more multicultural now"; different fashion and designs.
  - **Bishop knows this is from an earlier time.**
  - Sandstone's order (recounted): "go see Hazi and give him the map" — but **SCOUT has the map.**
- **The party splits:**
  - **Lachma, Bimbubop, Bishop, and Di** enter the inn — a tavern "more chaotic and diverse than previously remembered," with Nkosi present (a race "either non-existent or extremely rare before"); patrons give curious, non-hostile stares.
  - **Di:** "I look for a good table" — wants someone with arcane. Di and Lachma hunt for scholarly people.
  - **Bishop** scouts, listens, pickpockets: he spots an **unattended backpack** on a chair — "Is this how villains get started?" — and lifts **5 copper and silver.**
  - Di: "**Is Fatima here?**" — "the woman who he flirted with in the beginning."
- **Young Hazi:** in the **back-left corner** sits a lone human male, **long brown hair, robes**, studying **maps spread over a large table**, flask and a pint nearby.
  - Lachma **recognizes Hazi** — "this version... much younger and has long brown hair, whereas the Hazi that Lachma previously met was bald. Lachma realizes this instantly" — and **spills his drink, staying silent.**
  - Hazi introduces himself, invites Di and Lachma to his table; asks them to leave the tea; rolls up his maps to make space.
  - Bishop sits down too; Hazi notices, asks if he's "someone I should know" or "with Di"; Di confirms Bishop was also present "last time" — Hazi finds it "a bit weird" but lets it go.
  - Lachma's explanation that "this is Hazi" confuses him: "**I don't know who these people are.**"
  - **Bimbubop asks Hazi what he's looking for in his maps** — Hazi explains **his motivation: building the antiquities company.** Bishop judges "Hazi is being sincere."
  - Hazi doesn't believe the party's claims about knowing each other — but offers an opportunity: he introduces **[[zazi/npcs/karima|Karima]]**, "a Gnoll and good friend of his," who lives in the next quarter and **needs a task completed she can't do herself "for complicated reasons."** Tell her Hazi sent them.
  - Di wants "a book, to learn spells"; Lachma interrupts — **they have something Hazi wants: the map from the future** ("your future version wants").
  - Hazi: "I'll meet you tomorrow, for dinner, for the map" — but **Karima needs help more urgently.** He leaves quickly.
- **Bishop's telepathy demo (the party learns he can talk in their heads):**
  - He "projects an image of his face, pulls down his scarf to reveal his mouth (and his 'very luscious lips')" and communicates "I can talk this way."
  - He contacts **SCOUT telepathically** (SCOUT doesn't know Bishop can): the party has traveled **~20 years into the past**; Hazi doesn't recognize them; he wants the map SCOUT has.
  - SCOUT's reply: confused but believes Bishop; is **following/pursuing Khortum and his people**; there are **no other warforged — "he's the only one here"** (significant); he's **wrapped in a cloak to stay hidden**; will return to the tavern in about an hour.
  - Bishop also telepathically contacts Di — **Di sprays the tea from his mouth.** They figure out how to talk "only in each others' heads."
  - Di: "**I feel different too, I am magic man.**"
  - Bishop throws a **psychic knife at an unattended glass** — no effect: "the knife only affects creatures with a psyche/mind... 'My mind stabs their mind.'"
- **SCOUT returns** (cloaked, inconspicuous): apologizes for leaving; was tracking Khortum (who "wandered off following 'his people'"); confirms **he has the map**; reiterates he is the only warforged seen in this time period.
  - Puts the map on the table. **Di grabs it.** "I don't want to lose Khortum, I have to go... lets stay in contact."
- **Dinner with Hazi — the map handover:**
  - Di presents the map; produces **ink and a brush/writing utensil** (per the negotiation). Hazi's tell: **left hand gives the writing supplies to Di; right hand takes the map.**
  - "We inform Hazi, Sandstone and Zing wanted to give you [it]."
- **Hazi's world briefing (the "news junkie, only NewsMax"):**
  - A war in the **northeastern part of the continent**; **new mechanical beings** — "magically created, self-aware constructs 'forged for war' (warforged)."
  - To the party: **"You know one of these creatures that have been forged for the war?"** — he notices/suspects something about SCOUT. Di deflects: "This is not my native tongue..."
  - **Northwestern continent:** war raging; **raining magical fire**; charred landscapes; warforged "**rebell[ing] against their magical masters**."
  - **[[zazi/factions/vontir-empire|Vontir Empire]] (west):** minor rebellions in the western outposts; **Zazi is semi-autonomous, loosely aligned.**
  - **Hazi's personal goals:** collecting maps to **start a treasure-hunting business**; the chaos = opportunity.
  - **[[zazi/factions/loxodon-kingdom|Loxodon rising kingdom]]:** a growing island kingdom; **strange rumors of a divine presence in the material world** — Hazi "raises his eyebrows" at "gods" (unusual, controversial, significant).
  - **Bimbubop asks who is fighting the warforged:** Hazi doesn't know the details — **powerful wizards serve as generals and leaders**; reasons unclear (ancient hatreds, vendettas, pride); information scarce this far south.
- **Di's genealogical lore:** ~450–500 years ago, Di's people repelled the Vontir's "soft encroachment"; never conquered; a great-ancestor hero of the village defense — **"the ancestor's name is to be determined later."**
- **Lachma goes off freaking out** ("this is not possible"); Di, Bishop, Hazi continue talking.
  - Hazi shows Di "the process of how to deal with magic."
  - Bishop keeps talking through details to SCOUT.
  - SCOUT, told warforged have **no free will**, worries he "might leave the group," "might be in danger"; Bishop: "I lived like this for a while and think you can do this." SCOUT will cover up, not draw attention.
  - Bishop: "not disturbed by the age" — but "confused that he feels he can trust him now, but couldn't trust the old Hazi."
- **The night:** they stay at the inn.
  - **Di spends all night writing, "summoning familiar," and falls asleep** (the Peng setup).
  - **Lachma tries to attune** — to learn if they've "shifted into another world, or just moved through time."
  - **Bishop gets up early, stretches, thieves and overhears conversations.**
