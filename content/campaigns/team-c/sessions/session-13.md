# 📓 Session 13 — Evil Woman

**Date (as written):** 3/23/26 · **Previous:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-12|Session 12]] · **Next:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-14|Session 14]]

## Beats
- **The long rest:** while waiting on Hazi's network, the party rests at the [[zazi/locations/nuzul-inn|Nuzul Inn]] — "moderately busy"; Hazi is out; "between a moderate and comfortable inn, it is ethically sourced."
  - Di studies the spellbook. Lachma "is eating like a freak." Khortum mingles. Bishop eyes pickpocketing and eavesdropping targets.
  - After the rest (~11 PM): **no word from Hazi or his contacts.** The inn is quiet "save for a human barfly." Bishop heads out to eavesdrop.
- **The cloaked watchers:**
  - "The smells of tobacco and coffee have faded, giving way to cool desert air."
  - A few blocks **northwest of the inn** — "in the same direction as the charnel house in the gnoll sector."
  - Bishop, peaking around a corner, sees **four or five figures** — "unclear if Bishop cannot count or if they are obscured." **Humanoids in brown cloaks, faces covered.** **Two have warforged ankles** — "the glint of the street lamps illuminate the metal."
  - "Their voices sound like they emerge from soup cans. Hushed tones: '**They're close. They're staying at the inn. We must follow through with our orders.**'"
  - Their footsteps fade. Bishop returns "post-haste!"
- **The alarm:**
  - Bishop forges a connection with Lachma and Khortum: "People are coming, who are they? Two of them are warforged. That's twice as many as we've ever seen before!"
  - Lachma wakes Di — armor on, halberd summoned. Khortum keeps watch.
  - "We are uncertain of their orders, so we hesitate to attack them directly on sight."
  - Bishop goes back out to follow the clandestine group (the skulker). He sees a **drunken group making dirty jokes about elephant coupling.**
- **The ambush:**
  - Bishop is **startled by four humanoid creatures surrounding him — one attacks!** Initiative!
  - Bishop exclaims **mentally** (so Khortum and Lachma know he's in danger).
  - **The warforged attacks Bishop — "the expression menacing, maniacal, mechanical. In sharp contrast to SCOUT's countenance. It causes a fright."**
  - "It slices with a longsword across Bishop's chest. It mutters something unintelligible after its strike lands."
  - **Bishop disengages:** "a section of houses more like apartment buildings"; scrambles up the north-facing side; "The volume of the warforged rises as they escalate their pursuit." Two pursue; **one is the target of Bishop's mindknife — "scouring its positronic brain, electrifying its nodules"** — the warforged "loses its grip and falls."
  - Khortum rushes out of the inn — "his senses guide him definitely toward Bishop." Di and Lachma follow.
  - **Three warforged line up in pursuit** but **struggle to climb the walls.**
  - Bishop climbs out of reach; one presses "his metal fingers through the stone walls." The party skids into the alley; Bishop keeps the pursuers at bay: "**Cease your pursuit!**"
  - The warforged group charges the party — "jarring to see warforged so hostile and aggressive. They have harsher aesthetics, similar to Kraftwerk?"
  - Bishop leaps down onto his warforged and stabs — "a human woman looks out of her window and gasps as Bishop descends. The window flap hits one of Bishop's hands, but the second one drives straight home into the warforged brains. **Bishop's dismount is flawless, even the East German judge agrees.**" The warforged slides down the wall.
  - Khortum draws attacks; Di slides in behind — "I give you shock!"; Lachma's **poison mist** — "The warforged barely notice."
  - A warforged slices at Khortum — "easily parried." Di carves one approaching Lachma — "undeterred in its single-minded fury." Lachma's shield and armor carry him through.
  - Bishop uppercuts a psychic blade through a warforged — "a burst of energy out and its powers down with a flash."
  - Khortum strikes the one Di was on — "it sees stars and cartoon birds circle its head." Di hits the one that attacked Khortum; Khortum is hit.
  - Bishop jumps and stabs the least-injured — "the psychic blade shredding into its back." Khortum's warhammer swings — misses.
  - **Di's vicious swipe dislodges a warforged's head — then a critical hit!** Lachma's quarterstaff swing: "It is not his strength and he has earned only disappointment, but at least he hasn't hurt himself."
  - The warforged begin withdrawing. **"A bell begins to toll. It is the city watch!"**
  - Bishop kills a third warforged; Khortum races in pursuit and strikes the last — knocking it out and scooping it up.
  - Lachma leaps, transforms into a **constrictor snake**; the attack misses; he lands in front of the fleeing warforged. "This round, a mustering horn sounds. Time is running out."
- **The retreat:**
  - "Bishop blunders in pursuit. Khortum's attack knocks out one of the warforged and he scoops it up."
  - **Di: "To charnel house!"** — the party makes it there safely. Di has the key, unlocks the door, and they enter.

## Loot / state
- The party is back inside the [[zazi/locations/charnel-house|charnel house]] — with Tellya's body, the Sultan's keys, the spellbook, the Southlands map, and the mummy's hooked blade.
- Two downed warforged were left in the street (one scooped by Khortum — its fate not recorded; the party entered the charnel house).
- The city watch is moving; a mustering horn sounds.
