# 🗺️ The Pyramid Map

> The [[campaigns/team-c/README|Team C]] party's working map of the [[zazi/locations/pyramid-of-tiberesh|Pyramid of Tiberesh]] — drawn by [[campaigns/team-c/characters/scout|SCOUT]] under the [[zazi/factions/golden-falcon-antiquities|GFA(C)]] contract ("We are to draw maps"), and the **most valuable object the party owns**.

## In play
- **Drawn during the pyramid run (Sessions 2–8):** the antechamber, the sarcophagus hall (with the bronze chest's location marked — Session 4), the canopic room, the Pride Room, the de-organing room, the basin room, the trapdoor level with the riddle doors, the gypsosphinx hall, the anglerfish doors, Tiberesh's tomb.
- **The contract makes it the GFA(C)'s property** — which is why it is the currency of the time loop:
  - In the **present**, it is the party's work product and SCOUT's responsibility.
  - In the **past**, the party hands it to **young [[zazi/npcs/hazi-sucineri|Hazi]]** per [[zazi/npcs/sandstone|Sandstone]]'s order ("Find Hazi. Give him the map.") — Session 9, at dinner, with the writing-supplies trade.
  - "We inform Hazi, Sandstone and Zing wanted to give you [it]."
  - Hazi is "disappointed" when he cannot get it immediately in Session 12 — and the party had already given it to him in Session 9 (the notes are ambiguous whether the Session 12 "map" is the same one offered back, or a copy — open question).
- **The map is with SCOUT** in the past ("Sandstone said 'go see Hazi and give him the map', but Scout has the map" — Session 9).
- **SCOUT's abduction** (Session 12) happens with the map's future in his hands — and his cloak shred is the last trace.
