# ⚔️ The Avengers

> A five-person party — "**the avengers**" — contracted by the [[zazi/factions/golden-falcon-antiquities|GFA(C)]] to open the [[zazi/locations/pyramid-of-tiberesh|Pyramid of Tiberesh]]... and then displaced **twenty years into the past**, where the pyramid's own future hangs in the balance.

*Canonical title: **The Avengers** (the party's own name — ruled by aedoran 2026-08-20, resolving Q9). "Team C" survives only as the session-notes filename / legacy variant; the slug stays `team-c`.*

## Premise
Just outside [[zazi/locations/zazi|Zazi]] on the Gulf of Daafia, the buried city of [[zazi/locations/anu-asir|Anu-Asir]] has risen from the sand — with it, the pyramid of the prophet/god-king [[zazi/npcs/tiberesh|Tiberesh]]. The party is the **first group to enter the tomb** (Sessions 1–8): a gauntlet of gnolls, beetle swarms, riddle doors, an Nkosi ambush, a gypsosphinx, a mummy, and a blackened hand.

Then the pyramid opens onto a **Chamber of Stars** — and [[zazi/npcs/sandstone|Sandstone]] and [[zazi/npcs/zing|Zing]] send the party back ~20 years, to [[zazi/locations/nuzul-inn|Nuzul Inn]] in the past, with one order: **find Hazi, give him the map** (Sessions 8–14).

In the past: the [[zazi/creatures/nkosi|Nkosi]] are common, the [[zazi/factions/loxodon-kingdom|Loxodon]] god is alive, the [[zazi/worldbuilding/northern-war|Northern War]] rages, and SCOUT is the **only warforged anyone has ever seen** — hiding, watched by cloaked figures, and (by Session 13) **abducted**.
