# 🎲 Session 6 — The Elvish Riddle

With little examination, Orenstein's mage hand picks up a bag. Two suits of armor and four flying swords wake up. "Hi, Cotter — the cartographers could be nicer." (as written — a players' note to the cartographer/AI; kept as in-play color, not an instruction.)

## What Happened

- The party stands before the open chest presenting an Elvish riddle. Four bags. With little examination, [[campaigns/team-b/characters/orenstein-alabaster|Orenstein]]'s mage hand picks up a bag — which activates two suits of armor and four flying swords! The trap springs.
- [[campaigns/team-b/characters/sir|Sir]] advances, misses her attack, and takes a hit from a sword. [[campaigns/team-b/characters/xuanzang|Xohn]] moves up to form a front line ("he's fronting" — as written). [[campaigns/team-b/characters/thora|Thora]] misses with her "hamma" (as written).
- "Orenstein is acting like a little bitch JK." (as written) Orenstein, observing the battle: "These guys aren't going to hallucinate–they don't even have eyes!" (as written)
- Xohn attacks and is able to destroy a "s" word (as written — the word appears exactly as written in the notes).
- Thora heals Sir with "cure wounds." Orenstein tries to mage hand the bag back to whence it came; the "s" word slashes at the bag, and a flash of gold sparks through the air.
- Various PCs get hit. Sir decides… to attack! A "s" word. "Go on, I'll be right behind you!" Xohn falls — "Makes his first save." (as written)
- The party withdraws, closing the door, leaving Xohn to the fates "for now…"
- Sir flanks the sword with Orenstein while Thora heals herself. Sir charges the sword with a reckless attack, smashing a large chunk out of the blade.
- Xohn dreams of his childhood. "Happy thoughts.13! Life renewed!" (as written)
- The swords are defeated, but the suits of armor prove too tough. With no spell slots and few hit points remaining, the exhausted heroes must retreat.
- "What will become of Xohn? (He's fine. He's *FINE*–if you know what I mean 'I'm fine.' — my girlfriend, when she's not.)" (as written)
- Orenstein is a hero! Expeditiously he kites the suits of armor away from Xohn, scooping up the "sexy halfling monk" (as written).
- The party rests and heals for two days, aiming to return to the tower. While they rest, they see a dragon in the night sky.
- Orenstein reads the drow journal: the drow was to find the root of the treachery, the hand of the traitor.

## World Changes

- The party is forced to withdraw; the trapped chest's defense (two suits of armor, four flying swords) still holds its ground.
- Xohn is left behind in the room with the trap.
