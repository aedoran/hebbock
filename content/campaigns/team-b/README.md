# 🐐 Team B

> **A party of misfits** — a half-orc barbarian, a missing dragonborn paladin, a GOAT halfling monk, a Boomer warlock, and a blue dragonborn bard — climb the mountains of Hebbock to undo a curse, fight a red dragon, and find balance. *Churned from 20 sessions of notes (9/1/2024 → 3/16/2025).*

## The short of it
- **Premise:** the party assembles at the Mountain's Respite in [[mountains/locations/stonehaven|Stonehaven]] (midsummer, two weeks before the solstice) and heads for the [[mountains/locations/blackrock-peak|Blackrock Peak watchtower]] — a crumbling relic of the old [[zazi/factions/vontir-empire|Vontir Empire]] built by the [[mountains/factions/order-of-uncarved-block|Order of the Uncarved Block]].
- **Act one (S1–S9):** the watchtower — a manticore ("Get Eaten, Fuckers!"), a giant spider, an Elvish riddle chest, a pentagram shrine, and the ghost of [[mountains/npcs/vigo-dunaedin|Vigo Dunaedin]], who wants the blood of the traitor — his brother, of Astogrolluu (the new Vil'Tanee). Orenstein fails a wisdom save and becomes a **Boomer** (60 years old, 24-hour greater-restoration clock).
- **Act two (S10–S14):** the [[mountains/locations/kobold-air-tower|kobold air tower]] on the Eastern Ridge — urds and kobolds, the "Winds of Balance," the wind dials, the metal safe, the [[mountains/items/leyline-scroll|leyline scroll]], and a ten-hour flight on an 8-foot yin-yang flying carpet.
- **Act three (S15–S20):** [[mountains/locations/vryrrk-basta-caverns|Vryrrk Basta Caverns]] (the hidden dragonborn community of "Dragontown?"), the payment of the [[campaigns/team-b/items/dragon-statuette|dragon statuette]] to priest [[mountains/npcs/thrallax|Thrallax]], and the [[mountains/locations/yin-yang-tomb|yin-yang tomb]] — where the notes end, on a long rest, before the bearded face.
- **The party's working name:** "Goat Company" (S7, an "acceptable placeholder").

## Status (as of Session 20, 3/16/2025)
- Resting in the yin-yang tomb. [[campaigns/team-b/characters/thora|Thora]] missing since the statuette's blue-green flash (S12).
