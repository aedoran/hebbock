# Ulu

> Kuo-toa choir master of the defiled [[hebbock/locations/cathedral-of-dumathoin|Cathedral of Dumathoin]] — a priest-like figure with a spear for a baton, conducting the sine-wave chant that never breaks.

## Overview
Ulu stands at the heart of the mass grave, conducting the five kuo-toa who sing the droning, out-of-sync sine-wave chant — breathing in relay so the sound never breaks. The choir is bound to [[hebbock/npcs/the-witness|the Witness]]'s eye, and the moment [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/randelfine-carter|Randælfine]] struck all the acolytes at once, the eye struck back with a vision and a name.

## Details
- Race: kuo-toa
- Role: choir master, Cathedral of Dumathoin
- State: alive, mid-battle (Session IV end)

## Where He Is
