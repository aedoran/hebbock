# 🎲 Session 20 — The Bearded Face

The session begins in initiative, chasing a fish-man through deliberate architecture — and ends on a long rest, with a bearded face on the stone and a lot of questions.

## What Happened

- We begin in initiative: the party pursues the fish-man or explores their new, deliberately-constructed surroundings.
- Buckley moves ahead and finds a large bearded face on the wall — the stonework is impressive. He shouts, "I am upon him!" — he has caught up to the fish person!
- The fish-person flees into a room, and a clatter emerges. While Sir covers the flank, Buckley casts Thunderwave blindly into the room: the fish-person and a dark figure are targeted — the dark figure holds a longbow whose arrow was the source of the clatter.
- The fish-person is horrified to learn Sir has flanked, and dies at the end of the horrific greataxe attack (as written).
- A pause for inventory: "Hey guys, what do we have?" "I wonder if Orenstein has been hoarding everything. You *know* how he likes to do that."
- An investigation into the bearded face: Xohn recognizes the figure from a historical book — likely dwarven, "perhaps Moradin?" — from the History of Hebbock, from after the wars. Maybe the northeastern wars, orc wars.
- Xohn demonstrates to Sir that the fish-people and dwarves have more in common than she thought — and that we all have a great deal more in common than she thought. Sir realizes that by killing the fish-person she has in fact killed a part of herself. The bearded man in the ceiling has provided her great wisdom.
- The party finds a room for resting. Orenstein Alabaster opens the door to a second room with shrieking fungus! Its high-pitched wail attracts a horrible huge goblin, which attacks Sir twice with a greatsword. The creature retreats after Orenstein Alabaster's powerful fire illusion. Sir moves to close the door but is somehow outflanked. The creature attacks the illusory copy of Kim, much to its disappointment — giving Sir an opening to chop her battleaxe deep into its shoulder. Xohn runs in with kicks galore!
- Sir takes another hit and fails to retaliate, actually throwing her battleaxe down the hall. In the battle, Xohn's kick galore strikes purchase into its skull, dropping the creature to the ground.
- A long rest!

- The fish-person: a pouch of fish food pellets, a staff with a pincher, a cast iron hook, some rope.
- Inventory audit (the "hoarding" check): a scroll and 10 gp (old Vontir coins, yet we don't know); scroll of ley line navigation (Orenstein); scroll how to fly (Orenstein); 5 gemstones — green, yellow, red, gold, deep blue — and a silver dagger (X); "silk flying cloak ying yang, the thing we flew on" (as written — the party flew a carpet); a small wooden figurine that was a crude dragon; a quarterstaff with scorch marks (Orenstein); a tinderbox and 10 torches; a journal of dungeon layouts (Orenstein); 2nd potion of healing (Sir); leather armor with "features and small bones on it" (as written); a wooden shield (Sir); a potion of flying (Orenstein); Xohn has 2 kama (kama, kama, kamakamleon; he comes and goes, he comes and gooooooooooeeeeesss).
- Coins with an eye on one side and a sword on the other.

## World Changes

- The fish-person with the claw-topped staff is killed, and its dark-figure longbow companion is revealed and targeted by Thunderwave.
- The huge goblin drawn by the shrieking fungus is slain.
- The party takes a long rest in the cave.
