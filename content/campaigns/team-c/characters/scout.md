# 🤖 S.C.O.U.T.

> **Warforged ranger.** **S.C.O.U.T. = "Surveillance and Control Operations Unit Technology."** "Puppet or person? both." (CAST OF CHARACTERS)

## Known
- **Master cartographer** — the party's map is his; he "computes, calculates, connives, and conjures a longbow" (Session 3).
- **Catchphrases:** "**I FOUND MY TARGET**" (Session 3); "**ACQUIRING TARGETS**" (Session 3); "**GUARD MODE**" (Session 5 — activated on the popped brass lock; he also guards doors and perimeters at length, e.g. Session 12).
- **Memory:** "may have been **erased many times**" (Session 10). He "theoretically could be here, now, with another version of himself with a different memory" (Session 10).
- **Independence:** "Somehow, my independence was **granted or earned**" (Session 10) — versus the common belief that warforged have no free will.
- **The pyramid map is on him** — which is why the party's first order in the past ("find Hazi, give him the map") runs through him (Session 9).
- **In the past era he is the only warforged anyone has ever seen.** He hood up, cloaked, hiding — "I've got a bad feeling about this" (Session 8); "the people... giving SCOUT very strange looks."
- **The warforged of the notes** (Session 13) are a *different* kind: "menacing, maniacal, mechanical... in sharp contrast to SCOUT's countenance... harsher aesthetics, similar to Kraftwerk?" — his presence frightens Bishop; the warforged's face "causes a fright."

## Key moments
- **Session 1:** physically present at the table; recruits Di; "puppet or person? both."
- **Session 3:** the longbow conjured; "I FOUND MY TARGET"; the head shot that removes the hyena; cheers.
- **Session 5:** GUARD MODE; the brass lock pops.
- **Session 6:** "SCOUT and go back to the room with light"; keeps watch while the party sleeps.
- **Session 7:** triggers the gypsosphinx ("he must have triggered something"); injures himself climbing over; the arrows into the mummy; the Thunderwave off the skeletons (waited — that was Lachma; SCOUT "thunderwaves the two skeletons away from SCOUT" — the notes' phrasing).
- **Session 8:** hood up; "I've got a bad feeling about this."
- **Session 9:** follows Khortum's Loxodon trail "as soon as possible"; returns to the tavern; "I don't want to lose Khortum, I have to go"; hands the map to Di.
- **Session 10:** the wizard-account; the worry he "might leave the group"; lays low, cloaked.
- **Session 12:** the last known location "just outside the charnel house" — a shred of brown cloth, a scuffle, "carried out by experts." **Abducted.**
- **Session 13:** the ambush on *Bishop* — the cloaked watchers' orders: "They're close. They're staying at the inn. **We must follow through with our orders.**"
