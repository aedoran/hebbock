# 🐭 Bimbubop

> A mouse who lives in [[campaigns/team-c/characters/lachma|Lachma]]'s pockets and does most of the talking. "Lachma is merely a translate[r] for his mouse bimbubop" (CAST OF CHARACTERS).

## What is known
- **The voice:** the notes' "Bimbubop" lines are the campaign's most frequent third voice — questions, blessings, and the occasional "blesses you" healing tag.
- **Abilities via Lachma:** Tiny Blessing (gnoll's noggin, Session 3), goodberry poops (nine eaten by Khortum, one "constipated" for later), the **fae font of healing** (extra + temporary hit points, "Bimbubop blesses you," Session 11), and Infestation's bug-clouds (Lachma invokes "Bimbubop," Session 3).
- **The big reveal (Session 8, past era):** through "a greater understanding of dreams," Lachma understands that **Bimbubop is a conduit of fae communication to this world** — and that Bimbubop "is reaching out to Lachma to see if he can be a **Fae champion**."
- **Physicality:** Bimbubop can fall out of Lachma's pocket (Session 7 — causing Lachma to slip over the gypsosphinx); the party "cuddle[s] with Khortum" around Bimbubop while it sleeps (Session 6).
- Lachma prays "to a makeshift shrine of Bimbubop placed upon a bed" (Session 12).
