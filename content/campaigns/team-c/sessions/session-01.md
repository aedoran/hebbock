# 📓 Session 1 — It Starts in a Tavern

**Date (as written):** 5/31/25 · **Previous:** — · **Next:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-02|Session 2]]

## Setup
The CAST OF CHARACTERS is set; the world-setting blockquote is read: [[zazi/locations/anu-asir|Anu-Asir]] has risen from the sand on the Gulf of Daafia, with the [[zazi/locations/pyramid-of-tiberesh|Pyramid of Tiberesh]] across the River Nuria. "We begin in Nuzul. A tavern."

## Beats
- **[[campaigns/team-c/characters/di-guan|Di Guan]]** arrives by boat; strides to a table; orders **ceremonial tea** from the "average serving wench" — **[[zazi/npcs/fatima|Fatima]]**, "a professional wench" (tea is in circulation "due to bustling trade routes"). **Di Guan asks to be made fat.**
- **[[campaigns/team-c/characters/khortum-fil|Khortum]]** wants to see the newly emerged city; **[[campaigns/team-c/characters/scout|SCOUT]]** offers the exposition: a group is paying for antiquities recovered from the city and pyramid.
- **Bishop's pickpocketing** — "**Tongueless** has been trying to pickpocket the tavern customers, but his bird is thwarting him." (Alias question for [[campaigns/team-c/characters/bishop|Bishop]] — open.)
- **[[campaigns/team-c/characters/khortum-fil|Khortum]] intercepts the pickpocket** and offers him the chance to avoid tavern trouble by adventuring in the new city. "Khortum, alarmed, agrees."
- **[[campaigns/team-c/characters/scout|SCOUT]]** "has been asked to find a fighter" and approaches Di and his large halberd. **Di has never seen a warforged… until now!** "Puppet or person?" — "Both."
- Fatima delivers **steak and yams** ("must be in circulation due to further bustling trade routes"). Di sits with the table "after grandfather's birthday tea celebration" — grandfather is **Ping Gao**.
- **"The avengers assemble."**
- **Outside:** a make-shift tent city rises around Anu-Asir; guards post along the southern road.
- **[[campaigns/team-c/characters/bishop|Bishop]]'s thieves' organization** reports the **[[zazi/factions/golden-falcon-antiquities|Golden Falcon Antiquities]]** hiring worthy adventurers to salvage the city — with **rumors of shady dealings and non-payments** leaking out.
- A long line of prospective adventurers. A **bald, warm, handsome middle-aged man** ("a man of many details") surveys the line — **[[zazi/npcs/hazi-sucineri|Hazi Sucineri]]**, bald head of the GFA(C) — with **gnoll bodyguards** who "consult the rulebooks"; one has a **scorpion on a leash**. He is "pleasantly surprised" at the back of the line. Bishop's parrot: **"Cheater!"**
- **[[zazi/npcs/galena-ealamassi|Galena Ealamassi]]** runs the supply tent (mundane goods, "standard smells," rations, armor; **"This is not a GFA(C) company store"**; experienced alongside the GFA(C); warns: **do not cross the GFA(C)**). Adventurers have "seeds in their appearance." The party is **the first group to enter the tomb of Tiberesh** — "a cruel ruler who sought power."
- Store montage: suiting up.
- **The contract** (in Hazi's tent — couch, incense-y smells, scribes scribing; the bird does not enter; a table full of maps):
  1. Any **magical or religious relics** → property of the GFA(C).
  2. GFA(C) takes **50% of mundane treasure**.
  3. The party **draws maps**.
  4. Scope: **this one adventure**.
- Contracts signed; the party is now contracted. **Upon signing, Bishop's bird flies away.**

## Loot / notes
- Contract terms established (see above).
- Hazi's tent: incense, scribes, maps.

## Threads opened
- The GFA(C)'s shady reputation — and what "shady" means in practice.
- "Tongueless" as Bishop's alias.
- The bird's flight at the contract signing — omen, or something else?
