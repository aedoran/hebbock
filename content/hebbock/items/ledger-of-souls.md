# The Ledger of Souls

> A patron-gifted tome carried by [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/vlix-boggs|Vlix Boggs]] — a gift of [[hebbock/factions/queen-of-worlds|the Queen of Worlds]].

## Description
A tome. Its contents are not yet revealed in the chronicle.

## Properties
- Unknown — a patron-gift, so its nature presumably mirrors the Queen's: trickster, world-spanning, bound to souls.
- Given to Vlix when the Queen remade her in a single night in Hargate.

## Provenance
- Gifted by the Queen of Worlds at Vlix's transformation.

## Current Owner
- Vlix Boggs.

## Related
- [[hebbock/factions/queen-of-worlds|The Queen of Worlds]]
- The **pomegranate bond** — Vlix's other patron-gift: radiant in the bright months, necrotic when the dark ones come. (Same pomegranate as the mythic one, or a Hebbock original? See [[../../_questions|_questions.md]].)

## Open Questions
- What is in the Ledger? Does it list souls — whose? Is it a record, a claim, or a debt? **Deliberately amorphous** (aedoran's ruling): the contents are meant to stay shapeless — to be filled in through play, not defined in advance.
