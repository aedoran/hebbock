# 🎲 Session 9 — East Siiiidee

Down the iced steps and out onto the Eastern Ridge, [[campaigns/team-b/party|the party]] hunts a cure for the Boomer curse — and meets [[mountains/npcs/rhiltannaxis|Rhiltannäxis]]'s urds.

## What Happened

- The party debates whether to return to the pentagram.
- **Slam poetry in the style of HP Lovecraft**, yo biooootch! “East Siiiieeeddee…”:
  > Yo, it's an eldritch horror / lock behind a dimension door, or / Are we the terror / a terrible human error / lost in a pentagram puzzle / subject to government muzzle / but we will not be silent / we will be defiant and tryin' / to set things right / under gibbous moonlight
- The descent: [[campaigns/team-b/characters/sir|Sir]] rappels down the iced-over steps and makes it. [[campaigns/team-b/characters/orenstein-alabaster|Orenstein Alabaster]] has trouble — he slips because he's Boomer Orenstein now. Halfway down he slips and plummets. “I'm fine! How are my stocks doing??” “My third house!!” “Those damn young, lazy kids!” [[campaigns/team-b/characters/thora|Thora]] and Xuanzang / Xohn make it down; Sir catches Orenstein and they all tumble into the group, taking damage. The rope stays attached for future traversal — no thanks to the Boomer, who usually yanks up the ladder after he climbs it.
- The “large bronze bell with wave pattern” from the shrine is still on their minds.
- **Cure leads:** Thora knows that High Priest Thrallux, Cleric of Bahamut, can cast the spell to return Orenstein to his natural age. Xuanzang / Xohn knows there are airbender monks somewhere in the hills — but Thora doesn't know anything about these GOAT monks.
- Thora wants to climb back up to the ghost. [[campaigns/team-b/characters/sir|Sir]] has an idea: there's a dragon somewhere in these mountains, and she wants to use her outlander abilities to suss out where its lair is. She has no idea.
- **[[mountains/locations/stonehaven|Stonehaven]]** is the closest town — a full day's march.
- A dragon is sighted in the night sky — the mountains have a dragon, after all.
- Xuanzang / Xohn climbs back up to ask the ghost about the air temple. The ghost tells Xuanzang / Xohn and Thora the monks are off on the eastern ridge — but likely the traitor killed them too.
- The group travels the Eastern Ridge. Walking through the dark requires a survival check; they make it. They're closer to the equator, and after a few hours dawn arrives (“it's sexy time”). Four hours of walking — farther than they could see from their vantage point.
- A **tower** appears coming out of the side of the mountain. It splits into two towers as it rises to roughly 100 feet high.
- About 150 feet out, they hear the unmistakable sound of bullets. “I did my tai chi routine as a greeting but it didn't work to stop the bullets.”
- Five small humanoid shapes come down from the tower. **The battle commences.**
- Sir and Xuanzang / Xohn kill one of the flying beasts. Orenstein conjures a Silent Image of Xuanzang / Xohn — it's tai chiing away, with a Lovecraftian angle. He's still the GOAT.
- Thora's longbow misses the closest enemy. Orenstein gets hit by a rock. Sir misses. Xuanzang / Xohn kills another flying kobold (urd).
- “We have to get to Vryrrk Basta Caverns.” Thora speaks Draconic to the beings in the tower — and the lore of [[mountains/factions/order-of-uncarved-block|the Order of the Uncarved Block]] deepens: its library claims it “descends from the ancients, predates science, myriad moons ago.”
- **[[mountains/npcs/rhiltannaxis|Rhiltannäxis]] is a red dragon** — a cleric of one of the fire drake deities of Bahamut.
- Xuanzang / Xohn takes Sir's shield and charges. Sir and Thora land precise longbow shots on the two on the tower — one killed, one wounded. More urds take position and throw rocks down.
- As urds fall back to cover, Xuanzang / Xohn charges forward — **and falls into a pit trap!** Orenstein runs forward to save him; Sir readies a longbow attack.
- Cliffhanger: Will the party save Xuanzang / Xohn? Will the urds be vanquished? What about Oldenstein's ghostly curse?

## World Changes

- The party descends the mountain and moves to the Eastern Ridge, toward the tower.
- [[mountains/npcs/rhiltannaxis|Rhiltannäxis]] (red dragon, cleric of a fire drake deity of Bahamut) is confirmed as the tower's patron — the urds are his defense.
- Thora's quest is set: fight Rhiltannäxis at [[mountains/locations/kobold-air-tower|the kobold air tower]].
