# 🦅 Golden Falcon Antiquities (GFA(C))

> The salvage-and-antiquities firm that contracts adventurers to work [[zazi/locations/anu-asir|Anu-Asir]] and the [[zazi/locations/pyramid-of-tiberesh|Pyramid of Tiberesh]]. Headed (in the present) by [[zazi/npcs/hazi-sucineri|Hazi Sucineri]]; in the past, Hazi is a young map-collector still *building* it.

## The contract (Session 1)
- Any **magical or religious relics** are property of the GFA(C).
- The GFA(C) takes **50% of mundane treasure**.
- The party is to **draw maps** (the maps belong to the firm — which is how the pyramid map ends up with SCOUT, and how the party ends up owing young Hazi one in the past).
- Scope: **this one adventure only**.

## Reputation
- **Rumors of shady dealings and non-payments** are leaking out (Bishop's thieves' organization, Session 1). Galena: "not a GFA(C) company store," but she has "experience running a supply shop alongside the GFA(C)" and recommends **not crossing** them (Session 1).
- A **long line** of prospective adventurers waits for their contracts — the firm is the only formal gate to the pyramid.

## People
- [[zazi/npcs/hazi-sucineri|Hazi Sucineri]] — head of the firm (present); "a man of many details," with **gnoll bodyguards** (one keeps a **scorpion on a leash**).
- [[zazi/npcs/galena-ealamassi|Galena Ealamassi]] — supply tent (rations, armor; "standard smells").
