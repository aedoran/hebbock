# Pang

> The gnome boy of [[hebbock/locations/hargate|Hargate]] — the missing child of the counting dark. Walked off into the [[hebbock/locations/hargate-delve|Hargate Delve]] in his nightshirt on the night of the shared dream.

## Overview
Pang is the face of the plague: the dream-driven disappearance that has the townsfolk screaming. His mother's wail is what wakes the party in [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-01|Session I]]. And the chronicle's final image settles it: at the end of [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-04|Session IV]], the small boy standing entranced but living at the feet of [[hebbock/npcs/cororic|Cororic]] in the [[hebbock/locations/cathedral-of-dumathoin|Cathedral of Dumathoin]] **is Pang** (aedoran's ruling) — and [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/baelson|Baelson]] holds him safe behind his shield.

## Details
- Race: gnome (a svirfneblin like [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/pim|Pim]], or a different gnome-folk? See [[../../_questions|_questions.md]].)
- State: missing from Hargate (S1) → safe behind Baelson's shield (S4)

## Where He Is
- Last known: the inner cathedral, at Cororic's feet. "Almost done. Almost counted."

## Appearances
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-01|Session I]] — the wailing mother; the nightshirt; the empty doorway.
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-04|Session IV]] — **confirmed Pang** — the small boy at Cororic's feet, shielded by Baelson. "Almost done. Almost counted."
