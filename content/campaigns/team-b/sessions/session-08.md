# 🎲 Session 8 — The Ghost's Thirst

Up a mountain in [[hebbock/worldbuilding/hebbock|Hebbock]], [[campaigns/team-b/party|the party]] cracks open the High Elven leaf treasure — then night falls on the parapet, and a ghost arrives thirsty.

## What Happened

- Session openers, as tradition demands: “Here's my strategy on the Cold War: we win, they lose.” — Ronald Reagan; plus “I've got time to sleep all weekend” and “Wake me up if I fall asleep. Oh, also, Greg is the coolest” — Ben (true, true).
- The party finds a **gold ring with a leaf design**, matching the leaf on the velvet bag from the treasure chest. Orenstein wants to do something “very clever,” then wants them all to just grab the bags and run. Do they?
- Is the ring magical? The group tests it. [[campaigns/team-b/characters/sir|Sir]] bites into the ring. It slides onto her pinky finger. Sir is really into her new ring.
- Plan: [[campaigns/team-b/characters/orenstein-alabaster|Orenstein Alabaster]] guides a Mage Hand from behind the door, with Sir and Thora on either side of the doorway — “Go GOAT Squad, Go!” Nothing appears to happen when Orenstein grabs the bag.
- Orenstein opens the bag: an **amulet (a tortoise, another golden leaf)** — a symbol of the High Elven King, [[mountains/npcs/kalvulaen-viltannen|Kalvulaen Vil'Tannen]], “the Elven King of the High Elves in Vil'Tanee.” Orenstein is paranoid.
- Bigger bag now: a large golden ring to go on someone's head — another leaf. Another bag: three d20s, a little leaf etched into each one. [[campaigns/team-b/characters/xuanzang|Xuanzang / Xohn]] tries on all the High Elven stuff: nothing happens. They each wear one of the items anyway.
- Up the stairs. Xohn leaps atop Thora's shoulders, kills a strige, leaps onto Sir and kills a second stirge — the big brain has this great idea to kill them! Sir and Orenstein kill the other two. Lololol.
- The **shrine**: five plates with steel tracks and embedded gems, in the shape of a pentagram. The rock is weathered beyond recognition. “founding stars” is written on it in the same language as the dart found in the manticore. Eight quartz crystal gems. A large bronze bell with a wave pattern. The party will return to the puzzle at a later time.
- They ascend the ladder. Xohn almost falls! But catches himself with one hand. Because he's the GOAT.
- The party spends the evening on the parapet of [[mountains/locations/blackrock-peak|the Blackrock Peak watchtower]]. As night falls, a ghostly figure appears. Orenstein attempts to telepathically communicate with the ghost.
- Orenstein **fails a crucial wisdom saving throw and ages 40 years** — suddenly 60, chatting about his investment funds, insurance premiums and deferred payments on a reverse mortgage (only if he's a Boomer — and a white boomer too). “What a horror.”
- Thora understands this to be a ghost — but an innocent lost soul or a malevolent presence?
- **Xohn's circlet bears the mark of the traitor!** So there goes diplomacy. We tried.
- “Old Man Orenstein” is his new name. Old Man Orenstein likes to yell at clouds.
- The ghost is **[[mountains/npcs/vigo-dunaedin|Vigo Dunaedin]]**: “Only the blood of the traitor can quench his thirst.” (I thirst for Boomer Orenstein.)
- The traitor was Vigo's brother — and he is from **Astogrolluu, the new name of Vil'Tanee**.
- Orenstein has **24 hours** to have greater restoration cast on him to reverse the aging.
- Orenstein inquires about the puzzle and is rebuffed.

## Loot & Finds

- Gold ring, leaf design — now on Sir's pinky.
- Tortoise amulet with a golden leaf — a symbol of High Elven King [[mountains/npcs/kalvulaen-viltannen|Kalvulaen Vil'Tannen]].
- Large golden circlet (another leaf) and three leaf-etched d20s — [[campaigns/team-b/items/high-elven-leaf-treasure|the High Elven leaf treasure]] grows.
- The pentagram shrine: five plates, steel tracks, eight quartz crystals, a large bronze bell with a wave pattern, and “founding stars” in the manticore-dart language.

## World Changes

- Orenstein Alabaster is now 60 — “Boomer Orenstein” — with a 24-hour clock on greater restoration.
- The ghost of Vigo Dunaedin haunts the mountain parapet by night, demanding the blood of his traitorous brother.
- Xuanzang / Xohn's circlet is revealed to bear the mark of the traitor.
