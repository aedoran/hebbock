# Hargate

> A fading mountain town in [[hebbock/worldbuilding/hebbock|Hebbock]], built on the slopes above the [[hebbock/locations/hargate-delve|Hargate Delve]]. Its glory days as host of the **Goat Goat Festival** are long behind it.

## Geography
A mountain town whose livelihood ran on delving — and on the yearly festival that crowned the finest goat in the world. Now it's a place in decline, where three strangers can share an inn and a dream without anyone noticing.

## Notable Features
- **The Goat Goat Festival** — the yearly tournament that once made the town. A "front for a criminal underworld" in [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/baelson|Baelson]]'s eyes (he came hunting one; found a different kind of underground).
- **The delve mouth** — the way down into the [[hebbock/locations/hargate-delve|Hargate Delve]], at the foot of the town.
- **The tavern where Vlix grew up** — where the party hired its guides and set out.

## Inhabitants & Powers
- The town's dreaming sick — a plague of dream-driven disappearances (the gnome boy [[hebbock/npcs/pang|Pang]] walked off into the delve in his nightshirt).
- [[hebbock/npcs/brother-hethrin|Brother Hethrin]] and [[hebbock/npcs/sister-maugra|Sister Maugra]] of the [[hebbock/factions/vault-of-dumathoin|Vault of Dumathoin]] operate here, mounting the expedition that becomes the party.

## Current State
**Uneasy.** The counting dream has spread to the party, and something in the deep is calling the townsfolk in [[hebbock/npcs/cororic|Cororic]]'s voice.
