# 🔥 The Northern War

> The great war raging on the **northwestern continent** — the defining world event of both eras the party lives in.

## What is known
- **The belligerents:** factions led by "powerful wizards" who "serve as generals"; the reason for the war is unclear — "ancient hatreds, vendettas, or pride." Information is scarce this far south.
- **The weather of war:** "raining magical fire across the land"; charred, burned landscapes.
- **The warforged:** "new mechanical beings... magically created, self-aware constructs 'forged for war'." They were built by the wizards — "the wizards who made the warforged... fought each other." By the **past** era the warforged are **rebelling against their magical masters**.
- **Skyhammers:** airships with magical sails, "made from northern trees and fueled by dark magic and the elements" — common talk in the past era.
- **In the past era** (~20 years before the present): the war is at its height; the Vontir Empire's western outposts have "minor rebellions."
- **In the present era:** the war still rages (Hazi, the "news junkie," tracks it), though details remain scarce in [[zazi/locations/zazi|Zazi]].

## Why it matters
- It explains why [[campaigns/team-c/characters/scout|SCOUT]] is terrified in the past: he is a **warforged** — the only one anyone there has ever seen — and the "creatures of war" have no free will in the popular belief. He may have another self there, "with a different memory." His memory "may have been erased many times."
- It explains the hostiles in Sessions 12–13: hostile warforged in brown cloaks follow orders about the party, and a squad of four (two confirmed warforged among them) attacks Bishop in the streets.
- The wizards-as-generals detail, and the "soft encroachment" history of [[campaigns/team-c/characters/di-guan|Di's people]], tie the war to the older Vontir conflicts.
