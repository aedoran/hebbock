# Brain Cane

> The drug of the [[hebbock/locations/silt-pool|Silt Pool]]: *it makes you smarter, and talk a lot faster.* Cooked by [[hebbock/npcs/gri-the-asht-chewer|Gri the Asht-Chewer]] from the silt the goblins dredge.

## Description
A white powder, sold by the three-pound pouch. The party now carries several — plus whatever is in [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/pim|Pim]]'s small body.

## Properties
- **Makes you smarter, and talk a lot faster.** The party's read of it, from Nyx.
- In a small body, a drow-sized dose turns Pim into **Pimp** — faster, louder, and utterly convinced of her own invincibility.
- At full speed, it loosens tongues and morals: Randælfine's rapier through [[hebbock/npcs/brother-hethrin|Brother Hethrin]]'s chainmail was delivered "at full brain-cane speed" — twelve damage, with a four-item skewering list.
- Baelson, already high on St. Cuthbert, declines it "on the record."

## Provenance
- The silt of the Silt Pool, cooked by Gri. The goblins' whole economy runs on it — "All we want is the mushrooms!"

## Current Owner
- The party — Randælfine and Vlix each took a bag; the table's drug-cartel impulse was noted ("Yeah, sorry Tony. We're going to stop here and instead set up a drug cartel.").
