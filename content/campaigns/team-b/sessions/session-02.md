# 🎲 Session 2 — Rats at the Gate

The [[campaigns/team-b/party|party]] crosses the gate of the [[mountains/locations/blackrock-peak|Blackrock Peak watchtower]] and immediately meets rats, skeletons, a giant spider, and an unseen enemy. "This shit cray."

## What Happened

- The party stands at the gated entryway of the watchtower, a crumbling relic of the old [[zazi/factions/vontir-empire|Vontir Empire]].
- Peering in through the large doors, the paladin's sense pings a shadowed figure in the distance, its eyes glowing with red malevolence.
- The barbarian draws axe and shield, ready to strike; the halfling monk strikes a defensive pose; "the human raises an eyebrow, curious about the figure ahead!"
- [[campaigns/team-b/characters/sir|Sir]] cautiously steps forward… and is swarmed by starving rats!
- Rats defeated!
- [[campaigns/team-b/characters/xuanzang|Xohn]] spots a giant spider up on the ceiling!
- "Now there are skeletons, wtf!" Battle ensues. "This shit cray." (again)
- The skeletons are defeated — "by Xohn Xohn Xohn!"
- The spider skitters away into the dark.
- An unseen foe begins attacking [[campaigns/team-b/characters/thora|Thora]], the paladin. What happens next??
