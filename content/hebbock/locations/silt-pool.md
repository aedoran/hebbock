# The Silt Pool

> A waist-deep pool of silt on the floor of the [[hebbock/locations/bioluminescent-gorge|Bioluminescent Gorge]] — the goblins' mushroom camp, and the site of a battle.

## Geography
A firelit pool where six goblins dredge silt from the water, under the eye of a shield-bearing chief and a cloth-robed shaman muttering over colored mushrooms by a fire. The silt is cooked by the shaman into [[hebbock/items/brain-cane|brain cane]].

## Notable Features
- **The mushroom harvest** — the goblins' whole economy: "All we want is the mushrooms!"
- **The Brain Cane works** — where the drug is made (see [[hebbock/items/brain-cane|brain cane]]).
- **The kuo-toa alcove** — two kuo-toa ([[hebbock/npcs/glul|Glul]], [[hebbock/npcs/lib|Lib]]) hidden ahead of the camp.

## Inhabitants & Powers
- [[hebbock/npcs/nyx|Nyx]] — the goblin chief / mushroom entrepreneur.
- [[hebbock/npcs/gri-the-asht-chewer|Gri the Asht-Chewer]] — the shaman, cooker of brain cane.
- [[hebbock/npcs/glul|Glul]] & [[hebbock/npcs/lib|Lib]] — the kuo-toa of unusual sanity.

## Current State
**Ransacked.** The party won the [[#the-battle|Battle of the Silt Pool]]; the goblins broke or begged; Nyx was charmed and stripped; the party claimed the drug. Gri fled into the dark, poison-dusted and furious.

### The Battle
Pim's natural-20 crossbow opened it; Baelson waded in scooping silt; Lib belly-flopped and died; Vlix's Witch Bolt of nineteen damage stripped the pool of reactions (table physics subcommittee). The chief called it: "Enough. Enough. Stop."
