# ⬜ Order of the Uncarved Block

> The order that built the watchtowers of the [[mountains/README|mountain region]] — balance, inner peace, and a certain **return to nature**. *From the Team B notes (Sessions 3, 9, 13–16); the order's full shape is a growing mystery.*

## Origin
- Started **"many, many years ago"** — founded by **veterans of the orc purging war and the fall of the [[zazi/factions/vontir-empire|Vontir Empire]]**, half a millennia ago (the Session 3 account).
- The library's own claim is grander: the order **"descends from the ancients, predates science, myriad moons ago."**
- Its towers rose and fell with the Old Vontir empire — per Yongo the Younger's manuscript found in [[mountains/locations/vryrrk-basta-caverns|Vryrrk Basta Caverns]]: *"After the long war, despite victory, the Old Vontir empire collapsed."*

## Doctrine
- **Inner peace to reflect balance** — the "Winds of Balance": *rise with the sun, descend with the moon*, with an element↔direction mapping (the full poem is in [[campaigns/team-b/sessions/session-13|Session 13]]).
- **Return to nature** — the notes flag this as "definitely a death-drive thing."

## Organization
- **Monks** build and maintain the towers (the [[mountains/locations/blackrock-peak|Blackrock Peak watchtower]], the [[mountains/locations/kobold-air-tower|Eastern Ridge air tower]]).
- A **mummified monk** waits in the [[mountains/locations/yin-yang-tomb|yin-yang tomb]] (Session 18).
- An **undercover/spy subset** of the order exists, of which [[campaigns/team-b/characters/xuanzang|Xuanzang]] knows little.
- **Xuanzang** (halfling monk) is a member who **broke a rule** and was sent on a **quest to find balance again** — he doesn't know how.

## In play
- The order's legacy is everywhere in the campaign: the towers, the glyphs, the kama statues, the wind dials, the [[mountains/items/leyline-scroll|leyline scroll]] — and the red dragon [[mountains/npcs/rhiltannaxis|Rhiltannäxis]] who waits at the kobold tower.
