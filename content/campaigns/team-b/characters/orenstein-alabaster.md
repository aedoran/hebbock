# 📖 Orenstein Alabaster

> The human spellcaster — "Orenstein knows things." Session 1's header calls him a **wizard**; later notes call him a **warlock** (great power given by a god, "and expects everyone else to have been given this power as well"). The conflict is kept, not resolved.

## Who
- **The Boomer:** in Session 8, pressing telepathy on the ghost of [[mountains/npcs/vigo-dunaedin|Vigo Dunaedin]], he **fails a crucial wisdom save and ages 40 years** — suddenly 60. "Old Man Orenstein likes to yell at clouds." The running gag (stocks, insurance, reverse mortgages, "We Didn't Start the Fire," bird flu as "a construct of elves") compresses in the session pages; the 24-hour clock to a **greater restoration** is the campaign's act-one deadline.
- **The familiar:** [[campaigns/team-b/npcs/kim|Kim]] the sprite (origin unclear in the notes — "all four [summons] are horrifically flawed," as written).
- **The hands:** his **mage hand** is the party's trap-bait and treasure-arm; his **eldritch blasts** carry messages from the Great Old One ("probably 'eat my ass'," Session 15).
- **The knowledge:** he reads the drow journal (the traitor's hand), the map of Hebbock (scrawled "correctly" in his madness), the [[mountains/items/leyline-scroll|leyline scroll]], and the Vryrrk Basta library manuscript (naming the author **Yongo the Younger**, with Cotter).

## In play
- **S1–S7:** assembles the party; the kibble-vs-mouse rations offer; "Be best."
- **S8:** the failed save, the 24-hour clock; "I'm fine! How are my stocks doing??" (rappelling slip).
- **S11–S12:** "Orenstein's Glorious [Boomer] Body" (the monologue is compressed in the session page); 16 hours left on the grandfather-clock; the safe's contents; the scroll of ley line navigation; the **scroll how to fly**.
- **S13–S14:** brute-forces the 128 wind-dial combos in under 15 minutes; the cyclone (Sir catches him); aloft on the yin-yang carpet.
- **S15–S20:** the eldritch-blast siren fight; the huge eldritch blast that chunks the toad's "rubbery head"; the fire illusion that drives off the shrieking-fungus goblin; the hoarding audit.
