# 🏛️ Anu-Asir

> A city once believed to exist only in myth — submerged under sand, floodwater, and tall tales until recently. Now the hub of the [[campaigns/team-c/README|Team C]] adventure.

## As described (Session 1 setting text)
"Just outside [[zazi/locations/zazi|Zazi]], located on the Gulf of Daafia, lie the newly discovered remains of a city once believed to exist only in myth. Until now, the city of Anu-Asir lay submerged under accumulations of sand, floodwater, and tall tales. It is home to a population shrouded in mystery and rumors of **magical decimation** brought upon them by a **false prophet**. Although it lies in largely unexplored ruins, Anu-Asir is now a hub of activity for those wishing to uncover its secrets and profit from them."

## Now
- A **make-shift tent city** is being constructed around it (Session 1); guards post along the southern road.
- Droves of "the curious, hopeful, entrepreneurial, and eccentric, as well as those with less pure intentions" congregate around it.
- The [[zazi/factions/golden-falcon-antiquities|Golden Falcon Antiquities (GFA(C))]] hire adventurers to salvage it and the pyramid; [[zazi/npcs/hazi-sucineri|Hazi Sucineri]] (bald, warm, "a man of many details") is their present-era head, with gnoll bodyguards — one has a scorpion on a leash.
- [[zazi/npcs/galena-ealamassi|Galena Ealamassi]] runs the supply tent ("This is not a GFA(C) company store").

## The disaster
Ancient legends attribute "the disaster that plunged the city beneath the sand and the plagues that swept over its people" to the hubris of [[zazi/npcs/tiberesh|Tiberesh]]. Whether Tiberesh *is* the "false prophet" of the decimation, or a later usurper, is not yet distinguished in the notes — open question.
