# Vlix Boggs

> Once a frumpy, bespectacled tavern orphan of [[hebbock/locations/hargate|Hargate]]. Remade in a single night by trickster fae: teal-eyed, beautiful, sassy as a southern summer — and armed with an Eldritch Blast she barely understands.

## Overview
Vlix is the party's face, its honey, and its sharpest eye. Her charisma is her entire character sheet, and she knows it. She carries the **Ledger of Souls** — a patron-gifted tome — and a **pomegranate bond** that ties her soul to both life and death: radiant in the bright months, necrotic when the dark ones come.

> "Well, first of all, my eyes are up here, sugar."

## Appearance
Teal eyes; teal/blue flecks in her skin that pulse soothingly when she charms. Beautiful. Southern.

## Personality
- Insults as openers; honey as a weapon; sees through every lie, including [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/baelson|Baelson]]'s.
- Her breaking point is old and simple: a hand reaching for a child. "Let's not get handsy, sugar." — followed by an Eldritch Blast that hurled [[hebbock/npcs/cororic|Cororic]] ten feet.
- In Session IV, her celestial blood shrugged off radiant fire that would have killed a lesser level one, and she "learned in that moment something of what she truly is."
