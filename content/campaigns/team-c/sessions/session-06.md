# 📓 Session 6 — Traps and Doors

**Date (as written):** 9/20/2025 · **Previous:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-05|Session 5]] · **Next:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-07|Session 7]]

> Epigraph: *"The most dangerous trap is the one that tricks you with light!"* — Mehmet Murat ildan (????)

## Beats
- **The trapdoor reveal:** [[campaigns/team-c/characters/khortum-fil|Khortum]] "collapses into the room in a trance like state"; the party props his head with the zebra rug ("make sure the ears are safe"); [[campaigns/team-c/characters/di-guan|Di]] moves Khortum into a corner and "badly tries to cover him with the zebra rug."
- The wall protrusions: Di figures the room is "**for drawing venom from poisonous creatures and for religious purposes**."
- The trapdoor is opened to peer in: **a ladder going down about 20 ft.**
- **[[campaigns/team-c/characters/bishop|Bishop]] descends head-first** to peek; thumbs up — "safe for us all to continue down." Lachma carries Khortum's lantern.
- **Below:** two **statues flank golden doors with plaques:**
  - **North:** *"I sustain and guide all who live, while caged in a hidden, white prison."*
  - **South:** *"I am a twinkling beacon sheathed in stone; we all are drawn to doom."*
  - "Bishop waves the arms frantically to go back up."
- **The scarab key:** SCOUT returns to the room with the light; Bishop cautions everyone to stand back.
  - Bishop **dips one finger in the pool** and touches the **red scarab** — it "gently glows red." Thumbs up.
  - Bishop **dips two fingers** and touches the **orange scarab** — **the six cat-people are now out of stasis.**
- **The Nkosi fight** (six [[zazi/creatures/nkosi|cat-people]]):
  - First cat leaps at Lachma; another runs in on Di (hits); the next also on Di (misses).
  - Bishop **mind-slivers a cat.**
  - Lachma (in snake form) **bites a cat's neck and kills it**; SCOUT hits a cat.
  - Two cats attack snake-form Lachma; Di attacks both and **misses both**; a cat hits Di back; another lands.
  - Bishop's shortbow from the darkness — hits and **kills** the cat on Di.
  - A cat on SCOUT swings and misses; **Lachma and Bimbubop constrict** a cat.
  - A cat **lets out a large strange roar and transforms into a lion** "preparing to pounce."
  - Di "**scats**" and hits it "very, very hard."
  - Bishop, from the darkness, **leaps onto the lion, both daggers, hits with both.**
  - The lion goes for Bishop — he dodges the bite; the claw hits him.
  - Lachma **constricts and squeezes the life out of the lion.**
  - The formerly-grappled cat swings and hits Di; Bishop comes out of the darkness and hits again; **Di swings and "completely swings and scats and decapitates the cat."**
  - SCOUT attacks and **kills** the cat in front of him with a short sword.
  - **Lachma heals Di and SCOUT.**
- **Lighting the rainbow:** Bishop cleans the knives and does **Yellow, then Green, Blue, Violet** — "the rainbow is lit and glow in unison, and the **stone tablet moves aside** and a door becomes visible."
- "Everyone returns to cuddle with Khortum while SCOUT watches while everyone sleeps."
