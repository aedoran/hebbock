# Glul

> A kuo-toa cultist of unusual sanity — the leader of the two hidden in an alcove in the [[hebbock/locations/bioluminescent-gorge|Bioluminescent Gorge]], who spat that he hated "the drowned" and coveted magic.

## Overview
Glul is the voice of reason in a species that usually isn't given one. When [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/randelfine-carter|Randælfine]] fed him the "squeeze out the magic" deception — that the goblins below had stolen great magic of the depths and were *spongy* — Glul hissed first ("Quiet. Don't move. Not for us. For you. Shout here and the whole gorge will hear us."), then *sensibly declined to leap from lethal height* — but he led the party down toward the goblin camp.

## Details
- Race: kuo-toa
- Role: cultist, Silt Pool / gorge
- State: alive (last seen leading the party to the camp)

## Personality
- Unusually sane. Cautious. Hates "the drowned." Covets magic.

## Relationships
- [[hebbock/npcs/lib|Lib]] — the meeker of the two; the one who belly-flopped.

- The deep — his people are bound to the same dark that counts.
