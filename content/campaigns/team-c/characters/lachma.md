# 🌿 Lachma

> **Half-orc druid.** "Lachma is merely a translate[r] for his mouse [[zazi/npcs/bimbubop|bimbubop]] who lives in his pockets." (CAST OF CHARACTERS)

## Known
- **Forms:** snake constrict (kills two Nkosi/lions in Session 6; misses a warforged in Session 13), **donkey/ass** (the Session 10–11 "weapon of ass distraction"), and presumably more.
- **Magic:** poison spray / poison mist (mist is "super effective" — the gnoll needs "medical attention... a referral from his primary care veterinarian"), **Infestation** (bug-clouds; "ew it's gross"), **Hold Person** (cast twice on the Sultan — shrugged off both times), Thunderwave (the kill-shot on the chandelier, Session 11), healing ("Bimbubop will bless you" — 8 HP to Di, Session 5; the **fae font of healing** for extra + temp HP, Session 11), Tiny Blessing (Session 3, via Bimbubop).
- **Medicine:** identifies the de-organing room's purpose ("from a religious point of view, this chamber is used to prepare humanoids for mummification," Session 5); performs Tellya's autopsy (Session 11); deduces alligators may live in the city sewers from the poetry book's alligator-hide cover (Session 12).
- **The Bimbubop connection:** through "a greater understanding of dreams" (Session 8), Lachma learns Bimbubop is **a conduit of fae communication** reaching out to make Lachma a **Fae champion**; Lachma prays to "a makeshift shrine of Bimbubop placed upon a bed" (Session 12).
- The party's emotional barometer: "Lachma freaks out and waddles behind cover" (Session 3); "goes off freaking out saying this is not possible" (Session 9); Khortum's job is often "to calm Lachma's anxiety" (Session 10).

## Key moments
- **Session 3:** poison spray (no effect, "glared"), poison mist (effective), Infestation, two-handed staff + Bimbubop's Tiny Blessing.
- **Session 4:** left "in the security of a dead-end room full of wooden crates and weird organ jars."
- **Session 5:** the silent entrance that surprises Khortum; water on the purple scarab (nothing); the flask is his.
- **Session 6:** the snake constricts and squeezes; heals Di and SCOUT; "Bimbubop will bless you."
- **Session 7:** climbs the gypsosphinx; **Bimbubop falls out of his pocket → Lachma slips** (minor injury).
- **Session 10–11:** the donkey. "He is a great ass." Deploys Bishop, bucks, brays, gets kicked back by a gnoll, re-forms, Hold Person ×2, the fae healing, Thunderwave.
- **Session 12:** carries Tellya's body ("offers his ass-istence").
- **Session 13:** "eating like a freak"; poison mist on warforged ("barely notice"); the snake dive that misses.
