# Cathedral of Dumathoin

> A small cathedral built into the living rock at the floor of the [[hebbock/locations/bioluminescent-gorge|Bioluminescent Gorge]] — the seat of the [[hebbock/factions/vault-of-dumathoin|Vault of Dumathoin]], and the heart of the counting dark.

## Geography
Sixteen feet of ancient dwarven stonework, flanked by carved dwarves with maces and shields. Its great door is dimly lit by braziers. Inside: an antechamber of dust and scattered pews, and beyond it, the inner cathedral — a mass grave of two hundred dead laid in concentric circles, with [[hebbock/npcs/the-witness|the Witness]]'s eye floating eight feet above the exact center.

## Notable Features
- **The defiled doors** — someone has recently carved **new open eyes over the statues' eyes**. To the Dumathoin, the eye is sacred; to deface one is a grave transgression.
- **The antechamber carvings** — saner near the door, madder toward the back: *"It's not here. It's not here."* → *"This is not the prize."*
- **The mass grave** — two hundred dead in concentric circles (drow, dwarves, gnomes, humans, kuo-toa), rotted across decades, strange fungus blooming from the older rings. An open-air mass grave; an Underdark war crime.
- **The eye** — lidless, ethereal, a black hole the mind slides off of. It belongs to [[hebbock/npcs/the-witness|the Witness]].
- **The choir** — kuo-toa singing a droning, out-of-sync sine-wave chant, conducted by [[hebbock/npcs/ulu|Ulu]].

## Inhabitants & Powers
- [[hebbock/npcs/cororic|Cororic]] — the lost holy man, now the Witness's smiling herald.
- [[hebbock/npcs/ulu|Ulu]] — the kuo-toa choir master.
- [[hebbock/npcs/sister-maugra|Sister Maugra]] — entered alone in Session III; her fate is unknown.

## Current State
**Defiled and under the Witness's gaze.** The party is mid-battle within it (Session IV); Brother Hethrin has turned against them to defend his lost saint.
