# 🎲 Session 15 — The Flying Carpet

The party rides an 8-foot yin-yang flying carpet on a ten-hour flight to [[mountains/locations/vryrrk-basta-caverns|Vryrrk Basta Caverns]], hunting the High Priest who can lift the Boomer curse off [[campaigns/team-b/characters/orenstein-alabaster|Orenstein Alabaster]]. (Won't you take me to—Dragontown!?)

## What Happened

- The flight is a ten-hour slog. Two hours in, cold overtakes the party and Xohn's concentration is momentarily flustered — the carpet wavers dangerously, and he regains control through willpower. He stays at the helm through several more tests, exhausted by the elements. (Orenstein's bones are cold. His heart is warm, though.)
- The carpet catches an air rapid and zooms downward quickly. Sirens attack: Buckley's *Faerie Fire* lights one violet, the second winged creature saves. Xohn thinks about lighting a doobie to get higher, faster, and listens to some Phish and Grateful Dead, man.
- The siren song captivates Boomerstein just as he's ready to blast them out of the sky with "gibbous tendrils of arcane energy"; Buckley summons the image of a flying fat gerbil with tiny wings; Boomerstein falls for the sirens' promises of Margaritaville, then shakes it off "with the wisdom of previous generations that granted him all of the benefit but for which he takes all of the benefit (lolol)."
- Buckley sizzles the sirens with a spell. Xohn reaches a state of ass-kicking zen and kicks a siren in the ribs (assuming it has ribs — just as yummy as chicken breasts). Sir strikes with her axe. Boomerstein lands a mighty eldritch blast carrying a message from the Great Old One, which is probably "eat my ass." The sirens withdraw, and the party flies on above 1,000 feet, steeled against the cold.
- A rough landing in a field of meadows and leas. A dragonborn hails the party — great sword and (sexy) bronze skin, (black, tight) leather pants and boots/boobs: "What up, biotches?" He is [[mountains/npcs/argin-noroxious|Argin Noroxious]], who loved Buckley's performance and arranges the introduction to the tribe. Sir introduces herself as a friend of Thora.
- Buckley bards his backstory: barred from the warrior clan, he idolized a Blue Dragon Battle Bard named Jeff, disguised himself into a rival-clan battle, and accidentally shot Jeff — stripped of his surname and known as **Scott Jeffkiller**. His final meditation went wrong: he was thrust into "The Sapphire Dream," a shared world spanning generations of his tribe, where elders from the past and future spoke just out of his comprehension. He woke violently after hours of "sleep" and remains "slightly shifted into the very near future" — which makes him inexplicably lucky.
- The cavern is hidden away; stone stairs lead down into a dark, vast cave with torches lit like fireflies in the distance. Five dragonborn await, one of them the venerable priest Thrallax (the High Priest they came for is referred to as "Thrallux" in the notes).
- Boomerstein wants the party to part with treasure they earned to pay Thrallax for the spell; the curse takes firmer hold as time winds down. Thrallax accepts the dragon statuette as payment for the spell and material cost. Sir talks herself into letting it go — "it was the last connection to Thora."
- The ritual: a decorated hall, frankincense lit, an hour-long ceremony. Bahamut is invoked, the diamond dust is poured — and the Boomerstein curse is lifted! **Orenstein has returned to us.** GOATS gotta GOAT!
- A vibrant dragonborn community with many downtime options. 2–3 days of rest are granted and the characters level up to 3rd level. The unidentified potion is identified as a *potion of flying*.
- In the library, a book falls from nowhere: *The Curious Case of Argyle McBottoms*. Argyle is visited by three warlock summons, jumps into the crypt, chats with a dead relative, and is driven mad. Orenstein Alabaster takes the book; it is marked with a painting of a chain.
- Character work: Xohn commits to the **Path of the Open Hand**; Sir embraces an eagle totem spirit after the carpet flight; Buckley attends the **College of Glamour**.

## World Changes

- The Boomer curse on Orenstein Alabaster is lifted by Thrallax's ritual in Bahamut's name.
- The party reaches 3rd level after 2–3 days of rest in the dragonborn settlement.
- The dragon statuette is spent as payment; the party no longer carries it.
