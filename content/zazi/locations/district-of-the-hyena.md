# 🐺 District of the Hyena

> The **northeast district** of [[zazi/locations/zazi|Zazi]], "divided by various areas of control by the gnolls." Liminal space between the city proper and the gnoll quarter.

## What is known
- **[[zazi/npcs/karima|Karima]]'s tent** stands in its border zone — "not a formal affair," with many species present, "the scents of coffee and exotic spices," hookah smoke; she "likes meeting people in both high and low places. She does not discriminate." A second-in-command (the notes joke "Karima Jr?") keeps the tent when she's out.
- The **[[zazi/locations/charnel-house|Charnel House]]** is here, on **Bashtoor Street**.
- **Jackal's Run** — a road by the great sand pyramid; **Jackal's Run Bridge** at the pyramid's southeastern tip is the dawn rendezvous with Karima.
- The party's warforged ambush (Session 13) happens a block or two from the inn, "in a section of houses more like apartment buildings" — the pursuit runs through the district's streets as the city watch bell tolls.
- SCOUT's abduction happens "a city block away from the charnel house": a shredded brown cloak (possibly SCOUT's), footprints of a scuffle, "carried out by experts."
