# 🦅 Gypsosphinx

> "A creature with the body of a feline, the head of a human and wings." In the pyramid it appears as a **colossal moving statue** guarding the corridor to [[zazi/locations/pyramid-of-tiberesh|Tiberesh's tomb]].

## In play (Session 7)
- Paws curved around a **large cylinder at its base** — an icon Bishop recognizes. *What icon, what the cylinder is, and what the lion/scarab/cat/snake runestones open are **mysteries by design** (ruled 2026-08-21) — the campaign answers them, not pre-fills.*
- It **moves 10 ft/round** on a trigger (SCOUT's movement near it trips something; a portcullis descends behind the party at the same moment — "#triggered").
- The party climbs **over** it: Bishop, Khortum, Di easy; **SCOUT injures himself**; Lachma slips when **Bimbubop falls out of his pocket** (minor injury).
- It grinds stone on stone as it stops against the portcullis; the party wedges the portcullis open with rocks and lifts it with Khortum's help.

## Open questions
- Is it a statue animated by magic, a true creature, or a construct?
- What is the cylinder at its base, and what is the icon?
