# 🧕 Karima

> A **gnoll** of unusual bearing — "a long purple cloak... a yellow headscarf... a face that is clearly canine, yet also beautiful... long, dark eyebrows, bright, flashing eyes, and smooth, lustrous fur." "She neither barks nor bites." (Session 10)

## Who she is
- Lives in the liminal space between [[zazi/locations/zazi|the city proper]] and the [[zazi/locations/district-of-the-hyena|District of the Hyena]], in a tent that "is not a formal affair" — coffee, exotic spices, hookah smoke; many species present.
- "She likes meeting people in both high and low places. **She does not discriminate.**" (Session 10)
- Runs an entourage; a **second-in-command** (the notes joke "Karima Jr?") keeps the tent in her absence (Session 12).
- She is **[[zazi/npcs/hazi-sucineri|Hazi's]] contact in the district** (Session 12) — and a "good friend" in his past-era words (Session 9).
- Hates [[zazi/npcs/sultan-shukranil|the Sultan]] "and will do anything to hurt him" (Session 10).

## In play
- Hires the party (Session 10): **500 gold, gratitude, "maybe a passionate night"** to retrieve the body and belongings of her scholar friend **[[zazi/npcs/tellya-hakkam|Tellya Hakkam]]** from the [[zazi/locations/charnel-house|charnel house]].
- The body was found **by the River of Sand**; the scholar "had been studying Bastet, deciphering texts."
- Rendezvous: **dawn, at the southeastern tip of the pyramid, near Jackal's Run Bridge.**
- The party completes the job (burns the gnolls, retrieves body + belongings + two keys) — but whether Karima is actually paid, and what she does with the Bastet parchment, is **not yet on record** (Session 13 ends before the dawn handoff).

## Threads
- Her friend's parchment — the **Bastet rhyme** (see [[zazi/locations/charnel-house#the-job-sessions-10-12|Charnel House]]) — is unsolved: nineteen karams, a rope, a stake, a shadow, "secrets below... his to be caught."
- Who killed Tellya? The small sooty handprints, the mouth full of sand, the R/S-marked bodies.
- Why does the Sultan *hate* her?
