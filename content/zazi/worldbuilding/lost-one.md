# 🐘 The Lost One

> The god of the [[zazi/factions/loxodon-kingdom|Loxodons]] — alive and worshipped in the **past**, dead or absent in the **present**. The name "Lost One" is how [[campaigns/team-c/characters/khortum-fil|Khortum]] (and the notes) refer to him; a true name is not given. (Open question: what is the god's real name?)

## What is known
- In the **past era** (~20 years before the party's present) the Lost One is a living god: a stone temple stands in the Loxodon lands, with roadside shrines and hidden stations of devotion all along the road. Khortum is "drawn to the herd he has never experienced in the present" and follows the Loxodons to the temple — "the end of a pilgrimage across time."
- The god "spread out the soul before" — per the DM's parenthetical ruling in the notes — so Khortum's presence in the past is **not** the cause of the god's later faltering.
- In the **present**, the god is dead or absent: Khortum carries "a fragment of his lost god's soul," and his sense of loss is the engine of his character.
- The party calls the mummy in Tiberesh's tomb "the avatar of the Lost One" (Khortum's shout, Session 7) — implying the [[zazi/npcs/tiberesh|Tiberesh]] mummy is an *avatar* of this god, not the god itself. Whether Tiberesh *is* the Lost One, or a corrupt vessel of it, is unresolved (open question).
- In the past, rumors circulate in [[zazi/locations/zazi|Zazi]] that "a divine presence in the material world" may be physically manifesting among the Loxodons — Hazi raises his eyebrows at the word "gods," suggesting the idea is unusual or controversial there.

## In play
- **Session 9.5 — Khortum's Pilgrimage:** Khortum sleeps at the temple, sits before the large statue, "closes his eyes to find the serenity and purpose impossible in the present time," and is "imbued with a purpose. The herd must be protected."
- The Lost One protects Khortum in the charnel house: a "ramming elephant made of holy light" answers each blow against his Shield of Faith (Session 11).

## Open questions
- The god's true name.
- The exact relationship between Tiberesh (the mummy), the "avatar" claim, and the Lost One.
- What (or who) killed the Lost One, if "dead" is the right word — does a god not die but fade?
