# Brother Hethrin

> A dwarven cleric of the [[hebbock/factions/vault-of-dumathoin|Vault of Dumathoin]] — calm, roped, and certain of his order — who in the [[hebbock/locations/cathedral-of-dumathoin|Cathedral of Dumathoin]] turned on the party to defend his lost saint.

## Overview
Hethrin and [[hebbock/npcs/sister-maugra|Sister Maugra]] hired the party in [[hebbock/locations/hargate|Hargate]] to bring home [[hebbock/npcs/cororic|Cororic]], kindly. He is the party's moral compass, its rope-binder in the gorge, and — as of Session IV — its turncoat.

## Details
- Race: dwarf
- Role: cleric, Vault of Dumathoin

## Relationships
- [[hebbock/npcs/sister-maugra|Sister Maugra]] — his fellow cleric; the one the gods protect; she entered the cathedral alone and he "could not stop her."
- [[hebbock/npcs/cororic|Cororic]] — "his lost saint." Hethrin, horrified, screaming "No!", turned on the party to defend him.
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/baelson|Baelson]] — the one he anchored for the gorge traverse (and later, the one he screams at).

## Where He Is
- Mid-battle in the Cathedral of Dumathoin (Session IV end), at war with the party, having taken Randælfine's rapier through the chainmail.

## Appearances
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-01|Session I]] — inserts himself between the party and the widow; the expedition's sponsor.
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-02|Session II]] — "Let us handle this. We know what to do."; the traverse; the *Guiding Bolt* that collapses the first golem.
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-03|Session III]] — poison-dusted by [[hebbock/npcs/gri-the-asht-chewer|Gri]]; returns hauling ten three-pound pouches of brain cane.
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-04|Session IV]] — names the defilement; abstains from the drug; turns on the party.
