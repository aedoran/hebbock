# 🛡️ Sandstone

> "A older human in heavily worn armor" — co-runs the **Chamber of Stars** with [[zazi/npcs/zing|Zing]]. Appears only in Session 8.

## In play
- Welcomes the party out of the pyramid's star-portal: "We are glad you made it out. **Welcome to a chamber of stars.**"
- "We know who you are." (Khortum: "… Oh good, that will save some time.")
- Suppresses the party's panic — "Our panic and fear has been repressed. Our natural reactions has been controlled." (Why? How? Open question.)
- Shows them flickering images: **metal flying ships, beams of light, pyramids.**
- Sends them back: "This will lead right back to the inn Nuzul in which you began. **Find Hazi. Give him the map.** Things will be different, but go now. **We'll see you in the future.**"
- Stays behind — "they're not going, but the party has to go" (Session 8).

## Open questions
- Who *is* Sandstone — soldier, timekeeper, agent of **Enoreth** (god of time)?
- What are the metal flying ships? (A technology? A prophecy? Another world?)
- What is the relationship between the Chamber of Stars and [[zazi/locations/pyramid-of-tiberesh|the pyramid]] — the portal appeared *where the entrance was*, after the mummy's death.
- "We'll see you in the future" — when, and for what?
