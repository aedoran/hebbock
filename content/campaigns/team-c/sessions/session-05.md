# 📓 Session 5 — But cats don't like water.

**Date (as written):** 9/13/25 · **Previous:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-04|Session 4]] · **Next:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-06|Session 6]]

## Beats
- **Opening epigraph:** Di: "What is the self, really?" — a Buddhist cat held in stasis: "An illusion." (lol)
- **Lachma's silent return:** out of "the dark silence of a mouse meld" (he was left in the canopic room, Session 4), into the main room with the sarcophagi — "healing and killing are to be balanced"; the room is full of beetle and gnoll corpses.
- The party considers the Pride Room's details: the cat-people's "aura of a death cult"; a moment of "silence and inaction."
- **The scarab wall** (south):
  - Left: **Yellow, Blue, Red** · Center: **Violet** · Right: **Green, Purple, Orange**
  - Torch colors (for reference): **Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Indigo, Violet**
  - Lachma theorizes the pool reflections connect to the scarabs; **tosses water from his flask onto the purple scarab — nothing happens.**
  - A corridor also leads south from the west wall (Bishop's inspection).
- **The granite block:** Di "attempts to leave the room bravely" — a **massive granite block falls from the ceiling**; Di's **left leg (below the knee) gets stuck**; the block drops across the doorway, stranding Di on the other side.
  - Rescue: the party circles around via the map; the hall to Di has **three archways to the west** and a sickly smell; Di's leg and the floor under it are **oiled**; he uses his **halberd as a lever** — "On 3 or on 3 and go?" — and the halberd **completely snaps (into a battleaxe!)**, but Di is saved.
  - "Bimbubop will bless you" — **8 hit points restored** (Lachma). SCOUT is relieved.
- **The de-organing room** (50 ft across):
  - Two **granite slabs** and a pillar dominate; **canopic jars with varied iron instruments**; the pungent smell of "blood and opened bodies and entrails"; mild rot; bloodstains splatter the floor.
  - **Lachma's medicine:** "from a religious point of view, this chamber is **used to prepare humanoids for mummification**."
- **The painted golden door** (south wall):
  - Painted gold (not actual), inlaid with a **mosaic of lapis-lazuli scarabs and a jade serpent**; a **heavy brass lock**; normal-colored torches on the walls.
  - "A door. And it is a door."
  - Bishop's very good trap search: none.
  - **Bishop picks the lock — "easily pops open." SCOUT activates GUARD MODE.**
  - Bishop slides out most of the mosaic — "at auction... a conservative estimate, **around 40 gold pieces**, and obviously the insurance value upwards of 50 gold pieces... thank you for coming to Antiquities Roadshow."
- **The [[zazi/items/lever-of-the-dead|Lever of the Dead]]** — under a table lip, with the face of **Anubis**, "a jackal-headed god in Egyptian mythology who leads the dead to judgment." Lachma: "We found something!... This is very nearly an analog of Hermes. **It's a lever of the dead.**"
- **The pulls:**
  - Di's observation that "the organs must still be recent" → Khortum's confused surmise that "maybe the pyramid has traveled through time" (only to confuse himself). "The Master says, 'Slow thoughts are often the best thoughts.' SCOUT does not understand slow."
  - Bishop draws a bow and hides. **"FUCK IT, WE PULL!"** — first pull: granite rumbling from where the block dropped on Di; "there is likely a connection, depending on whose passive perception you ask."
  - The block has **reset** (slid back). Bishop looks down the hall. "FUCK IT, WE PULL AGAIN!" — **the slab comes crashing down.** "This is the worst slot machine."
- **The basin room** (40×50 ft):
  - A large **circular basin** (20 ft across, "like Paris Hilton, not that deep") used for "liquids of some sort"; two **rusted, vibrant-green-stained protrusions** from the east wall; a floor "riddled with pock marks and tiny craters."
  - A **tattered, decayed zebra rug** in the north, with a **large urn** resting upon it; footprints near the rug.
  - "When it comes to these pipes, something may have been hung."
  - Di is "confused by the zebra rug, perhaps confused by his recent trauma, in defiance of the mastery of mind and body."
  - Khortum holds the door; SCOUT covers the other room.
  - **Bishop examines the urn and realizes there is *something* under the zebra rug** — he pulls the rug out from under the urn to reveal **a trapdoor**, "and it leads to the edge of a cliff from which we hang until next time!"
