# Session I — *It's Not a Wishing Well*

*July 5th*

## Recap
[[hebbock/locations/hargate|Hargate]] is a fading mountain town in [[hebbock/worldbuilding/hebbock|Hebbock]], its glory days as host of the **Goat Goat Festival** — a yearly tournament to crown the finest goat in all the world — long behind it. Three strangers take rooms in the same inn: [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/baelson|Baelson]], convinced the festival fronts a criminal underworld; [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/vlix-boggs|Vlix]], treating herself to a stay at the tavern that just employed her; and [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/randelfine-carter|Randælfine]], who followed a week of signs and portents up from the deep.

That night, all three dream the same counting dream.

They wake to screaming: a gnome mother wails for her boy **Pang**, who walked off into the delve in his nightshirt — the latest in a plague of dream-driven disappearances. [[hebbock/npcs/brother-hethrin|Brother Hethrin]] and [[hebbock/npcs/sister-maugra|Sister Maugra]] of the [[hebbock/factions/vault-of-dumathoin|Vault of Dumathoin]] insert themselves between the party and the widow with a proposition: a generation ago their holy man [[hebbock/npcs/cororic|Cororic]] vanished into the Underdark below Hargate; now something down there calls townsfolk in his voice. They will mount an expedition and bring him home, kindly.

Motives align imperfectly but sufficiently: Baelson smells injustice, Vlix wants out of town, Randælfine wants to be present when the nightmares arrive. In the tavern where Vlix grew up, the party hires two svirfneblin guides — [[hebbock/npcs/vesque|Vesque]] and young [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/pim|Pim]] — and descends.

## Key Events
- The threshold benediction: Randælfine's "Conjure your greatest nightmare and hold fast to it — for it shall be the most friendly thing we encounter." Baelson: "My greatest nightmare is a world of injustice." Randælfine: "...That we will find."
- The tunnels turn magnetic — weapons tug to the walls, compasses useless, Pim has lost the way.
- Randælfine's telepathic whispers set the dwarves against the gnomes; Vlix seals the fractured peace with *Friends* and a touch of makeup. ("Sometimes you catch more flies with honey.")
- **The golem fight:** two headless magnetite-and-scrap golems tear free of the tunnel walls. Baelson's mace strikes home and *sticks* to the first golem's magnetic hide — the Night Angel wrestles his own weapon while Maugra is battered down with internal injuries. Vlix's *Witch Bolt* disrupts the creatures' humming cores; Hethrin's *Guiding Bolt* collapses the first golem. The second stops humming, reverses polarity, and **detonates**, peppering Baelson with shrapnel.
- In the debris: Cororic's compass (needle spinning uselessly) and a metal-bound notebook in kuo-toa scratch-script. One phrase, repeated over and over:
  > *"It's not a wishing well. It's a question."*
- **Vesque is found dead** — opened by claws no person possesses — while a hopeful gnomish voice calls *"I found the way!"* from ahead. Vlix reads the tracks: beasts only. No gnome had come this way. The voice was a lure.
- Two pale, clawed things burst from the dark. Baelson steps forward to meet them.

*And somewhere below, something was still counting.*
