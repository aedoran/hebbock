# Vesque

> A svirfneblin guide hired in [[hebbock/locations/hargate|Hargate]] — first to die. Opened by claws no person possesses, in the dark of the [[hebbock/locations/hargate-delve|Hargate Delve]].

## Overview
Vesque was one of the two svirfneblin guides the party hired (with young [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/pim|Pim]]) to lead the descent. She lost the way in the magnetic tunnels. The party found her dead — opened by claws no person possesses — while a hopeful gnomish voice called *"I found the way!"* from ahead. Vlix read the tracks: beasts only. No gnome had come this way. The voice was a [[hebbock/creatures/miscallers|miscaller]]'s lure.

## Details
- Race: svirfneblin
- Role: guide, Hargate delve
- State: **deceased** (Session I)

## Relationships
