# 🌀 Leylines of the Mountains

> Wind, magic, and memory — the ridge-lines of the mountain region of [[hebbock/worldbuilding/hebbock|Hebbock]] are threaded with something older than the [[zazi/factions/vontir-empire|Vontir Empire]]. *From the Team B notes; the mechanics are deliberately vague — a mystery by design until aedoran rules.*

## What the notes establish
- A **leyline runs along the Eastern Ridge** — the party meditates in the top of the [[mountains/locations/kobold-air-tower|kobold air tower]] and soars along it, "traveling along the ridge… zipping above the landscape" (Session 14).
- **Orenstein's map of Hebbock** (from the [[mountains/locations/blackrock-peak|Blackrock Peak watchtower]]) is used to guide the flight along the leyline.
- The [[mountains/factions/order-of-uncarved-block|Order of the Uncarved Block]] built the towers to harness wind — their "Winds of Balance" practice (rise with the sun, descend with the moon) sounds like a reading of the same currents.
- The [[mountains/locations/yin-yang-tomb|yin-yang tomb]] itself sits where "two ridges converge" — and a **yin-yang flying carpet** carries the party between the tower and [[mountains/locations/vryrrk-basta-caverns|Vryrrk Basta Caverns]] ("a day away" by air, ten hours by carpet).
- The Order's library claims the order "descends from the ancients, predates science, myriad moons ago."

## What remains unknown
- What *is* the leyline — wind, magic, or something else?
- How many run through the region, and do they connect to the **leyline scroll** found in the kobold tower's metal safe?
- Does the world heart (Hargate) have leylines of its own?
