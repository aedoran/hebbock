# 🎲 Session 18 — Giant Toads

After working out the wind, the mummified monk, and the five points in the ground, the party moves deeper — and a giant toad decides to be a party.

## What Happened

- After pondering the air gusts, the mummified monk, and the five points in the ground, the party moves deeper into the cave. Sir and Buckley lead. They "hop" down and find a giant toad.
- The toad ignores Sir's offering of a ration, but when she goes to retrieve it, the giant toad attacks! She fights back, and the fight is on: Xuanzang / Xohn brings the torch forward and throws darts; Orenstein Alabaster lands a huge eldritch blast, chunking out a part of the toad's rubbery, repulsive head. Kim the sprite (Orenstein's familiar) goes invisible.
- The toad's gross tongue latches onto Sir, reeling her into its poisonous maw. A second toad enters the fray, plopping in next to Kim.
- Buckley's thunderwave damages the toad but it stays in place; he also grants the party temporary hit points. Orenstein blasts it again; Xuanzang / Xohn kicks the toad, and it kicks back! Xuanzang / Xohn's torch is extinguished, and Kim's new audience of five disappears in the darkness. The toad resists being ensmallened by Buckley's magic.
- Sir chops and kills the toad, and Xuanzang / Xohn is released from his prison inside the toad's mouth — Xuanzang / Xohn is grateful. Sir flies down with the spirit of an eagle, yelling "MORE FROGS!" The rest of the party is confused in the darkness.
- The frog-reptile people begin to surround Sir. Buckley jumps down and wheels around for optimal breath weapon placement: four are trapped in the range of a lightning bolt springing from Buckley's mouth. Sir strikes one of them down, looping around with the grace of an eagle to put herself between Buckley and the frog-reptile creatures.
- The frog-reptile-men unleash a flurry of furious attacks, severely damaging Sir and targeting Buckley's liver.
- Kim moves the torch to where Orenstein is granted vision. Orenstein casts Phantasmal Force. Xuanzang / Xohn leaps down, runs up, and the GOAT strikes down two of the frog-reptile people — "it's kicks galore for these stinky foes!"
- The battle rages: Sir and Xuanzang / Xohn are dropping foes; Buckley is deftly staying out of harm's way, subjecting foes to emotional rollercoasters and trauma triggers; Orenstein's illusions beguile foes. Despite being armed with shortswords, the foes find themselves overwhelmed. Three have fled; the rest are bleeding out.
- Xuanzang / Xohn has been hit again and is fading fast. Buckley comes in with a huge healing spell, bringing Xuanzang / Xohn to full — only for another frog-reptile to stab Xuanzang / Xohn once more.
- "With time having run out on our players, the battle ends on a cliffhanger!"
