# 🏔️ Mountains

> The mountain region around [[mountains/locations/stonehaven|Stonehaven]] — a cold high place of peaks, watchtowers, and something older than the [[zazi/factions/vontir-empire|Vontir Empire]]. Churned from the Team B campaign notes (9/1/2024 – 3/16/2025, 20 sessions).

## Where it sits
The notes place these mountains in [[hebbock/worldbuilding/hebbock|Hebbock]], but their exact relation to the Hargate heartland and to the [[zazi/locations/zazi|Zazi]] continent is **not yet established** — see [[_questions|Open Questions]]. The [[mountains/locations/kobold-air-tower|Eastern Ridge air tower]] sits on the "eastern ridge"; Stonehaven is a full day's march from it.

## What the region holds
- **[[mountains/locations/stonehaven|Stonehaven]]** — the nearest town: the Mountain's Respite inn, Gilda the drunk, elder Eamon Stonehearth, and the for-profit mountain prison (warden Grimbold, dead).
- **[[mountains/locations/blackrock-peak|Blackrock Peak watchtower]]** — a crumbling relic of the old [[zazi/factions/vontir-empire|Vontir Empire]] built by the [[mountains/factions/order-of-uncarved-block|Order of the Uncarved Block]]; haunted by the ghost of [[mountains/npcs/vigo-dunaedin|Vigo Dunaedin]]; home to the High Elven leaf treasure and the pentagram shrine.
- **[[mountains/locations/kobold-air-tower|The kobold air tower]]** — the Order's eastern-ridge tower, now held by kobolds and urds; the "Winds of Balance," the wind dials, the metal safe, and the yin-yang flying carpet.
- **[[mountains/locations/vryrrk-basta-caverns|Vryrrk Basta Caverns]]** — a hidden dragonborn community ("Dragontown?"), home to priest [[mountains/npcs/thrallax|Thrallax]] and the library where Yongo the Younger's manuscript was found.
- **[[mountains/locations/yin-yang-tomb|The yin-yang tomb]]** — a cave of yin-yang symbols where the notes end on a long rest (3/16/25), before the bearded face.
- **[[mountains/worldbuilding/leylines|Leylines]]** — the ridge is threaded with a leyline the party learned to fly.

## Powers & threads
- The **[[mountains/factions/order-of-uncarved-block|Order of the Uncarved Block]]** — balance, inner peace, "return to nature" — built the towers and sent its wayward monk [[campaigns/team-b/characters/xuanzang|Xuanzang]] on a quest.
- **[[mountains/npcs/rhiltannaxis|Rhiltannäxis]]** — a red dragon, cleric of a fire drake deity of Bahamut — waits, by the notes' account, at the kobold tower.
- **The traitor of Astogrolluu** — Vigo's brother, the root of the High Elven betrayal, and the mark on Xuanzang's circlet.
