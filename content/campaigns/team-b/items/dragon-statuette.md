# 🐲 Dragon statuette

> A dragon figurine — later inventoried as "a small wooden figurine that was a crude dragon" (Session 20). The most cursed trinket the party ever owned.

## Provenance
- One of three treasures — with 4 flasks of pink bubbly liquid and a helmeted monkey — that **[[mountains/npcs/eric-the-idle|Eric the Idle]]** exchanged for half the party's rations (Session 12, 1/25/2025).

## What it does
- When [[campaigns/team-b/characters/thora|Thora]] touches it, she **disappears in a flash of blue-green flame** (S12).
- Sir touches it in reckless pursuit — nothing. Sir covers her hands in kobold blood (draconic blood might trigger it — the plan) — nothing. Sir drinks the kobold blood — no help. The statuette comes back "an absolutely bloody mess of a Sirdragon."
- [[campaigns/team-b/characters/orenstein-alabaster|Orenstein]] presents it to [[campaigns/team-b/characters/buckley|Buckley]] at the tower (S13).
- **Thrallax accepts it as payment** for the spell casting and material cost to reverse the Boomer curse (S16) — Sir's letting go of it was "the last connection to Thora."
