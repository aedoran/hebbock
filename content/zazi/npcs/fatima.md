# 🍵 Fatima

> The "average serving wench" of the [[zazi/locations/nuzul-inn|Nuzul Inn]] who serves [[campaigns/team-c/characters/di-guan|Di Guan]] his ceremonial tea — and, per the notes, "a professional wench." (Session 1)

## In play
- Presents the menu; delivers a **steak and yams** (Di's table has "bustling trade routes" running through it — the notes' running joke).
- Di's standing request: **"Di Guan asks to be made fat."**
- She approaches Di's table when SCOUT recruits the fighter — "what a crowded table of adventure" (Session 1).
- In the past era, Di asks "Is Fatima here?" — referring to "the woman who he flirted with in the beginning" (Session 9). Whether this is *the same* Fatima twenty years younger, or a different woman with the same name, is not established.

## Open questions
- Is she the same person in both eras? (If time is a loop, she should be older now — but the past is ~20 years *earlier*, so the present Fatima is the older one; the question is whether her name/role persists.)
- Any role beyond the tavern?
