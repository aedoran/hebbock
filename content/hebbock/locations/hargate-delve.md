# The Hargate Delve

> The Underdark below [[hebbock/locations/hargate|Hargate]] — where [[hebbock/npcs/cororic|Cororic]] vanished a generation ago, and where something patient and very old is counting the living.

## Geography
A deep system below Hargate. Its upper tunnels turn **magnetic** — weapons tug toward the walls, compasses spin uselessly, and the guides lose the way. It descends into the [[hebbock/locations/bioluminescent-gorge|Bioluminescent Gorge]] and, at the gorge's floor, into the [[hebbock/locations/cathedral-of-dumathoin|Cathedral of Dumathoin]].

## Notable Features
- **Magnetic upper tunnels** — the delve's first hazard.
- **Miscaller halls** — where the eyeless ventriloquists hunt (see [[hebbock/creatures/miscallers|miscallers]]).
- **The kuo-toa scratch-script** — a metal-bound notebook written in it was found in the golem debris.

## Inhabitants & Powers
- [[hebbock/creatures/miscallers|Miscallers]] — the eyeless horrors that throw other voices.
- [[hebbock/creatures/magnetite-golems|Magnetite golems]] — headless constructs that tear free of the tunnel walls.
- Kuo-toa, goblins, svirfneblin, and the [[hebbock/npcs/the-witness|Witness]]'s choir all hold the deep.

## Current State
**Defiled and dangerous.** The [[hebbock/locations/cathedral-of-dumathoin|Cathedral of Dumathoin]] at its heart has been desecrated, and the [[hebbock/npcs/the-witness|Witness]] is counting from within.
