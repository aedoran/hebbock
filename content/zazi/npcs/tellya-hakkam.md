# 📖 Tellya Hakkam

> A **scholar** — brown hair, human — found dead **by the River of Sand** in the past era; the body (and the quest) of [[zazi/npcs/karima|Karima]]'s commission. **Deceased** (off-page).

## Name variants (open question)
- **Tellya Inasec Hakkam** — Karima's full naming (Session 10)
- "**His name is Hakam**" — Karima's own correction, "force the shells off a little bit" (Session 10)
- Which is canonical? (see [[_questions|Open Questions]])

## Description (from Karima, Session 10)
- Brown robe **with stars on the shoulder**; **gold necklace with a sun medallion**; brown hair; human.

## His research
- "Studying **Bastet**, deciphering texts" (Karima, Session 10).
- His satchel held: **30 platinum pieces, 3 jet stones, a scroll with a cat symbol, and a battered parchment fragment** — the **Bastet rhyme** (full text in [[zazi/locations/charnel-house#the-job-sessions-10-12|Charnel House]]).
- His mouth was **full of sand**; **sooty handprints — too small to be gnoll prints — inside and out on the satchel**.

## The parchment (a fragment of a larger piece)
> …He would then be wise to show his obedience to Bastet and crawl backward from her sight
> A wise man would crawl away, averting his eyes from her holiness and hunger and might
> Nineteen karams back he should crawl
> Then in offering to the goddess, cast a karam of rope into the river with a prayer
> That done, he should offer one karam to the goddess of the river in a stout knot about his stake, the stoutest his strength can bear
> For the goddess is fickle and hungry and aware
> Make the stake cast a shadow across half its length from the noon's shadow upon the river's western shore before securing it with all his might
> Casting the rope into the river with a prayer, he should take courage and descend in thought
> That secrets below are his to be caught.

A set of instructions: crawl, rope, stake, shadow, descent — "secrets below are his to be caught." Unsolved.
