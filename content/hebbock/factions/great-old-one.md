# The Great Old One

> An eldritch power of the deep — known in Hebbock as **Cthulhu**. The patron of [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/randelfine-carter|Randælfine Carter]], whose grand design is to aim the Great Old One's hunger at Lolth herself and watch the [[hebbock/factions/matriarchy-of-the-deep-cities|matriarchy]] burn.

## Purpose & Goals
- Devouring. Insofar as madness has design, the goal is the consumption of all that lives — and Randælfine has pointed that hunger specifically at the deep cities.

## Leadership
- The Great Old One itself — Cthulhu.

## Notable Pacts
- **Randælfine** — a mad male drow who crawled out of the deep-cities dungeons and into the Great Old One's embrace. His design: let the deep cities be devoured.

- See [[hebbock/worldbuilding/great-old-one-cosmology|The Great Old One (cosmology)]] — where it sits in Hebbock's cosmology, and whether it is one of many or the only deep power.

## Involvement in Campaigns
