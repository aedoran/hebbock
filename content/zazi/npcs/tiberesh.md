# 📜 Tiberesh

> "The prophet and self-proclaimed god-king" whose hubris ancient legends blame for [[zazi/locations/anu-asir|Anu-Asir]]'s fall and its plagues. His mummified body — and something else — waited in the alabaster sarcophagus at the heart of the [[zazi/locations/pyramid-of-tiberesh|Pyramid of Tiberesh]]. **Killed** (or unmade) in Session 7.

## As described
- Scholars "claim this pyramid is the burial place" of Tiberesh — a claim the party's expedition confirms and complicate.
- **Little is known** about him (Session 1 setting text). Di recognizes the causeway alcoves — "naked men sacrificing themselves" — as "a hallmark of the god Tiberesh" (Session 2).
- Galena calls him "a cruel ruler who sought power" (Session 1).
- Bishop's insight, drawn in the sand: **the strength of the river (Nuria) and the strength of Tiberesh are connected** (Session 2).
- The [[zazi/worldbuilding/pantheon|fish-headed god]] dominates the pyramid's exterior — dagger in the left hand, curved rod in the right. Khortum believes the feline-embossed bronze chest "is related to an ancient god or religion."

## The tomb (Session 7)
- 80-ft chamber, south turn, sand drifts, two braziers, translucent alabaster panels, a platform and table.
- The party's entry ignites the doorway into flame; the room begins filling with sand; the lid slides off; a human-like figure wrapped in wrappings emerges from **roiling green smoke**:
  > "I am reborn! I will turn this world upside-down on your behalf!"
- Combat: the mummy draws **healing light from the sarcophagus**; **skeletal warriors and a skeletal archer** rise from the sand; Bishop's psychic sliver fails; it toasts Lachma's bug-infestation ("mere bugs").
- Khortum's challenge: **"Face the avatar of the Lost One!"** — i.e., the mummy is (or carries) an **avatar of the [[zazi/worldbuilding/lost-one|Lost One]]**, the Loxodon god. (Is Tiberesh *the* Lost One, or a corrupt vessel of it? **Open question** — see [[_questions|Open Questions]].)
- The kill: Bishop spots, inside the open sarcophagus, **a blackened, glowing mummified hand**. He **stabs it** — the alabaster walls rupture "with giant lightning cracks," platter-sized holes appear, the mummy roars and collapses, the skeletons fall, and **sand pours in like water**.
- Escape: the party forces the locked **golden doors** on the far side (Bishop "slides the latching mechanism... cool as a cucumber"), beats the sand, and reunites over the gypsosphinx (see [[zazi/locations/pyramid-of-tiberesh|Pyramid of Tiberesh]]).

## Threads
- The **blackened hand** — whose? Tiberesh's, or something wearing Tiberesh? Where did it go when the room flooded?
- The "false prophet" of Anu-Asir's "magical decimation" — is that Tiberesh, or someone before him?
- The four ceiling sarcophagi — **Xerx, Phi, Tosanath, Baelxi** (beetle swarms, ancient coins, empty) — who are they? Court? Priests? Rivals?

## Open questions
- Is the mummy Tiberesh himself?
- What is the fish-headed god, and what is its relationship to Tiberesh?
- What exactly is the river–Tiberesh connection (Bishop's sand drawing)?
