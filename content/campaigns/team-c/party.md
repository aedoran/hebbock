## The party as an organism (Sessions 1–13)
- **Formation (Session 1):** Di arrives by boat, orders ceremonial tea, asks "to be made fat." Khortum rescues the pickpocket (alias "Tongueless") from his own trouble; SCOUT recruits Di; "the avengers assemble."
- **The comm chain (Sessions 9–11):** the party's defining logistics joke — "Lachma is talking on behalf of a mouse, Bishop is telepathically communicating with SCOUT and Di, and Khortum talks slow as an elephant does." Working solution: SCOUT → Bishop (telepathy) → Di (telepathy) → the party (loud).
- **Bishop's parrot** — present in the present era (squawks "Cheater!" at Hazi; *flies away the moment the contract is signed*); the parrot is *also* seen at the start of the charnel-house fight (Session 10, "Also, a parrot flies out") — the same bird, or a replacement? (Open question.)
- **Bimbubop** is the sixth voice in the tent — technically Lachma's, canonically the party's.

## Contract & obligations
- Relics (magical/religious) → GFA(C). 50% mundane → GFA(C). Maps → GFA(C) — which is how [[campaigns/team-c/characters/scout|SCOUT]] ends up carrying the pyramid map, and how the party owes young [[zazi/npcs/hazi-sucineri|Hazi]] one in the past.
- The party's *one adventure* is the pyramid; the time displacement turns it into two.
