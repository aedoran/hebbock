# The Great Old One (Cosmology)

> The deep's hunger, named **Cthulhu** in Hebbock. Where it sits in the world's cosmology, and what it wants.

## Details
- **What it is:** an eldritch power of the deep — "a black hole the mind slid off of." Confirmed **separate from** [[hebbock/npcs/the-witness|the Witness]] (aedoran's ruling): Cthulhu's hunger and the Witness's count are different things.
- **What it wants:** to devour. Insofar as madness has design, the design is consumption — and [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/randelfine-carter|Randælfine]] has aimed that hunger specifically at the [[hebbock/factions/matriarchy-of-the-deep-cities|deep cities]], at Lolth herself.
- **Its channels:** Randælfine's warlock patronage; the **Arms of Hadar** re-flavored as the **Arms of Cthulhu** — black tentacles.
- **Its relationship to the matriarchy:** Randælfine was imprisoned and tortured by the matriarchy; his design is to have the deep cities devoured in return. The matriarchy's name in Hebbock is implied to be Lolth.
