# 🎲 Session 7 — Goat Company

Two days of recovery, a subtle change in Xohn, a "high five" over tubers — and the [[campaigns/team-b/party|party]] smashes the animated armor: "We won!! Woot woot woot."

## What Happened

- After two days of recovery, on the third day, the party returns to the tower.
- [[campaigns/team-b/characters/sir|Sir]] recognizes a subtle change in [[campaigns/team-b/characters/xuanzang|Xohn]]: he has the "hunter's attunement."
- Sir and [[campaigns/team-b/characters/thora|Thora]] go a-huntin' and "high five" each other. They find tubers.
- The base, noted: it's elevated, near a spring, goats everywhere (and one GOAT: Xohn). They have "high air tubers," which they call "smokin' a toobie." (as written)
- The plan: strong people flank the door, open the door, let one out, close the door.
- The manticore carcass. "Ew." Sir finds something, "a flash of something": a dart with a carved character. "ORENSTEIN KNOWS THINGS." (as written) The character means "acting seal" — theater! Not the singer Seal. "A kiss on the rose on the grave." (as written)
- The dart seems like a puzzle piece… "or is that just Orenstein?" (as written)
- Before opening the door, the party brainstorms a group name. No decision, but "Goat Company" (or "GOAT's Guardian") is "an acceptable placeholder."
- The weather is nice: a high pressure system from the coast, low dew point, low humidity.
- The party makes it back to the door on the second floor — the armory. Thora steps into the doorway and thinks "WTF did I get myself into."
- A morning star attacks Thora. Battle ensues: the morning star is destroyed, and banging starts on the door and the walls above. "Uhoh. They are breaking down the door slowly."
- A magical suit of armor runs out of the door. Xohn tries to attack but cannot get through the armor ("hah").
- Sir's reckless attack on the suit succeeds — serious damage. Xohn lands mighty blows with "kick galore" — "He's the GOAT."
- Thora "bigly smited" the suit of animated armor; it collapses into a pile of useless metal, clasps and buckles.
- Sir charges in, goading the armor; Xohn runs through its legs into a flanking position and lands a successful headbutt. Thora and [[campaigns/team-b/characters/orenstein-alabaster|Orenstein]] move up. ("Be best," Orenstein tells the group.) Sir clears line of sight for Orenstein's devastating eldritch blast (as written — note the warlock flavor, which conflicts with the session 1 header's "human wizard"; flagged in [[_questions|Open Questions]]).
- Thora grips her warhammer with both hands and crushes the animated armor in a hail of sparks and clattering.
- We won!! Woot woot woot.

## Loot & Finds

- Tubers, and "high air tubers" ("smokin' a toobie," as written).
- A dart with a carved character from the manticore carcass; the character means "acting seal" — theater, not the singer Seal; "a kiss on the rose on the grave" (as written). It seems like a puzzle piece… or is that just Orenstein?

## World Changes

- The second-floor armory is cleared: morning star destroyed, animated armor reduced to useless metal.
- Xohn has the "hunter's attunement."
- The party's working name: "Goat Company" (or "GOAT's Guardian") — an acceptable placeholder, no decision made.
