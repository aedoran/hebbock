# The Counting Dark

> *A Chronicle of the Hargate Delve — Sessions I–IV.* The party descends into the deep to bring home [[hebbock/npcs/cororic|Cororic]] and finds instead the [[hebbock/locations/cathedral-of-dumathoin|Cathedral of Dumathoin]] — defiled, full of two hundred dead, and watched by an eye that is counting.

## Premise
In the fading town of [[hebbock/locations/hargate|Hargate]], a plague of dream-driven disappearances walks the streets. The [[hebbock/factions/vault-of-dumathoin|Vault of Dumathoin]] has lost a holy man — [[hebbock/npcs/cororic|Cororic]] — a generation ago, and something below now calls the townsfolk in his voice. The party descends to bring him home, kindly. What they find: the deep is a machine. The goblins cook a drug. The kuo-toa sing to an eye. The eye counts the living. And the lost saint smiles at the top of it all, *pleasant*, while a small boy is almost done.

**The counting dream** (shared by all three original party members, Session I):

> "You feel a faint wrong light behind your eyes, the color of nothing — and under it a voice, patient, kind, and very old. It is counting. It reaches a number. It pauses. And in that pause, you understand, the way you only understand things in dreams: it is counting people. And it has not yet counted you."
