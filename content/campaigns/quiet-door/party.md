## How They Met
All three original members took rooms in the same Hargate inn on the night of the shared counting-dream — each for their own reasons: [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/baelson|Baelson]] hunting an imagined criminal underworld, [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/vlix-boggs|Vlix]] treating herself to a stay at the tavern that just employed her, and [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/randelfine-carter|Randælfine]] following a week of portents up from the deep. The [[hebbock/factions/vault-of-dumathoin|Vault of Dumathoin]]'s clerics, [[hebbock/npcs/brother-hethrin|Brother Hethrin]] and [[hebbock/npcs/sister-maugra|Sister Maugra]] formalized the party's purpose: bring [[hebbock/npcs/cororic|Cororic]] home.

[[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/pim|Pim]] found the party in the [[hebbock/locations/bioluminescent-gorge|gorge]] in Session III, having shadowed them in the dark, and chose to stay.

## Dynamic
- **Baelson** is the furnace — loud, certain, and perpetually one step into trouble. His "justice" is a weapon and a blinder in equal measure.
- **Vlix** is the grease — charm, insults, and a pomegranate's double nature. She smooths every edge and sees through every lie (including Baelson's).
- **Randælfine** is the shadow — he manipulates everyone, trusts no one, and is delighted by what everyone fears. The party's most dangerous member, to the party.
- **Pim** is the witness and the bridge — she moves between the surface and the deep, and is quietly fascinated by Baelson's intensity.
