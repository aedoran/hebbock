# 🐉 Thora

> A dragonborn paladin — her light is **green-blue** (where [[campaigns/team-b/characters/buckley|Buckley]]'s is blue-green). The party's voice, its healer-knowledge, and its missing member.

## Who
- **The vision:** in the Mountain's Respite (Session 1) Thora has a vision of the mountain prison — *rusty gates, packed in old snow… strange noises and lights* — and begins chatting with the bar patrons about it.
- **The tarot:** the town healer's cards read for her: "a soothing balm… a wise ruler who roams the land."
- **The quest:** Thora knows that **High Priest [[mountains/npcs/thrallax|Thrallux/Thrallax]], Cleric of Bahamut**, can cast the spell to return Orenstein to his natural age — and that [[mountains/npcs/rhiltannaxis|Rhiltannäxis]], the red dragon, waits at the [[mountains/locations/kobold-air-tower|kobold air tower]]: **fight him** is the quest the notes give her.
- **The language:** her dragonborn language skills let her read kobold intent (the sacrifice for the dragon, Session 10).
- **Kin:** [[mountains/npcs/argin-noroxious|Argin Noroxious]] greets the party at [[mountains/locations/vryrrk-basta-caverns|Vryrrk Basta Caverns]] because [[campaigns/team-b/characters/sir|Sir]] introduces herself as *a friend of Thora* — the five dragonborn who await the party are, by context, Thora's people.

## In play
- **S2–S5:** senses the red-eyed shadow at the gate; flame breathes the spider out of hiding; is wounded by the manticore's tail thorn; dropped, stabilized, and carried by Sir.
- **S7:** the high-five with Sir after the hunt.
- **S12:** **touches the [[campaigns/team-b/items/dragon-statuette|dragon statuette]] and disappears in a flash of blue-green flame.** Sir picks up the statuette in reckless pursuit — nothing happens.
- **S13–S20:** missing. The statuette was given to Thrallax as payment, and Sir's letting go of it was "the last connection to Thora." Where Thora is — and whether the statuette's flash is the key — is the campaign's great open thread. See [[_questions|Open Questions]].
