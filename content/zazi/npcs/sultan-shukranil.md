# 👑 Sultan Shukranil Mo Shator

> "The sultan" of the [[zazi/locations/charnel-house|charnel house]] on Bashtoor Street — a **cannibal** who runs a corpse incineration service he does not actually fulfill. **Dead** (killed by the party, Session 11).

## Name variants (open question)
- **Sultan Shuk-ri Nill Mo Chatoor** — Karima's phrasing (Session 10)
- **Sultan Shukranil Mo Shator** — the narration's (Session 10)
- The notes use both; which is canonical? (see [[_questions|Open Questions]])

## Who he is
- A gnoll of "hints of madness... a manic smile... cackling" (Session 10–11).
- "Cunning... he'll stop at nothing to protect his interests" (Karima, Session 10).
- Runs the charnel house with **two robed assistants** (poorly concealed meat cleavers + spice pouches) who **eat the corpses they are paid to incinerate** (confirmed by Di's telepathy and the party's later search — Session 10, 12).
- "No one interrupts feeding time, you have no idea who you're messing with." (Session 10)
- Hates [[zazi/npcs/karima|Karima]] and "will do anything to hurt her" (Session 10).

## In play (Sessions 10–11) — the end
- The "crazy ass plan" goes wrong: [[campaigns/team-c/characters/lachma|Lachma]]-as-donkey flips out; a grieving young woman (the **green-smoke woman**) is seen at the door; combat.
- The Sultan's abilities: a **ghostly skeletal hand** (summoned, fist-grabs Di), **necrotic life-drain** (Di's shoulder "feels old and leather"), shrugs off **Hold Person** twice, **ice and stone** spell, and at the climax turns his "skin and hair into **transparent stone**," leaps to a chandelier, and rages.
- His end: Lachma's **Thunderwave** ragdolls him off the chandelier into the firepit — "dead upon the ground with a broken body... like a hot dog on a grill." (Session 11)
- On his body: **two keys** (large, small). The small one opens the stained-glass door to his private rooms (bed, library, shrine, "museum" — a mummy holds a **hooked blade with gold trim**; Session 11–12).
