# 🎲 Session 3 — Xohn's Kick Galore

Donnie the bard contributes a (heteronormative) song, the unseen foe gets kicked in the junk, and the giant spider earns a critical hit: "Xohn's kick galore!"

## What Happened

- Donnie's (heteronormative) song about Sir: "Oh, hear ye, hear ye, a tale to unfurl / Sir's not a sir, but a half-orc girl! With axe and shield, she's fierce and bold, / A barbarian's heart, in feminine mold. / Strength and fury in her veins do flow, / Her gender matters not in battle's glow. / So let us cheer for Sir, the brave, / A warrior queen, no damsel to save! / Emojis can't capture her might and grace, / But picture an orc with a lady's face. / In Stonehaven's tale, she'll play her part, / A half-orc heroine to warm our heart!"
- Sir perceives a shadowy foe; [[campaigns/team-b/characters/thora|Thora]] lands a mighty blow against the unseen foe.
- [[campaigns/team-b/characters/orenstein-alabaster|Orenstein]] is hit by a mass of sticky web.
- [[campaigns/team-b/characters/xuanzang|Xohn]] successfully kicks the foe in the junk!
- Sir is impressed by her new companions, who "can fight pretty well."
- Thora is hit in the chest and falls, but is not unconscious — "This could go either way." Sir pummels the foe in "a shocking display of barbarity and brutality," then crosses to free Orenstein from the web. Orenstein panics a bit, squeaking a little; Thora's second death save succeeds (natural 20, gains 1 HP).
- Sir gets stuck in web too, then breaks out; Sir wonders at Orenstein's weakness and brings the axe high. Orenstein looks at the blade's gleam in terror — and finds Sir "vaguely attractive" as she stands over him.
- Xohn's darts at the spider: a miss — "damnit." Then: critical hit! Xohn used "Xohn's kick galore!" (as written).
- The spider takes significant damage; Sir charges and misses; Orenstein is right in front of it, shoots, and misses… Thora lays on hands and restores 5 HP.
- The giant spider scuttles out the back door into the dusk light. "Cue Final Fantasy video game music."
- The party finds a recent skeleton in the spiderwebs. Beyond the partition was formerly a barracks.
- "We participate in democracy, and Sir is very confused." The party camps in the tower for fitful rest as "the screams of the damned emanate from the cursed tower."
- During Xohn's watch, a cloud of flying creatures descends from the tower onto the camp. An unpleasant night after winning the battle.
- Orenstein wants the new "base" called "Rumsfield" (the tower: "Halls of Burton?").

## World Changes

- The giant spider retreats out the back door but is not destroyed.
- The party establishes a base of operations in/around the tower.

## Notes on Xohn — [[mountains/factions/order-of-uncarved-block|the Order of the Uncarved Block]]

- Some version of the Order built the tower "many, many years ago"; started by veterans of the orc purging war and the fall of the [[zazi/factions/vontir-empire|Vontir Empire]] half a millennia ago. The order has changed since, and its genealogy is "shrouded in mystery (of course)."
- Purpose: find inner peace to reflect what the order sees as balance in the world; achieving that peace is meant to allow the order to "return to nature" — "(there's definitely a death drive thing going on)."
- Xohn broke some rule and was sent on a quest to find that balance again. He doesn't know how.
- A branch/subset of the order goes undercover and spies on other organizations; Xohn doesn't know much about it.

## Sir's background

- Sir was left on the doorstep of a hut in a small village. A brusque nature — surprising even to the unwashed peasants — and she eventually outgrew the village, wandering the land on hunting and odd jobs for just enough coin. It wasn't until she entered a lowly tavern one day that her life of adventure began.
