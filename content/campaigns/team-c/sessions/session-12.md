# 📓 Session 12 — Sandstorm by Darude

**Date (as written):** 3/15/26 · **Previous:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-11|Session 11]] · **Next:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-13|Session 13]]

## Beats
- **The jackaloid fight concludes** (the [[zazi/npcs/sultan-shukranil|Anubis jackaloid]] of the [[zazi/locations/charnel-house|charnel house]]):
  - The creature is surrounded by a **whirling sandstorm**; [[campaigns/team-c/characters/khortum-fil|Khortum]] **fails his Strength check and is restrained**, struggling with his warhammer against the wind and sand.
  - **[[campaigns/team-c/characters/di-guan|Di]]** (outside the sand's range) hits twice with the halberd — "the jackal-man drops, as does the sand effect."
  - "Di poses over the monster as a delta male."
  - [[campaigns/team-c/characters/lachma|Lachma]] gives Khortum "his last healing blessing."
- **The library (catalogued):**
  - Di had been looking over the **spellbook** — takes it to study later.
  - **Illustrated treaties about embalming.**
  - **A gold-trimmed map of the Southlands** on a table.
  - **A selection of poetry in a book with an alligator-hide cover** and gold-and-obsidian buckle — Lachma deduces "alligators may be common, and perhaps not even surprising, in the sewers of the city."
  - Lachma invites Khortum to look at the regional map; Khortum: "I am from even further away. It took me a long time to get here…" — "oh, that is a time-travel joke."
- **SCOUT is missing:**
  - Around lunchtime: **Bishop cannot communicate with SCOUT.**
  - Di asks his **hawk familiar Peng** to search the city — "Peng is overwhelmed by the bustle of the city and the multitude of delicious pigeons."
  - The party is in the **[[zazi/locations/district-of-the-hyena|District of the Hyena]]** (northeast district, gnoll areas of control); they have time to return to the [[zazi/locations/nuzul-inn|Nuzul Inn]] with Hazi, but "the party has taken a beating and needs to rest."
  - They are near where [[zazi/npcs/karima|Karima]] wanted to meet them — "a short trip down the Jackal's Run road by the pyramid."
  - **Lachma offers his ass-istence** carrying Tellya's body to the meeting point.
  - Khortum props his body against the main door to rest; **Di and Lachma deep-search the downstairs** — "nothing more of significance" — but the search **confirms the gnolls were eating the bodies they were meant to incinerate.**
  - **One item of treasure is held by a mummy in the museum room: a hooked blade with gold trim.**
- **The spellbook:** **18 spells; 1 hour of study per spell** to decipher. Lachma prays to "a makeshift shrine of Bimbubop placed upon a bed." Bishop moves out to search for SCOUT.
- **Bishop's search (Peng as air support):**
  - SCOUT's last known location: **just outside the charnel house**; SCOUT "had been trying to communicate to Bishop something about an immense danger, help is required."
  - **A city block away:** a shred of brown cloth — "may have come from SCOUT's large cloak"; **footprints indicating a scuffle**; "every sign points to SCOUT having been **abducted or taken against his will**."
  - Bishop shadows the area to overhear the conversation about the scuffle — the gnolls speak "in the gnoll language and common language." "**Witnesses didn't see shit.**"
  - (Bishop has a hard time getting the door open — "mostly because he is relying on Di to communicate to Khortum to stop guarding the door. It is painful.")
- **The party searches for SCOUT:** exits and locks the charnel house; Bishop leads to the scuffle scene; Khortum: "it must have been a fast, chaotic scene"; "not much of a trail to follow... **this abduction was carried out by experts.**"
- **To Hazi:** the party approaches Hazi at his lunch table; the job is "fine, but one of our friends is in trouble." They offer the map — **Hazi's eyes widen in excitement.**
  - Khortum's description: friend abducted "a few hours ago in the district of the hyena, one block from the charnel house... about this tall... wore a brown robe"; apologizes for not revealing the friend's existence.
  - Hazi agrees to help via his contacts; **suggests contacting Karima** — and the party deduces **Karima is his contact in the district.**
  - "Hazi is disappointed he cannot get the map now." (He never got it — it was given in Session 9; the offer here is a repeat or a different map? Ambiguous in the notes — open question.)
- **Karima's tent (again):** Karima and her entourage are absent; the **second-in-command (Karima Jr?)** is in charge.
  - Di asks where Karima is — **ignored, then rebuffed more forcefully.** "Di sizes up the crowd and realizes he cannot force his way through to an answer."
  - Khortum asks when she returns — **"tomorrow."** The party leaves.
- **The session's closing deduction:**
  - Khortum wonders who would take SCOUT; forms the idea that **the woman from the charnel house** (Session 10) "may have information regarding the disappearance of SCOUT."
  - "Who does she represent? Was it her that disappeared into green smoke?"
  - Di recalls: low-cut dress; "an attractive woman with a green necklace and jewelry." **Peng had not seen where the green smoke went.** "Around the time of the green smoke, is when the connection with SCOUT was lost."
  - The conversation reconstructed: the woman was **to pick up the corpse of her uncle**; the Sultan **asked for proof**; an exchange/argument; the woman disappeared.
  - "It is 3 PM, and the party has until dawn to meet Karima."

## Loot (secured)
