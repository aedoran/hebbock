# 🌍 Zazi — World Overview

> The setting of the [[campaigns/team-c/README|Team C]] campaign: a desert world of a cat-city, a sunken ancient city, a burial pyramid, and a war of wizards and warforged on the northern continent.

*Everything here is sourced from the Team C session notes (2025-05-31 → 2026-05-17, as written). Where the notes are thin, it says so.*

## Geography (as known)
- **The Gulf of Daafia** — a sea; the city of [[zazi/locations/zazi|Zazi]] sits on it.
- **[[zazi/locations/zazi|Zazi, "City of Cats"]]** — a semi-autonomous city, loosely aligned with the western [[zazi/factions/vontir-empire|Vontir Empire]]. Multicultural; in the past era (~20 years before the "present") the cat-folk [[zazi/creatures/nkosi|Nkosi]] are a much larger share of the population.
- **[[zazi/locations/anu-asir|Anu-Asir]]** — a newly surfaced ancient city just outside Zazi (south). Once thought to exist only in myth; it lay buried under sand, floodwater, and tall tales. Its population was supposedly destroyed by a "magical decimation" brought by a **false prophet**. Now a hub of salvagers, treasure-hunters, and shady interests, ringed by a tent city.
- **[[zazi/locations/pyramid-of-tiberesh|Pyramid of Tiberesh]]** — south of Anu-Asir, across the **River Nuria**, reached by a narrow limestone causeway. Tomb of the prophet / self-proclaimed god-king [[zazi/npcs/tiberesh|Tiberesh]], whose "hubris" is blamed for the city's fall and its plagues.
- **The River Nuria** — the river between Anu-Asir and the pyramid. Bishop's insight (Session 2, drawn in the sand): **the strength of the river and the strength of Tiberesh are connected.**
- **The River of Sand** — near the charnel house and the "great sand pyramid"; where [[zazi/npcs/tellya-hakkam|Tellya Hakkam]]'s body was found.
- **The Southlands** — a region; a gold-trimmed map of it sits in the charnel house library.
- **Northern continent (northwest)** — the seat of the [[zazi/worldbuilding/northern-war|Northern War]]: magical fire rains, charred landscapes, warforged rebelling against their wizard-masters.
- **The Loxodon island kingdom** — a growing island power ("even further away" than the Southlands, per Khortum). Home to the [[zazi/factions/loxodon-kingdom|Loxodons]] and the god they call the [[zazi/worldbuilding/lost-one|Lost One]].

## History (as known)
- **~450–500 years before the past era:** the people of [[campaigns/team-c/characters/di-guan|Di Guan]] repelled the Vontir Empire's "soft encroachment" and were never conquered. One of Di's great ancestors was a hero of that battle (name TBD — open question).
- **Ancient era:** Anu-Asir flourished, then was destroyed — plague and "magical decimation" attributed to [[zazi/npcs/tiberesh|Tiberesh]]; the city sank beneath the sand.
- **The present (Sessions 1–8, 2025):** Anu-Asir surfaces; the [[zazi/factions/golden-falcon-antiquities|GFA(C)]] organizes tomb-salvage; the pyramid of Tiberesh is entered for the first time.
- **The past (Sessions 8–14, ~20 years earlier):** the party is displaced in time by the **Chamber of Stars** (run by [[zazi/npcs/sandstone|Sandstone]] and [[zazi/npcs/zing|Zing]]). In this era: the Nkosi are common, the [[zazi/factions/loxodon-kingdom|Loxodons]] are rising, the [[zazi/worldbuilding/lost-one|Lost One]] is a living god with a real temple, the warforged of the Northern War are newly forged and rebelling, and the party's old allies don't exist yet — the party meets a *younger* [[zazi/npcs/hazi-sucineri|Hazi]].

## The Chamber of Stars
A 50-foot cylinder room with star-field walls and a portal into a swirling foreign sky. It showed the party flickering images of **metal flying ships** and pyramids, then sent them back to [[zazi/locations/nuzul-inn|Nuzul Inn]] in the past with one instruction: **find Hazi, give him the map. We'll see you in the future.** Open questions: what is the chamber, who built it, what are the metal ships? (see [[_questions|Open Questions]])

## Religion
See [[zazi/worldbuilding/pantheon|the gods of Zazi]] — the notes name **Bastet** (cat goddess), **Anubis** (jackal-headed judge of the dead, "an analog of Hermes" per Lachma), **Enoreth** (god of time, a constellation in the star-portal sky), an unnamed **fish-headed god** (reliefs, alabaster, the anglerfish doors), and the [[zazi/worldbuilding/lost-one|Lost One]] of the Loxodons. Djinn are rumored.

⚠️ The notes describe Anubis as "a jackal-headed god in **Egyptian mythology**" — whether this world's gods are *literally* Egyptian, or a homegrown Egyptian-flavored pantheon, is an open question.
