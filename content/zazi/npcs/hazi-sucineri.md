# 🧢 Hazi Sucineri

> A man who appears in **two eras at once**: a bald, warm, handsome middle-aged head of the [[zazi/factions/golden-falcon-antiquities|GFA(C))] in the present (Sessions 1–8), and a young man with **long brown hair** — "hair waving dramatically," obnoxiously performative — in the past (Sessions 9–12), still building the antiquities business the party will help him found.

## Present era
- "A bald, warm man... handsome too, and smiling. A man of many details." Doesn't seem to care for people in line; his **gnoll bodyguards** "consult the rulebooks" — one has a **scorpion on a leash**. (Session 1)
- Runs the pyramid contract: relics to the GFA(C), 50% of mundane treasure, maps drawn by the party, one adventure (Session 1).
- A **"news junkie" ("only NewsMax")** who tracks the [[zazi/worldbuilding/northern-war|Northern War]] (Session 9 — the party's source on world events).
- Bishop's parrot squawks **"Cheater!"** at the sight of him (Session 1) — Bishop's professional thief instincts flag something. In the past, Bishop "is not disturbed by the age" but notes he "can trust [young] Hazi now, but couldn't trust the old Hazi" (Session 9).
- His contacts reach into the [[zazi/locations/district-of-the-hyena|District of the Hyena]] — **Karima is his contact there** (Session 12).

## Past era
- First met in the back-left corner of the [[zazi/locations/nuzul-inn|Nuzul Inn]] with long brown hair, robes, maps, a flask, a pint (Session 9).
- Wants **maps** to "start a treasure hunting business"; believes the current chaos is an opportunity to make money (Session 9).
- Introduces the party to [[zazi/npcs/karima|Karima]], his gnoll "good friend" who "needs help for complicated reasons" (Session 9).
- Shows Di "the process of how to deal with magic" (Session 9).
- Asked pointedly, "You know one of these creatures that have been forged for the war?" — he notices something about SCOUT (Session 9).
- Hazi is *disappointed* he cannot get the map now in Session 12 — he knows exactly what the map is worth.

## Threads
- **The map.** The party gave young Hazi the pyramid map from the future, "because Sandstone and Zing wanted to give you [it]." Why *him*? What did he do with it? The present-era Hazi's GFA(C) exists because of what the party set in motion — a closed time loop, or something else? (Open question.)
- Why does old Hazi not remember any of this? Time displacement presumably never registered on him.
- "Tongueless" — Bishop's alias in the tavern (Session 1) — see [[campaigns/team-c/characters/bishop|Bishop]] and the open question.
