# 🎲 Session 19 — Frog-Reptiles

The frog-reptile battle ends, the cave gets a lot stinkier, and the party finds a backpack full of loot — and architecture by, as the notes put it, "Frank Lloyd Wright."

## What Happened

- HAHA! Xohn kicks and kills a frog-reptile.
- Xohn is severely hurt after being attacked (as written "attached") by a frog-reptile and clawed.
- Buckley "cure wounds" (as written) on Xohn — he is cured. Whew!
- Sir strikes the trog (as written) in the chest with her axe, and Orenstein Alabaster "shoots toads in a barrel" with Eldritch Blast.
- The laughing trog (as written) is hit by Xohn, stops laughing, stands up, and is put down by Sir and Xohn.
- Buckley sneaks ahead to scout the cavernous drop. It extends down beyond his darkvision; wind rises up from the drop, an ominous sound indicating large airflows.
- Sir discovers a symbol/rune on a short sword after investigation. Orenstein Alabaster decodes it as "venomous fang."
- The party makes its way around the deep pit and finds a stinking bog with trickling water and humanoid bones in the distance. Orenstein Alabaster casts a spell to fetch a skull; his casting draws energy from the far realm, gives him a ghastly glow, and channels death itself. The skull has been eaten — brains slurped out — and it was some type of elf!
- A backpack of loot: 3 climber's kits; 3 silk ropes; 6 gems; a scroll tube; 2 potions; a pouch of strange coins with a hole in the middle; a gold plaque (worth 50); 12 leather straps with coins of silver (50 sp each).
- A spider coin! A spider on one side, a web on the other.
- The party moves around the deep pit and finds deliberate architecture — "by Frank Lloyd Wright."
- A fish clops along down the hall, wielding a claw-topped staff. It sees the party and attacks! But the party drives it away — leaving everyone wondering where it came from and who built this and why (as written).

## Loot & Finds

- Backpack: 3 climber's kits, 3 silk ropes, 6 gems, scroll tube, 2 potions, pouch of strange holed coins, gold plaque (50), 12 leather straps with silver (50 sp each).
- The spider coin (spider on one side, web on the other).
- A short sword bearing a rune Orenstein Alabaster decodes as "venomous fang."
- A human bone bog with an elf skull — brains slurped.
- Deliberate, deliberate architecture throughout the deep cave.
