# Randælfine Carter

> A mad male drow, criminal-born, imprisoned and tortured by the matriarchy of the deep cities, who crawled out of the dungeons and into the embrace of [[hebbock/factions/great-old-one|Cthulhu]] — aiming the Great Old One's hunger at Lolth herself and waiting for the matriarchy to burn.

## Overview
Randælfine is the party's shadow and its most dangerous member — to the party. His grand design, insofar as madness has design, is cosmic: let the deep cities be devoured. He speaks in a syllabic giggle that is almost a language, dances forever at the border where torchlight dies into shadow, and whispers uninvited into the minds of anyone within thirty feet. He is delighted by everything you fear.

## Session IV Features
At-will *False Life* (a half-minute of gibbering, gesturing, and sprinkled spirits, once an hour, forever); the **Arms of Hadar**, re-flavored as the **Arms of Cthulhu** — black tentacles.

## Appearance
Male drow. Undulates every limb at a different frequency. Moves in arrhythmic shadows.

## Personality
- Feeds on fear; respects cleverness ("Game recognizes game").
- A master of deception played for the table's delight: the "squeeze out the magic" speech to the kuo-toa; the ice-cream-truck jingle Vlix added underneath.
- Keeps secrets from the party: saw [[hebbock/npcs/cororic|Cororic]] through the [[hebbock/items/eye-stone|Eye Stone]] and announced it was fake *aloud*, while whispering "Don't trust the dwarves" *silently* to every party member.
- When the choir's vision seared his mind and he learned the eye belongs to [[hebbock/npcs/the-witness|The Witness]] — "the drow let the whiskey take that one."
- Breakdances. Moonwalks. Once, out of a hold spell, on a sixteen.
