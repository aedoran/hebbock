# 🧺 Galena Ealamassi

> The **traveling saleswoman** who runs the supply tent outside [[zazi/locations/anu-asir|Anu-Asir]]. (Session 1)

## In play
- Sells rations, armor, "mundane goods... all this can be had for a price. **This is not a GFA(C) company store.**"
- Has "experience running a supply shop alongside the [[zazi/factions/golden-falcon-antiquities|GFA(C)]]" — and her parting advice: **do not cross the GFA(C).**
- The adventurers around her have "seeds in their appearance" (the notes' phrasing).

## Threads
- Her warning is the only explicit caution about the GFA(C)'s "shady dealings and non-payments" rumors — a small data point for [[zazi/factions/golden-falcon-antiquities|the firm's]] reputation.
