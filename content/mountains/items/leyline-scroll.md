# 📜 Leyline scroll

> An **Old [[zazi/factions/vontir-empire|Vontir]] "Ley Line navigation scroll"** found in the metal safe of the [[mountains/locations/kobold-air-tower|kobold air tower]] (Session 14, 2/8/2025). The full text is quoted in [[campaigns/team-b/sessions/session-14|Session 14]].

## What it is
- A leather scroll tube from the safe (alongside a corked vial of pearly white liquid and 20 gp in old Vontir coinage).
- A map/scroll for traveling the **[[mountains/worldbuilding/leylines|leylines]]** — the same ridge the party learned to fly on the yin-yang carpet.

## In play
- Xohn's own scroll is in the same (unnamed) language; the Vryrrk Basta library manuscript is too — the Old Vontir knowledge thread is closing. What the pearly-white vial does is unknown. See [[_questions|Open Questions]].
