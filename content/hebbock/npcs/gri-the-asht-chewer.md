# Gri the Asht-Chewer

> A goblin shaman, cooker of [[hebbock/items/brain-cane|brain cane]], at large. The robed mutterer over colored mushrooms by the fire at the [[hebbock/locations/silt-pool|Silt Pool]].

## Overview
Gri is the shaman of the Silt Pool camp — the one who turns the dredged silt into the drug the party now carries three-pound bags of. When the Battle of the Silt Pool broke, Gri poison-dusted the dwarves and fled into the dark. He is the most dangerous person the party has met who is still *free*.

## Details
- Race: goblin
- Role: shaman, Silt Pool
- State: **at large** (fled Session III, poison-dusted and furious)
