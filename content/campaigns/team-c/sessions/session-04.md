# 📓 Session 4 — This is good shit.

**Date (as written):** 8/30/25 · **Previous:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-03|Session 3]] · **Next:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-05|Session 5]]

## Beats
- **The bronze chest** ([[campaigns/team-c/characters/scout|SCOUT]] and [[campaigns/team-c/characters/di-guan|Di]] guard the doorways; [[campaigns/team-c/characters/khortum-fil|Khortum]] and [[campaigns/team-c/characters/bishop|Bishop]] investigate):
  - Bishop opens it: **inlaid with red velvet, embossed with the face of a feline.**
  - Khortum: "the cat is related to an ancient god or religion."
  - The party **marks the chest's location on the map.**
- **The ceiling sarcophagi** (Bishop searches for traps; "looks clean"):
  - **Top-left sarcophagus — the slab is pushed off: it ERUPTS with a swarm of beetles.** "We don't know how many beetles, but they are a semi-organic whole that can be attacked. Like a hive mind, but each one probably has its own political opinions."
  - Bishop's dagger: **6 damage**. A second attack "passes through the swarm." The swarm is enraged (though "who would know the thoughts and feelings of an insect?").
  - **The swarm lands a critical** on Bishop — "a weak spot in all his cloth armor! Was Bishop distracted by tickling scurry?" — "massive welts."
  - Di strikes true; the swarm dissipates.
  - **A second swarm under the NE sarcophagus lid** — "a little bit horrifying, so we are a little bit horrified." Di's halberd strikes true; the swarm moves on Di — **7 damage** ("his scale mail is ineffective").
  - Bishop's "plan to sit on the sarcophagus" = a front-row seat; he "leaps into killing action, after winning an unfought battle of running away."
  - Bishop's **mind sliver into the hive mind** — "the minds of many foes"; the beetles "have no comprehension of psychic abilities" and, in panic, keep attacking Di.
  - Di's blade and butt; "Di must have killed beetles in a past life, as he does in the present life, and the beetles move on from their lives." "The room is now silent as a tomb again. The beetle-y spirits of the departed are at rest once more."
- **Tosanath's sarcophagus** (southwest): Bishop listens — "dry, dusty air that is not flowing." Di opens it: **coins! Ancient.** "Bishop has a history of stealing money, and this money is worth money. Maybe it's a valuable to a cartographer on another continent."
- **Phi's sarcophagus:** opened. "**No beetles. No Beatles. No treasure.**" "Pretty boring. It's still a dry, dusty tomb."
- **The canopic storage room** (through the southern door):
  - Lower ceiling; piled to the top with **canopic jars and wooden crates**; the room is harder to discern ("taking longer to discern... half as deep in inches, content and entertainment").
  - **11 decorative bronze boxes** — "a style we've seen before, in the coins. **The ancestors have not been well respected**, Di observes."
  - Contents: **20 vials of mystery liquid, evaporated.** Estimate: "conservatively, might be worth **5 gold pieces**. But in the hands of the right buyer, this could fetch even more."
  - "So far, we have busted into this pyramid and have stripped it of copper. This is meth country."
  - **Lachma is left behind** "in the security of a dead-end room full of wooden crates and weird organ jars; he should be fine and suffer no alarm upon waking."
- **The march north:** the party discusses using walls as shields "and general ancient defense strategies." Khortum leads the marching order — shield and hooded lantern.
- **The Pride Room** (single-file entry; it "opens up much wider"):
  - **7 magical torches**, each a different color — **Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Indigo, Violet.**
  - A **shallow pool** abutting the north wall; the torches' reflections make a **kaleidoscope of colors** around the room.
  - **Larger-than-life carvings** spanning the pyramid's width: **"Falcon-headed men slay lions"** ("as cat and birds are natural enemies"). Servants pour water into the pool.
  - **Three bronze lion statues** surround the pool.
  - A **10-foot table** depicted with **multicolor scarabs.**
  - A **metallic door on the east wall, rusted-iron lock.**
  - **A sarcophagus to the west.**
  - Dimensions: **50 ft long, 100 ft wide at its widest.** "The atmosphere is magical, less gloomy, more fabulous. This room is full of life or death."
- **Inspection round:**
  - Khortum is curious about the water ("which could give life in a barren desert").
  - **Di ponders whether the falcon-headed men are connected to the GFA(C)** — "Khortum and SCOUT are ready." (Unresolved.)
  - Bishop's attention is "drawn to anything shiny or maybe full of treasure"; SCOUT is sent to the locked door.
  - **Di's focus on the falcon-headed men — and his ARCANE INSIGHT: the carvings are ILLUSIONS.** "We have been deceived! What exists there instead are **nkosi**! Cat like creatures, in status, weapons at the ready."
  - Di: "**Sorcerous shenanigans!**"
  - Bishop, who had been inspecting the sarcophagus and confirming no traps, hides behind it. Di summons all to his side "to inspect the lion."
- **Break for the end of the session** — "left with the mystery of sorcerous shenanigans."
