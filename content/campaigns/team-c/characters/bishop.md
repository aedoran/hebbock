# 🎩 Bishop

> **Desert elf rogue.** Mute — with a parrot on his shoulder who says **"Cheater!"** (CAST OF CHARACTERS). In the past era, he gains **telepathy** and can speak — from the mind.

## Known
- **Professional thief** — "cool as a cucumber," "very luscious lips" (revealed when he drops his scarf for SCOUT), a lockpicker ("the lock easily pops open"), dagger-and-shortbow fighter, sneak-attack specialist, "twists the knife... and rolls for PAIN."
- **The parrot** — foils his pickpocketing in Session 1; *flies away the moment the GFA(C) contract is signed* (Session 1); a parrot flies out at the start of the charnel-house fight (Session 10) — same bird or replacement? (Open question.)
- **Telepathy (past era only):** "Bishop speaks! From the mind! Psychic whispers." His **psychic knives / mind slivers** "affect creatures with a psyche/mind" only — "My mind stabs their mind"; no effect on inanimate objects (the dart-board glass test, Session 9). Used on gnolls (2 psychic, "too dumb to die"), the beetle hive mind (mental horror), cats, and warforged ("scouring its positronic brain").
- **Marine biology:** knows ocean life, "because one time he went to the docks of a port city" (Session 7, anglerfish doors).
- Trust read on Hazi: can trust *young* Hazi; *couldn't* trust the old one (Session 9) — the parrot's "Cheater!" may be a professional hunch (or a time-loop echo — unconfirmed).
- In the present, mute; in the past, a talker (telepathically). Why the difference is unexplained — open question.

## Key moments
- **Session 1:** the pickpocket recruited by Khortum; "the avengers assemble."
- **Session 2:** his "insight" — drawn in the sand: **the strength of the river ↔ the strength of Tiberesh**; opens the twin golden doors where Di and Khortum fail.
- **Session 4:** the dagger vs. the first beetle swarm (6 damage, then a critical welts); the mind sliver into the hive mind.
- **Session 5:** pops the brass lock; slides out the lapis/jade mosaic (≈40 gp); "FUCK IT, WE PULL" ×2 on the [[zazi/items/lever-of-the-dead|Lever of the Dead]].
- **Session 6:** the two-finger orange-scarab mistake (six cats wake); lights the remaining scarabs (Yellow, Green, Blue, Violet); the "rainbow is lit" door opens.
- **Session 7:** the kill-stab on the **blackened mummified hand**; "cool as a cucumber" through the sand flood; forces the locked golden doors open.
- **Session 8:** "my mind stabs their mind" era begins.
- **Session 11:** "throwing psychic blades at the Sultan's balls, straight up, no chaser" — the yip.
- **Session 13:** the ambush; the flawless dismount ("even the East German judge agrees"); mindknives drop two warforged; kills a third; retreats to the [[zazi/locations/charnel-house|charnel house]].
