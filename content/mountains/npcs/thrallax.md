# ⛪ Thrallax

> A **venerable priest** of the dragonborn community in [[mountains/locations/vryrrk-basta-caverns|Vryrrk Basta Caverns]] — **Cleric of Bahamut**. One of the five dragonborn who await the party (Session 16, 2/22/2025).

## Who
- The notes spell his name two ways — **Thrallax** and **High Priest Thrallux**; both forms are in the record.
- The one who can cast the spell (a **greater restoration**, by Thora's naming) to lift [[campaigns/team-b/characters/orenstein-alabaster|Orenstein]]'s Boomer curse and return him to his natural age.

## In play
- Thrallax **accepts the [[campaigns/team-b/items/dragon-statuette|dragon statuette]] as payment** for the spell casting and material cost — which [[campaigns/team-b/characters/sir|Sir]] talks herself into being okay with, because "it was the last connection to [[campaigns/team-b/characters/thora|Thora]]." Whether the spell has been cast is not yet in the notes — see [[_questions|Open Questions]].
