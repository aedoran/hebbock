# Magnetite Golems

> Headless constructs of magnetite and scrap that tear themselves free of the tunnel walls of the [[hebbock/locations/hargate-delve|Hargate Delve]] — and *detonate* when they die.

## Biology / Nature
- Construct: magnetite and scrap, assembled from the delve itself.
- **Magnetic hide** — metal weapons strike home and *stick fast* (Baelson's mace was lost to the first one; he was left wrestling his own weapon).
- **Humming core** — a sustained arc (Vlix's *Witch Bolt*) disrupts it; the golems hum until the end.
- **Polarity reversal on death** — the second golem stopped humming, reversed polarity, and *detonated*, peppering [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/baelson|Baelson]] with shrapnel.

## Habitat
- The magnetic upper tunnels of the Hargate Delve, where weapons are pulled toward the walls.

## Behavior
- They stand up from the walls when the party passes. Two at once. An "ugly, grinding fight."

## Threat
- High for a level-1 party: the magnetism neutralizes steel, and the death-detonation is area damage.

## Notable Encounters
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-01|Session I]] — the party's first real battle; [[hebbock/npcs/sister-maugra|Sister Maugra]] was battered to the ground with internal injuries. Cororic's compass and notebook were found in the debris.
