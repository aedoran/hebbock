# 🎲 Session 5 — The Spider Falls

A succulent goat with rainbow sprinkles lures the giant spider into the open — where [[campaigns/team-b/characters/thora|Thora]] and [[campaigns/team-b/characters/xuanzang|Xohn]] "absolutely smash it to death."

## What Happened

- The party returns to the tower. [[campaigns/team-b/characters/orenstein-alabaster|Orenstein]] summons the silent image of a succulent goat entering with rainbow sprinkles. The goat illusion works, provoking the giant spider into attacking and revealing its position.
- Xohn (AKA GOAT) runs but does not see the spider. Neither does [[campaigns/team-b/characters/sir|Sir]], but Orenstein uses a silent image spell to point out the spider's location.
- Thora bursts the area with flame breath! Xohn throws a dart! Sir throws an ax but fails — ax ends up on the ground.
- Orenstein listens to Sir and closes the door. When the spider tries to retreat, it finds itself trapped with Thora and Xohn, who absolutely smash it to death. Victory!
- Xohn finds a secret door; behind it Orenstein finds a weapon cache. Sir and Thora take longbows, which are tested and work well — they have weathered the years. No quivers, but arrows are abundant.
- A body of a dark-skinned elf is found, drained of its fluids, encased in spiderwebs. There is a book that Orenstein can read: the drow from Menzoberranzan is looking for evidence of the betrayal, and "it is soon time for a ritual. Traitor traitor traitor etc." (as written)
- The party moves up to the second floor. As Sir sticks her head out, she is attacked! Four skeletons shoot arrows at her and miss. There is also a glowing purple horror with white wisps.
- The party smashes the skeletons, but Orenstein is left to fend for himself against the purple spectery ghost. The skeletons are slain, and the party rushes to Orenstein. "With a significant trade off, Sir deals the final blow." (as written)
- In the south of the room, a map of Hebbock is posted with various markings and symbols. Orenstein's madness convinces him he has scrawled down the map correctly, "but he has not at all." (as written)
- Xohn (AKA GOAT GOAT GOAT 🐐) finds a second secret door! It slides open to a set of stairs going up: racks of broken weapons and a chest. Orenstein's mage hand opens the chest — a plaque in Elvish, "some riddle bullshit," and velvet pouches!

## Loot & Finds

- The giant spider, killed.
- Weathered longbows (tested — they work) + abundant arrows, no quivers; Sir and Thora take the longbows.
- The drained body of a dark-skinned elf in spiderwebs, with a readable book: a drow from Menzoberranzan seeking evidence of betrayal; a ritual is near.
- A map of Hebbock with markings and symbols (copied badly, per Orenstein's madness).
- A chest on the upper stairs: an Elvish riddle plaque and four velvet pouches.

## The Elvish Riddle (as written)

> "Four there be, of fates entwined, Wrought by gods, in shadows find.
> One doth hide from watchful eye, In silent steps, unseen dost fly.
> Another's gaze, both sharp and keen, Nary a mark unseen hath been.
> The third in armor born of earth, No blade nor spear can find its worth.
> The last in fortune, fate doth bend, By luck alone their woes amend. Nanu Nanu"
