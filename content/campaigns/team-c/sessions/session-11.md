# 📓 Session 11 — Weapon of Ass Distraction

**Date (as written):** 2/28/26 · **Previous:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-10|Session 10]] · **Next:** [[campaigns/team-c/sessions/session-12|Session 12]]

## Beats
- **The fight begins:** gnolls pursue [[campaigns/team-c/characters/lachma|Lachma]]-as-donkey (the distraction for [[campaigns/team-c/characters/bishop|Bishop]]'s infiltration).
  - Lachma-as-ass "brays and bucks... barely restrained chaos. He is a great ass." He deposits Bishop surreptitiously, runs back to [[campaigns/team-c/characters/khortum-fil|Khortum]] "in a chaotic circle."
  - Bishop pops up in front of the pursuing gnolls; crawls a bit; **springs upon one with psychic knives, "carving into its flesh and soul"** ("we speculate upon Bishop's ethics").
  - The other gnoll hits Bishop quickly; Khortum signals Lachma to resume donkey form; Lachma charges in with Bishop and the gnolls.
- **At the top of the staircase:** [[campaigns/team-c/characters/di-guan|Di]] attacks **[[zazi/npcs/sultan-shukranil|the Sultan]]** — dodges the blade, gets the **butt of the halberd.**
  - The Sultan retaliates with a "guttural incantation," summoning **a ghostly skeletal hand** between himself and Di — a fist that attacks Di; Di feels a chill, casts shield, but the hand still strikes, "assailing and contacting and grabbing" him.
  - The Sultan cackles: "**You will be very tasty to eat!**"
- **Down by the charnel fire:** the second gnoll strikes Khortum.
  - Lachma reverts and casts **Hold Person** — "Nobody touches Di like that!" — but the Sultan **shrugs it off with a cackle** ("refusing the irony of being held by a spell while holding someone with a spell").
  - Bishop "stabby time" — "slicing against into the gnoll's ethereal essence of being, a plane where thought and entity meet. The psychic thread between the gnoll and its astral existence is severed" — the gnoll collapses, "scattering his spices and cleaver."
  - Khortum's warhammer vs. the second gnoll — it's faster; Khortum braces for its counter-attack.
  - Di attempts a second wind — "the skeletal chokes that life out of him."
  - "**Wall not fall!**" — Di "channels the anger of his ancestors" and hits the Sultan with the halberd's blade — the Sultan, "now also aware of Di's proficiency," dodges the butt end.
  - Di's fury, Action Surge — to no avail.
  - "The Sultan stops cackling... The now dour hyena of power, peeks down for a moment, casting another spell to touch Di. A shadowy hand reaches for Di, **sucking his life force out of his shoulder, which now feels old and leather.**"
  - Di swears to his ancestors.
  - The second gnoll turns on Bishop — a wild attack, misses.
  - Lachma: "Di's in trouble!" — tries **Hold Person again — the Sultan's magical channel overpowers it.**
  - Bishop jabs **psychic blades into the gnoll's trapezius muscles** — the gnoll drops to its knees.
  - Khortum positions in front of the gnoll, raises the warhammer, **offers it a chance to repent.**
  - Upstairs, Di "unleashes another flurry of attacks, fighting through the necrotic damage" — the blade hits the Sultan.
  - The Sultan stumbles toward the platform railing, turns, **casts a protective spell — "turning his skin and hair into transparent stone."**
  - With "increasing fury," he climbs the railing and **leaps to the chandelier**, grabbing the chain.
  - "The gnoll below, looking up and beyond Khortum, sees his master take to the skies."
- **Lachma rushes up to heal Di** and "summons from the essence of the fae font of healing, granting additional hit points and temporary hit points. 'Bimbubop blesses you.'"
- **Bishop aims up:** "throwing psychic blades at the Sultan's balls, **straight up, no chaser.**" The balls are hit. "The Sultan yips as a neutered dog and menaces Bishop."
- **Khortum gets his answer:** the gnoll "does not repent but rather spits" — Khortum **smashes the gnoll's head with a divine smite.**
  - Khortum throws a javelin up at the Sultan — "a useless clatter."
  - Di hits the Sultan with the halberd's head, **slides the shaft through his hands**, and follows with a backstroke. "The Sultan howls, cursing in common, French, and Gnoll languages."
  - The Sultan casts again — "furiously hailing down **ice and stone**, absolutely pelting Bishop and Khortum" — and "returns to his cackling ways."
- **The kill:**
  - "Lachma strides to the edge of the railing and casts **Thunderwave** at the Sultan. A loud thunderclap shakes the charnel house, cracking the icy ground and shaking the chandeliers. **The Sultan is blown off the chandelier, ragdolled down into the firepit, landing dead upon the ground with a broken body. The Sultan lays in the fire like a hot dog on a grill.**"
- **Aftermath:**
  - "Khortum emerges from under his shield as the hail subsides. The party has taken a beating."
  - They **put up a closed sign, lock the main door, and throw the gnolls on the fire.**
  - Searching the Sultan's body: **two keys — one large, one small.** Khortum rekindles the fire.
- **The bodies:**
  - Di searches the alcoves: **a white sheet marked "R"** — reeks of rotting flesh. **Second alcove: a white sheet marked "S"** — the body.
  - Lachma's medical exam: **brown hair, white robe, red scarf**; **mouth full of sand**; a satchel; abrasions all over; **sooty handprints on the satchel inside and out — "too small to be gnoll prints."**
  - The third and fourth alcoves: corpses "putrified and marked with an 'R.'"
  - **The corpse has an erection** — "comedic relief in this horrible, somber tomb." Around it: **a money belt — 30 platinum pieces, 3 jet stones, a scroll with a cat symbol, and a battered parchment fragment** (the **Bastet rhyme** — full text on [[zazi/npcs/tellya-hakkam|Tellya Hakkam]]).
  - "Di is going to study the scroll and the parchment. Bishop notes he overheard a rumor about **treasure buried under the palace**. Maybe this money is part of that treasure?"
- **The small key opens the stained-glass door:** a **bed chamber**, a **small library**, and a **collection room ("museum")**.
  - Di in the library: **dark cookbooks** (including ***"To Serve Man"***).
  - Khortum in the third room: **a shrine to death magic — Anubis iconography.**
  - **Under a rug, something burrows** — "**Popping up, an uncanny jackaloid emerges from under the rug, and it is furious.**"
- **The jackaloid fight (begins):**
  - Khortum "calls upon the protection of the Lost One in this dark magic sanctuary" — **Shield of Faith**, warhammer — "triggering a divine smite. Bones crunch."
  - Di from the library: a **fire bolt** "weaving between Bishop and Khortum."
  - **The creature dissolves into sand... and reconstitutes behind Bishop and Khortum.**
  - It lashes out; Khortum's Shield of Faith answers each blow with "**the image of a ramming elephant made of holy light**" (the [[zazi/worldbuilding/lost-one|Lost One]]'s protection).
  - Bishop: "It's brain, not bread!" Di: "Now it's toast!"
  - Lachma runs in, **fully healing Bishop.**
  - Khortum misses; Di "slides up, and double-whiffs!"
  - **From the sandstone floor, a whirlwind of sand engulfs the floor.** "Everything in the sand is heavily obscured. What will the creature do now? What will the characters do now? Are the gnolls finished being burned?"
