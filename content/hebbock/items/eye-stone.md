# The Eye Stone

> An ordinary stone — but on one face, embedded rather than carved, the outline of an open eye. *"You hold it over your eye,"* Nyx said, *"and it lets you see things."*

## Description
A small stone, unremarkable at a glance. One face bears the **outline of an open eye** — embedded in the stone, not carved into it. Hold it over your eye and it lets you see things.

## Properties
- **Shows you death.** [[hebbock/npcs/nyx|Nyx]]'s single use: *someone smiling over his body, right before he died* — the "someone" being [[hebbock/npcs/cororic|Cororic]].
- **Randælfine's use** (Session III, DM slid him a private message): he saw the same — and knew Cororic was "the one from the stone."
- **Vlix's use** (Session III): she held it to her right eye, lowered it slowly, and said only: *"I see what you mean about the dwarves."*
- It may be a **window onto the Witness** — or a product of it. Whether the stone shows *the* future or *a* future is unknown.

## Provenance
- Came "in trade from a fish-person" — the kuo-toa of the [[hebbock/locations/bioluminescent-gorge|gorge]] (see [[hebbock/npcs/glul|Glul]]).
- Given to the party by [[hebbock/npcs/nyx|Nyx]] in exchange for a freedom he completely believed was real.

## Current Owner
- The party; Vlix held it last (Session III). Randælfine announced aloud it was a fake, while whispering "Don't trust the dwarves" to the party — so the stone's true nature is a party-wide open secret.
