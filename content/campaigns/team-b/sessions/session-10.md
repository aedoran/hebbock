# 🎲 Session 10 — Urd Slings

Out of the pit trap and up the hill — a slow, rocky slog under urd sling fire to the door of [[mountains/locations/kobold-air-tower|the kobold air tower]].

## What Happened

- Session opener: “Be yourself. Follow your instincts. Success depends, at least in part, on the ability to 'carry it off'.” — Donald Rumsfeld, returning muse.
- [[campaigns/team-b/characters/xohn|Xohn]] is in a hole trap. The party advances, readying weapons, under fire from urd slings — a slow climb up the steep, rocky hill.
- Battle ensues. Xohn makes it out of the traphole and gets hit a bunch of times. The group successfully kills a bunch of the **little evil dudes** (urds and kobolds).
- The group is crawling up the hill, firing at urds and kobolds — **it is a slog** moving forward.
- After killing all perceived threats, [[campaigns/team-b/characters/sir|Sir]] retrieves her shield from the bottom of the pit, and the party advances around the base of the tower. **They discover the door.**
- Gathered at the door, Sir opens it, steps forward and springs a trap. Her **Danger Sense** lets her jump aside from a net full of heavy rocks — another devious kobold trap! She moves to the stairs down, ready to guard while the party scouts.
- The tower's interior: rickety wooden ladders lead up, and every ten feet a wooden ring lines the edge of the tower.
- Xohn approaches more cautiously and notices three kobolds crouched in waiting in the alcoves of the room.
- [[campaigns/team-b/characters/orenstein-alabaster|Orenstein Alabaster]] kills one, Sir wounds another. Xohn and Thora launch attacks that are more effective as distractions.
- An urd joins the fray, swooping above Thora, dropping rocks.
- Cliffhanger: a new round of initiative brings the intrepid heroes to the edge of a cliff. Will they survive the kobold air tower? Find out next time!

## World Changes

- The party reaches the base of the kobold air tower and fights its way to the front door.
- The tower's defense doctrine is revealed in practice: pit traps, rock nets, sling fire, and urds as flying artillery.
