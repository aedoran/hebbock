# 🏙️ Zazi, City of Cats

> A semi-autonomous city on the **Gulf of Daafia**, loosely aligned with the western [[zazi/factions/vontir-empire|Vontir Empire]. Home of the [[zazi/locations/nuzul-inn|Nuzul Inn]], the [[zazi/locations/district-of-the-hyena|District of the Hyena]], and — in the past era — a thriving Loxodon presence.

*The name "City of Cats" is from the session notes' locations list. The Nkosi connection (cat-folk majority in the past) is inferred from the party's observations — flagged, not ruled.*

## Layout (as the party knows it)
- **The inn quarter** — the [[zazi/locations/nuzul-inn|Nuzul Inn]] stands here. In the past the same building looks *newer*, with a crescent on the sign, and feels "more multicultural."
- **The next quarter over** — where [[zazi/npcs/karima|Karima]] "holds court" in a tent between the city proper and the gnoll district: spices, coffee, hookah smoke, a liminal space.
- **[[zazi/locations/district-of-the-hyena|District of the Hyena]]** — northeast district, gnoll-controlled in various areas; contains the [[zazi/locations/charnel-house|Charnel House]] on Bashtoor Street and the "great sand pyramid."
- **The palace** — somewhere in the city; a rumor (overheard by Bishop) says **treasure is buried under the palace** — possibly connected to the 30 platinum pieces found on Tellya's body.
- **Jackal's Run** — the road by the pyramid; the party meets Karima "at the southeastern tip of the pyramid near Jackal's Run Bridge at dawn."
