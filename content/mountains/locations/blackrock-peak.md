# 🗼 Blackrock Peak watchtower

> A **crumbling relic of the old [[zazi/factions/vontir-empire|Vontir Empire]]**, built "many, many years ago" by the [[mountains/factions/order-of-uncarved-block|Order of the Uncarved Block]]. The party's first and haunted destination (Sessions 2–9), and the source of the [[campaigns/team-b/items/high-elven-leaf-treasure|High Elven leaf treasure]].

## The approach
- A gated entryway of large doors; through them the paladin's sense pings a **shadowed figure whose eyes glow with red malevolence** (Session 2).
- Around the base: the **manticore** that boomed "Ahoy! Get eaten, fuckers!" — its carcass held a **dart with a carved character** ("acting seal"; "A kiss on the rose on the grave" — the language matches the shrine).
- The party camp: elevated, near a spring, goats around, "high air tubers" they call "smokin' a toobie"; camp named **Rumsfield** (a running Rumsfeld joke).

## Inside (as cleared)
- **Giant spider in the ceiling** (killed Session 5 via Orenstein's rainbow-sprinkled goat illusion and the closed-door trap); darts and a critical hit: "Xohn's kick galore!"
- **Skeletons** — "by Xohn Xohn Xohn!" — and an **unseen foe** who gets kicked in the junk (Session 3).
- **Second floor:** a **map of Hebbock** with markings and symbols (Orenstein's madness convinces him he's scrawled it correctly — he has not, at all); a **drow journal** (a drow of Menzoberranzan seeking "the root of the treachery, the hand of the traitor"; "soon time for a ritual"); an armory behind a trap door.
- **Secret door → stairs up:** racks of broken weapons and a **chest** with an Elvish riddle plaque and four velvet pouches (leaf-marked). Lifting a bag with mage hand springs **two suits of armor and four flying swords** — the party's own trap (Sessions 6, 8).
- **Weapon cache:** weathered longbows (tested — they work), abundant arrows, no quivers. Sir and Thora take the longbows.
- **The pentagram shrine:** five plates on steel tracks with embedded gems, a pentagram; weathered beyond recognition; eight quartz crystal gems; a **large bronze bell with a wave pattern**; "**founding stars**" written in the manticore-dart language (Session 8). The party leaves the puzzle unsolved.
- **The parapet, at night:** the ghost of [[mountains/npcs/vigo-dunaedin|Vigo Dunaedin]] appears.

## Loot (as taken)
- The [[campaigns/team-b/items/high-elven-leaf-treasure|High Elven leaf treasure]]: gold ring (Sir's pinky), tortoise amulet (symbol of High Elven King [[mountains/npcs/kalvulaen-viltannen|Kalvulaen Vil'Tannen]]), a golden circlet, three leaf-etched d20s — **Xuanzang's circlet bears the mark of the traitor**.
- The manticore's dart; two longbows and arrows.

## In play
- The watchtower is where the campaign's first act happens and where [[campaigns/team-b/characters/thora|Thora]]'s quest points next: [[mountains/npcs/rhiltannaxis|Rhiltannäxis]], the red dragon, waits (per the notes) at the [[mountains/locations/kobold-air-tower|kobold air tower]] on the eastern ridge — the next tower up from here.
