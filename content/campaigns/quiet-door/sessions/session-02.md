# Session II — *The Vault of Dumathoin*

*July 19th*

## Recap
The clawed things of Session I are named in the blood: **miscallers** — eyeless, four-limbed, ape-quick horrors, pale as drowned flesh, all claw and hiss, who throw other creatures' voices. One had been throwing [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/pim|Pim]]'s, screaming in agony from down the hallway, while it crouched in front of them.

The fight is farcical and brutal by turns. [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/baelson|Baelson]] drops his torch to swing — "These tunnels are a cesspool, and I'm here to clean them!" — misses catastrophically and stomps out his own light. [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/vlix-boggs|Vlix]]'s critical Eldritch Blast drops a miscaller to one knee; [[hebbock/npcs/brother-hethrin|Brother Hethrin]] cracks its spine as it flees. Baelson relights his torch, launches a flying football tackle, grapples the last one to the ground — and there [[hebbock/npcs/sister-maugra|Sister Maugra]]'s *Sacred Flame* descends and smites it dead in his arms. The gods, it seems, do not look kindly on those who touch Sister Maugra.

One miscaller escapes into the dark. Randælfine stokes the furnace — "Pursue the unjust. *Pursue.*" — and Baelson charges blind down a southeasterly tunnel, bellowing, while the rest stroll after at a safe ten feet. The air thickens into a dry, moistureless mist. Baelson rolls a 19 on survival and learns the bitter truth: *the monster had never come this way at all.* "The fog of transgression will not save your soul," he informs the empty fog.

Then the ledge. A thin, treacherous line tracing the northern wall of a vast gorge — the inside of a hollow mountain. Water drips through a cloud-ceiling of mist and bursts into blue biological flashes on the stone, over slopes strewn with patches of bioluminescent fungus: a galaxy of little lights descending into the dark. At the very bottom, built into the living rock, a small cathedral flanked by two giant carved dwarves, its door dimly lit by braziers.

> **Sister Maugra:** "Behold — the Vault of Dumathoin. And that is where Cororic is."

They climb the ledge single file, past pools where impossible mushrooms grow heads out of heads, and the ledge simply ends: a ten-foot gap of sheer wall, eighty feet above the gorge floor. Maugra shoulders Baelson aside — "Let us handle this. We know what to do" — and the dwarves make the traverse with rope. Hethrin slips once, four feet out, catches himself, lands.

A literal cliffhanger. The DM has a World Cup final to attend.

## Key Events
- **The miscaller fight** — ventriloquism revealed; Maugra's smite; one escapes.
- **The mist-deception** — the fugitive was never in that tunnel at all.
- **The Bioluminescent Gorge** — the party's first true wonder of the deep.
- **The traverse** — the dwarves' rite of passage; the party reaches the cathedral threshold.
