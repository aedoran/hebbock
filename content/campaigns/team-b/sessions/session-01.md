# 🎲 Session 1 — A Party of Misfits

A strange collection of characters assembles at the Mountain's Respite in [[mountains/locations/stonehaven|Stonehaven]] and, after some tarot and one very large burp, sets out for the old watchtower.

## What Happened

- The new party assembles: [[campaigns/team-b/characters/sir|Sir]] (half-orc barbarian), [[campaigns/team-b/characters/thora|Thora]] (dragonborn paladin), [[campaigns/team-b/characters/xohn|Xohn]] (halfling monk, "WU-TANG 4EVA"), and [[campaigns/team-b/characters/orenstein-alabaster|Orenstein Alabaster]] (human wizard per the S1 header — later notes say warlock; conflict kept, flagged in [[_questions|Open Questions]]).
- Midsummer, a fortnight before the summer solstice; hot in the mountains. Elk and deer, no bison; horse-sized goats; "trout in the sky, eagles in the rivers, something like that" (as written).
- Stonehaven: a village built into the mountainside, dwarves and half-orcs united in defense against orcs.
- The Mountain's Respite Inn, run by Thraine, a dwarf; solid oak door, weathered. "Why, it is the party! Fancy that."
- Strange noises from Grimbold's tomb — a prison, a plot hook, a piece of the puzzle? "All of the above? None of the above, or maybe some?"
- Thora has a vision: rusty gates packed in old snow, strange noises and lights. She chats it over with bar patrons including Gilda the lady dwarf; Xohn listens very intently; Orenstein butts in asking who Grimbold is.
- The bartender "knows him when he was younger" — it turns out Grimbold is dead (a warden of a for-profit mountain prison; those the weather didn't take went to the prison). Pronoun carelessness noted: "It would be weird if the bartender was dead. (That's a good point.)"
- Orenstein offers drinks to anyone who'll talk; he's "there for fun," and there's some weird flirting between Orenstein and Thora, the dragonborn.
- Gilda is the only one who knows where the prison is, but is not exactly trustworthy — she has a "situational memory" (as written) and delivers the biggest, most disgusting burp of the night, in Orenstein's face. Sir basically threatens Gilda; Gilda falls asleep.
- The bartender names people who might know; Thrain confirms Gilda is basically a drunk. The party threatens to scare children. Orenstein is serious about his investigations — "Orenstein is just very curious dude."
- Tarot from the half-orc town healer: Thora is "a soothing balm for the world, spreading love and warmth. She will find a wise ruler who roams the land. This is the time for her journey." Xohn: "The way is the destination" — said to Orenstein.
- Elder Eamon Stonehearth (Orenstein "talks to Eamonmonmon") brews tea and hears the group is looking for the tomb. He has never seen it, points out Gilda is the town drunk, and gives directions: find the three peaks coming together, follow the footpath, and come upon the watchtower.
- Sir is relieved the plan exists; Sir is also very concerned about getting her gold. Sir and Xohn have a "kind of an odd couple thing developing"; Orenstein is "kind of obsessed with how weird this party that's forming" is.
- They climb the ridgeline into the center peak. The [[mountains/locations/blackrock-peak|Blackrock Peak watchtower]] is old and crumbling, built of the stones of the peak, with a double iron-reinforced gate. The paladin senses an evil being at the end of the room past the stairs. They go in. "We kill!"

## World Changes

- The party commits to the mission and leaves Stonehaven for the [[mountains/locations/blackrock-peak|Blackrock Peak watchtower]].
