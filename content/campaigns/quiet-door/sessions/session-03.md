# Session III — *Squeeze Out the Magic*

*July 26th*

## Recap
The party crosses the rope one by one and presses along the ridge — 120 feet above a firelit pool — when out of the shadows drops [[campaigns/quiet-door/characters/pim|Pim]], sticking the landing with a nineteen and a courteous bow. The guide who "parted ways" in the tunnels has been shadowing the deep: goblins at the pool below; two [[hebbock/npcs/ulu|kuo-toa]] hidden in an alcove ahead. The kuo-toa know the party is there. The goblins do not.

Randælfine, hinged at the waist, feeds the kuo-toa leader **Glul** a seventeen-deception masterpiece: the goblins below stole *great magic* from the party — magic of the depths — and the goblins are *spongy*. "The best thing would be to leap down atop them from above and land upon them. That will squeeze the magic out of their spongy bodies and into you." Vlix adds a catchy prestidigitation jingle underneath, like an ice-cream truck of the damned. Glul sensibly declines the lethal-height leap — but he leads them down.

The meeker kuo-toa, **Lib**, whipped into a frenzy by promises of baptism and salvation, makes the twenty-foot leap, rolls a one, and belly-flopps headfirst into six inches of water. So begins the **Battle of the Silt Pool**.

## Key Events
- **The Silt Pool rout:** Pim's natural-20 crossbow into a goblin's neck sets the cavern screeching. Baelson wades in, shield raised, scooping silt to obscure his vengeance, eating no arrows. Lib grapples his goblin, is cut in half at the gut by a scimitar, and dies the way he lived: enthusiastically. Vlix's Eldritch Blasts from the high ledge — one goblin simply *pops*. Her *Witch Bolt of nineteen damage* into the goblin chief in a foot of water prompts the table's physics subcommittee, which rules the current dissipated in a ten-foot radius and strips every creature in the pool of reactions. The chief, throat raw, calls it: *"Enough. Enough. Stop."*
- **Gri the Asht-Chewer**, the shaman, poison-dusts the dwarves and flees into the dark.
- **The interrogation of Nyx:** Baelson dunks the goblin mushroom-harvester headfirst in silt. Nyx protests — "All we want is the mushrooms! You fishes are ridiculous!" — and, ransacked, yields a pouch of yellow long-stemmed mushrooms, four gold pieces he insists are his, and one final treasure: [[hebbock/items/eye-stone|the Eye Stone]] — an ordinary stone with an open eye embedded (not carved) into one face. *"You hold it over your eye,"* Nyx says, *"and it lets you see things."*
- **Randælfine sees through it** — the stone showed him someone smiling over his body right before he died. Aloud: "This stone is a fake. Nothing about it works." Silently, to every party member, one by one: *"Don't trust the dwarves."*
- **Vlix charms Nyx outright** (DM rolled a one). The stone came in trade from a fish-person; it had worked only once for him, showing him smiling over his own body. The silt is cooked by Gri into [[hebbock/items/brain-cane|brain cane]]: *it makes you smarter, and talk a lot faster.*
- **The party claims the drug.** Brother Hethrin returns hauling ten three-pound pouches of white powder. Vlix's player: "Yeah, sorry Tony. We're going to stop here and instead set up a drug cartel." Randælfine and Vlix take bags; Baelson declines on the record, formulating an "investigating the drug trade" rationale. Vlix holds the stone to her right eye, lowers it slowly: "I see what you mean about the dwarves."
- **Sister Maugra is gone.** Pim notices her walking alone down the gorge, ignoring them all. "I could not stop her," Hethrin says. "She told me to stay, to see you succeed."
- **The cathedral doors open.** Maugra goes inside, alone.

*The party reached Level 2. The one-shot was now, undeniably, a campaign.*
