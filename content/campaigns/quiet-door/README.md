# Quiet Door

> A party of seekers descends into the [[hebbock/locations/hargate-delve|Hargate Delve]], where something patient and very old is counting the living — and one lost holy man has become its smiling herald.

## Premise
*The Quiet Door* is the active campaign of the [[hebbock/worldbuilding/hebbock|Hebbock]] table. Its opening arc, [[campaigns/quiet-door/arcs/the-counting-dark|The Counting Dark]], begins in the fading mountain town of [[hebbock/locations/hargate|Hargate]] in the world of [[hebbock/worldbuilding/hebbock|Hebbock]], where dream-driven disappearances have the townsfolk screaming. Four unlikely souls answer the call — and the call to their dreams — to bring home a lost holy man of the [[hebbock/factions/vault-of-dumathoin|Vault of Dumathoin]]. What they find in the deep is a defiled cathedral, a mass grave of two hundred dead, and [[hebbock/npcs/the-witness|The Witness]] — an eye that is counting, and has not yet counted them.

**Tone:** dark fantasy with a heavy comic heart. The table leans into voice, farce, and moral ambiguity without losing the stakes.

**DM:** aedoran.
