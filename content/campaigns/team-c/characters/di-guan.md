# 🍵 Di Guan

> **Human fighter.** "Strong fighter who talks in broken common." (CAST OF CHARACTERS)

## Known
- **Weapon:** a halberd — which **snaps into a battleaxe** when he levers his trapped leg free under the granite block (Session 5). He fights with head, shaft, and **butt** ("Di's butt is in the gnoll leader's face, and yells 'for the people!'").
- **Gags (house canon):** "Di Guan asks to be **made fat**" (Session 1); "**for the people**"; "**Di-capitates**" (to decapitate); "I give you **shock**!" (Session 13); "I feel different too, **I am magic man**" (Session 9).
- **Heritage:** his people repelled the [[zazi/factions/vontir-empire|Vontir Empire]]'s "soft encroachment" ~450–500 years before the past era; they were never conquered; one of his great ancestors was a **hero of that battle** — the name is "to be determined later" (Session 9; open question).
- **Ping Gao** — his grandfather, whose **birthday tea celebration** happens in Session 1 (the ceremonial tea with Fatima).
- In the past era he becomes "a **magic man**" and can **summon a celestial hawk named Peng** (Session 8–9). Peng is air support, gets "overwhelmed by the bustle of the city and the multitude of delicious pigeons" (Session 12), and does not see where the green smoke went.
- **Telepathy relay (Sessions 9–11):** Bishop → Di (in the head) → the party (loud); Di "chats out loud while Bishop talks in his head," then figures out how to talk "only in each others' heads."
- **Scholarly streak:** all night writing (Session 9); all night reading (Session 10); studies the charnel-house **spellbook** (18 spells, 1 hr each); asks for "a book, to learn spells" as his price from young Hazi (Session 9).
- **Arcane Insight** — his Session 4 reveal: the falcon-headed-men carving is an **illusion**; the true subjects are [[zazi/creatures/nkosi|Nkosi]]. "Sorcerous shenanigans!"
- **"I have never seen a warforged… until now!"** (Session 1, on SCOUT.)
