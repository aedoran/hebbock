# 🕯️ Lever of the Dead

> A lever under a table lip in the [[zazi/locations/pyramid-of-tiberesh#de-organing-room|de-organing room]] of the [[zazi/locations/pyramid-of-tiberesh|Pyramid of Tiberesh]], with **the face of Anubis** — "a jackal-headed god in Egyptian mythology who leads the dead to judgment."

## In play (Session 5)
- Found by [[campaigns/team-c/characters/lachma|Lachma]]: "We found something! ... This is very nearly an analog of Hermes. **It's a lever of the dead.**"
- **First pull:** granite rumbling — the huge slab that nearly killed [[campaigns/team-c/characters/di-guan|Di]] in the basin room **resets** (slides back up).
- **Second pull (Bishop: "FUCK IT, WE PULL AGAIN!"):** the slab **comes crashing down** across the doorway.
- The party's verdict: "**This is the worst slot machine.**"
- Whether the lever *controls* the block, or does something else entirely (a dead-man's mechanism? a seal on something below?), is unconfirmed — the notes only establish the block's response.

## Threads
- The painted golden door (lapis scarabs + jade serpent, ≈40 gp) that leads from this room into the basin room is on the same side of the room; the lever's purpose may be tied to the mummification process or to something the room was *built to keep out* (open question).
