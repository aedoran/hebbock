# 🎲 Session 11 — Orenstein's Glorious Body

Born of combat in the depths of initiative, [[campaigns/team-b/party|the party]] fights up through [[mountains/locations/kobold-air-tower|the kobold air tower]] for any answers that might renew [[campaigns/team-b/characters/orenstein-alabaster|Orenstein]]'s Glorious Body.

## What Happened

- Session title per the players: “Hells Yeah, GOATs! AKA: 'Orenstein's Glorious Body' or 'Curse of the Ghostly Boooooomer'.” Opener: the Rumsfeld “amidst all the clutter… let the flak pass” line, from “Donald Rumsfeld, America's drunk uncle Santa Claus, guiding us to victory.”
- The session is born of combat, within the depths of initiative, where life and death balances on the edge of a blade.
- Kobolds — the **tiny trapping lizards of draconic descent** — are struggling to hold their tower against the party's intrusion. The party surges forward for answers that might renew Orenstein's Glorious [Boomer] Body. (Boot[a]y?) 🍑
- “Eldritch Blast biotches!”
- [[campaigns/team-b/characters/thora|Thora]] is hit by a bullet — some damage.
- [[campaigns/team-b/characters/sir|Sir]] throws her second handaxe in retaliation, **john woo'ing the shit out of a kobold** in an alcove, the handaxe cleaving through, arterial spray flying in an arc above the party.
- [[campaigns/team-b/characters/xohn|Xohn]]'s **crossbow of justice (AKA “Boomr-slayah”)** slays another kobold.
- Orenstein's illusion draws forth a CLATTER.
- The newly Boomered Orenstein unleashes a full generational rant mid-fight: his blasting powers are “of course powered by a society and community that he won't acknowledge,” he complains about the price of eggs while supporting the deregulation that would cause even greater shortages (bird flu is a construct of elves), and young peasants should pull themselves up by the magical bootstraps of speed — even as he saddles talented youth with wizard school debt that takes decades of adventuring to pay off, and builds ceilings of platinum and electrum then argues the young should dig up through them without being given any tools. He also complains about divine spells, being unable to convert from the arcane format.
- The rant's favorite list: “We Didn't Start the Fire” by Bardy Joel (a perfect combination of rock and roll and cola wars — though one culture didn't invent rock and roll, it was **stolen from halflings**); Forrest Gump; the Beatles, the best band ever, “no moment greater than their appearance on the Orc Sullivan show”; and peasantball — so long as none of the peasants kneel in protest during the royal entrance. His travel style rebooms to match: plazas and lobbies even in the world's most dangerous places, an assumption that every creature speaks Common, smiling-emoji illusions of yellow minions with amplified “thoughts and prayers” wishes, suspicion of the tower's union, and disdain for the kobold immigrants who “took over” it.
- The core question of the curse, asked flatly: “Orenstein really doesn't see the problem with his newly acquired generation. He's an amazing individual who has accomplished everything himself. **How could his new age be seen as a curse at all?**”
- Xohn, climbing a ladder, triggers a **kama trap** that damages his legs as he ascends.
- Sir is hit heavily by a volley of kobold fire, then leaps down — improbably sticking the landing — and runs out the front door on her last hit point.
- Cliffhanger at the top of the initiative order: Orenstein has to decide whether to descend into the pit. Does he have faith in his own illusion and power? Will the party get a moment's respite?

## World Changes

- The party is fighting its way up the kobold air tower, floor by floor.
- Sir leaves the fight at 1 HP, bleeding out of the front door.
