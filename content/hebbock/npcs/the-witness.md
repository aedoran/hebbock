# The Witness

> *The thing behind the eye. It is counting.*

![[the-witness.png|480]]

## Overview
The heart of [[campaigns/quiet-door/arcs/the-counting-dark|The Counting Dark]]. An eye — eight feet up, lidless, ethereal, "a black hole the mind slid off of, impossible to focus on" — floats above the exact center of the mass grave in the [[hebbock/locations/cathedral-of-dumathoin|Cathedral of Dumathoin]]. It is counting people. It reached a number in the party's shared dream, paused, and *has not yet counted them*.

## Details
- Kind: unknown — an eye, or the eye of something. A presence, not a body (no body seen in Sessions I–IV).
- Voice: "patient, kind, and very old."
- Power:
  - **The counting dream** — visited all three original party members in Hargate (and, via its heralds, the town).
  - **Tolling the dead** — in Session IV it pointed at Vlix and tolled the dead; her life force refused to leave her, and it tilted its head "like a confused dog."
  - **The choir** — the kuo-toa choir of the cathedral is *bound to it*; striking all the acolytes at once sent a vision back (four psychic damage) and a name: **the Witness**.
  - **"The eye is at hand!"** — Cororic's cry. The eye can arrive. Its full nature is the arc's great unknown.

## Appearance
- The eye: lidless, ethereal, black. Impossible to focus on.
- No other form has been seen.

## Personality
- Patient. Kind. Very old. It does not rage; it *counts*, and it is *delighted* — though the delight is Randælfine's reading, not a confirmed fact.

## Relationships
- [[hebbock/npcs/cororic|Cororic]] — its smiling herald in the flesh; "the one from the stone."
- [[hebbock/npcs/ulu|Ulu]] — conductor of its choir.
- The kuo-toa acolytes — bound to it; they sing so the sound never breaks.
- The party — not yet counted.

## Where It Is
- [[hebbock/locations/cathedral-of-dumathoin|Cathedral of Dumathoin]] — above the concentric rings of the dead.

## Appearances
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-01|Session I]] — as the counting dream, and the sense that "something was still counting" below.
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-03|Session III]] — indirectly: the [[hebbock/items/eye-stone|Eye Stone]] shows death; Vlix, after holding it, says "I see what you mean about the dwarves."
- [[campaigns/quiet-door/sessions/session-04|Session IV]] — seen at last; named at last; its herald fights, flees, and cries "The eye is at hand!"

## What Remains Unknown
- Its exact nature is **intentionally unknown** (aedoran's ruling) — a mystery to be revealed through play, not a gap to be filled. It is not the [[hebbock/factions/great-old-one|Great Old One]]; the two powers are separate.
- What the count is *for*, and what happens when it finishes counting the party — to be revealed.
