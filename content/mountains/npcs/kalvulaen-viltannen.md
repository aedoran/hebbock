# 🍃 Kalvulaen Vil'Tannen

> "**The Elven King of the High Elves in Vil'Tanee**" (Session 8, 11/23/2024) — the High Elven King whose symbol the party carries.

## What is known
- The **tortoise amulet with a golden leaf** (pulled from the treasure chest at the [[mountains/locations/blackrock-peak|Blackrock Peak watchtower]]) is a **symbol of Kalvulaen Vil'Tannen**.
- The whole [[campaigns/team-b/items/high-elven-leaf-treasure|High Elven leaf treasure]] (ring, amulet, circlet, three leaf-etched d20s) is leaf-marked.
- **Vil'Tanee** is the old name of **Astogrolluu** — the home of [[mountains/npcs/vigo-dunaedin|Vigo Dunaedin]]'s traitorous brother.

## In play
- The High Elven king is the thread that ties the leaf treasure, the traitor's mark, and Astogrolluu together. Whether he lives, rules, or roams the land (the tarot said "a wise ruler who roams the land" — of Thora) is open. See [[_questions|Open Questions]].
