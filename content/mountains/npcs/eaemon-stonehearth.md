# 🌿 Eamon Stonehearth

> An **elder of [[mountains/locations/stonehaven|Stonehaven]]** (Session 1, 9/1/2024). Orenstein calls him "Eamonmonmon."

## What he does
- Brews a tea for the party as Orenstein introduces the group.
- Has **heard of the tomb but never seen it** — and points out that [[mountains/npcs/gilda|Gilda]] is the town drunk.
- Gives the party their only directions: **look for the three peaks coming together, follow the footpath, and you come upon the watchtower** — the path to the [[mountains/locations/blackrock-peak|Blackrock Peak watchtower]].

## In play
- Eamon is the town's voice of reason in the campaign's opening. His name (Stonehearth) may not be a coincidence — the stone-and-hearth folk of these mountains seem to run deep.
