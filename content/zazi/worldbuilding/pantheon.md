# 🕊️ The Gods of Zazi

> The divine landscape as the Team C party encounters it. ⚠️ The notes name **real-world Egyptian deities** (Bastet, Anubis) — see the open question in [[_questions|Open Questions]] on whether the pantheon is literally Egyptian or Egyptian-flavored.

## Named gods
- **Bastet** — the cat goddess. The dead scholar [[zazi/npcs/tellya-hakkam|Tellya Hakkam]] was "studying Bastet, deciphering texts" when he died; his notes are a rhyme of crawling away from "her holiness and hunger and might." She is the obvious patron of [[zazi/locations/zazi|Zazi, City of Cats]] and likely of the [[zazi/creatures/nkosi|Nkosi]].
- **Anubis** — "a jackal-headed god in Egyptian mythology who leads the dead to judgment." Appears as: the face of the [[zazi/items/lever-of-the-dead|Lever of the Dead]]; iconography in the charnel house's death-magic shrine; and the jackaloid creature that burrows out from under a rug there (it dissolves into sand and reconstitutes — an "avatar of Anubis" per Khortum). Lachma calls the lever "very nearly an analog of Hermes."
- **Enoreth** — god of **time**. A constellation bearing its symbol is visible in the star-portal sky of the [[zazi/worldbuilding/zazi#the-chamber-of-stars|Chamber of Stars]] (Lachma and Di both recognize it). The most natural patron of the Chamber of Stars — but that is inference, not yet stated in the notes.
- **The fish-headed god** — unnamed. Alabaster reliefs along the causeway ("They're pescatarians"), the twin golden doors (dagger-hand / curved-rod-hand), the anglerfish onyx doors in Tiberesh's tomb ("the fish wrote it went to Yale"), and a feline-embossed bronze chest that Khortum believes "is related to an ancient god or religion."
- **The [[zazi/worldbuilding/lost-one|Lost One]]** — the god of the Loxodons: alive and worshipped in the past (temple, roadside shrines, stations of devotion); dead or absent in the present, leaving [[campaigns/team-c/characters/khortum-fil|Khortum]] with a fragment of its soul and a sense of loss.
- **The goddess of the river** — named only in Tellya's parchment ("one karam to the goddess of the river"). Possibly a facet of the river/Tiberesh connection (see [[zazi/locations/pyramid-of-tiberesh|Pyramid of Tiberesh]]).

## Rumored / unconfirmed
- **Djinn** — "if they are real," per tavern gossip in the past era.
- **The Vontir war-wizards** — "powerful wizards serve as generals"; whether any are divine is unknown.

## Worship in play
- The past-era **temple of the Lost One** (stone, dim, incense-rolled) is where [[campaigns/team-c/characters/khortum-fil|Khortum]] completes "a pilgrimage across time" (Session 9.5).
- The charnel house is, functionally, a death temple: [[zazi/npcs/sultan-shukranil|the Sultan]] runs it, but the shrine to death magic beneath belongs to Anubis.
- Tiberesh's pyramid is full of Tiberesh-cult imagery — self-sacrifice alcoves, the fish god, the setting-sun columns — but the party finds the "falcon-headed men slay lions" carving is an **illusion** (Di's Arcane Insight, Session 4): the true subjects are [[zazi/creatures/nkosi|Nkosi]].
