# Session IV — *Be Still and Be Seen*

*August 1st*

## Recap
Newly leveled and terribly confident, the party descends to the doors of the **Cathedral of Dumathoin** — sixteen feet of ancient dwarven stonework, flanked by carved dwarves with maces and shields, someone having recently carved **new open eyes over the statues' eyes**. Brother Hethrin names it for what it is: defilement. To the [[hebbock/factions/vault-of-dumathoin|Dumathoin]], the eye is sacred. "This church may not be what we want it to be," he says. "But Cororic is inside."

Baelson — natural one looking for the missing child's tracks, worked into a frenzy — kicks the doors open.

## Key Events
- **The defiled doors:** new eyes carved over the old — a grave transgression the party reads on the threshold.
- **The antechamber:** dust, scattered pews, a stench like nothing living — *many* deaths, layered across years and species. Dwarven carvings crawl the walls, saner near the door, madder toward the back: near the entrance, over and over, ***"It's not here. It's not here."*** Deeper in: ***"This is not the prize."*** Baelson, reading the despair of a man who sought and never found, begins — entirely by accident — to *vibe* with him. (The table breaks into U2. It could not be helped.)
- **The bouquet:** Randælfine's natural-20 perception on the far doors — dead drow, dead dwarves, dead gnomes, in every stage of decay. "We will find something *totally* different in there. We should open it."
- **The preparations:** a bump of [[hebbock/items/brain-cane|brain cane]] passed around — Pim becomes **Pimp**, Vlix partakes, Baelson is already high on St. Cuthbert, Hethrin politely abstains.
- **The inner cathedral:** two hundred dead in **concentric circles** — drow, dwarves, gnomes, humans, kuo-toa, rotted across decades, strange fungus blooming from the older rings. An open-air mass grave; an Underdark war crime.
- **The eye:** eight feet above the exact center — lidless, ethereal, a black hole the mind slid off of, impossible to focus on.
- **The choir:** five kuo-toa singing a droning, out-of-sync sine-wave chant, breathing in relay so the sound never breaks, conducted by a priest-like figure with a spear for a baton — [[hebbock/npcs/ulu|Ulu]]. Zombie-eyed thralls sway among the corpses.
- **Cororic:** a smiling old dwarf, standing pleasantly over the body of a small boy. *"Welcome! I didn't realize you would come too. It's wonderful."*
- **The charge:** Randælfine's telepathy — *he is the one from the stone. He is a murderer. Don't trust him.* Baelson, whose whole soul has narrowed to the child, charges. Cororic touches his shoulder: "The boy is almost done. Almost counted. There's no need for this level of aggression."
- **Vlix breaks:** "Let's not get handsy, sugar." — Eldritch Blast, 17 to hit, Cororic hurled ten feet.
- **The battle in the boneyard:**
  - Brother Hethrin, horrified, screaming "No!", turns on the party to defend his lost saint.
  - Randælfine's rapier through Hethrin's chainmail for twelve, at full brain-cane speed: "I'm going to skewer you and skewer your organs and skewer your eyes and skewer your kneecaps—."
  - Vlix's celestial blood shrugs off radiant fire that would kill a lesser level one — she learns something of what she truly is.
  - Cororic barely fights. He *counts*. He points at Vlix and tolled the dead — she saves on a natural 20, and he tilts his head like a confused dog when her life force refuses to leave her.
  - **Baelson is charmed.** Looking into Cororic's eyes — "Fellow paladin, there is no need for this violence" — the Night Angel feels something he has no word for. Doubt. Unable to raise a hand against the tragic, fallen figure: "I understand why you abandoned the Dumathoin and gave way to this heresy." ("We're potentially making him more dangerous," Benjamin notes. "Just pointing that out.")
  - Randælfine **breakdances** out of Cororic's "Be still and be seen" — moonwalking through a hold spell on a sixteen.
  - The choir advances in waltz-time, hurling sticky nets. Pim cartwheels clear on a nineteen; Pimp takes a bow.
  - Randælfine unfurls the **Arms of Cthulhu** — black tentacles lashing a ten-foot circle of ghoul-faced singers and turncoat cleric alike — and the moment he strikes all the acolytes at once, something strikes *back*. A vision sears his mind (four psychic damage) with a name: the choir is bound to the eye, and the eye belongs to **[[hebbock/npcs/the-witness|the Witness]]**.
  - Vlix's Witch Bolt finds Cororic for twenty-one points, flipping him legs-over-head — and the old dwarf, still smiling, still pleasant, spends his entire movement fleeing fifty feet to snap the lightning's tether, crying out to his acolytes: *"The eye is at hand!"*

**End of session, mid-battle, blades out, the choir still singing:**
**Baelson 11/23 · Pim(p) 16/16 · Randælfine 10/11 · Vlix 9/15**

*The boy is safe behind Baelson's shield. The Witness has a name. And somewhere in the counting dark, it has not yet finished counting them.*
